# Bead Pouches

> Bead pouches are core magic utility items in Old Town. They determine how many bead types a mage can carry and manage. Upgrading from a fraying cloth pouch to a meteor-threaded omni-pouch is a key magic progression milestone.

## Bead Pouch System

Beads are tiny spell-tokens consumed by magic. A mage cannot cast without beads. A bead pouch determines how many bead types can be carried simultaneously.

**Key rule:** Without a bead pouch, a mage can only carry 1 bead type (in hand). With a pouch, they can carry multiple types and cast complex spells.

## Pouch Types

| Pouch | Bead Types | Capacity | Identity | Notes |
|-------|-----------|----------|----------|-------|
| **Threadbare Bead Pouch** | 2 types | 100 beads | Early-game cloth pouch, cheap and fraying | Starter pouch, given in tutorial |
| **Waxed Bead Pouch** | 3 types | 250 beads | Weatherproof, candlewax-sealed, apprentice upgrade | First upgrade, cheap |
| **Warden Bead Pouch** | 4 types | 500 beads | Licensed pouch for proper spellwork and teleports | Mid-tier, requires license |
| **Grave Bead Pouch** | 4 types | 750 beads | Crypt-runner pouch with bone toggles and black cord | Stores Bone/Grave/Soul at 50% weight |
| **Starfall Bead Pouch** | 6 types | 2000 beads | Endgame omni-pouch, meteor-thread, silver clasps | Best-in-slot, rare prestige item |

## Pouch Mechanics

1. **Bead type slots.** Each pouch allows carrying N bead types simultaneously. A mage with a Threadbare pouch can carry Gust + Wit, but not Gust + Tide + Wit.
2. **Bead capacity.** Each pouch holds a maximum number of beads total. 100 beads = 100 of any combination.
3. **Weight reduction.** Grave Bead Pouch reduces the weight of Bone, Grave, and Soul beads by 50%.
4. **Stacking.** Beads stack up to 100 per inventory slot. Pouches go in the equipment or inventory slot.
5. **No dropping on death.** Pouches are kept on death. Beads inside are lost on death.

## Starter Pouch

Every new mage receives a **Threadbare Bead Pouch** during the magic tutorial. It holds 2 bead types and 100 beads.

Players say:
- "Get a waxed pouch before training magic."
- "Warden pouch is huge for teleports."
- "Grave pouch makes crypt runs cheaper."
- "Starfall pouch is best-in-slot."

## Design Rules

1. **Pouches are progression.** Upgrading from Threadbare → Waxed → Warden → Grave → Starfall is as important as upgrading a weapon.
2. **Pouches are kept on death.** They are utility equipment, not consumable.
3. **Pouch capacity matters.** 100 beads runs out fast. 2000 beads is endgame.
4. **Bead type slots matter.** Early mages can only cast simple spells. Endgame mages can cast complex multi-bead spells.
5. **No pouch = no magic.** A mage without a pouch can only carry 1 bead type and cast the simplest spells.
