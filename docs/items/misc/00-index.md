## Misc Item System Overview

Misc items are the glue of the item system. They are the economy, the projectiles, the trinkets, the special objects.

**Key principles:**
- **Currency is the economy.** Coins are the primary currency.
- **Ammunition is consumed.** Arrows and bolts are used by ranged weapons.
- **Accessories are build glue.** They amplify playstyles, not replace them.
- **Special items are situational.** Keys, maps, tokens.

## Design Rules

1. **Currency is stackable.**
2. **Ammunition is stackable.**
3. **Ammunition is consumed.**
4. **Accessories are non-stackable.**
5. **Accessories are build glue.**
6. **Quest points are non-tradeable.**
7. **Special items are situational.**
8. **Cosmetics are non-functional.**
9. **Bolt tips add effects.**
10. **Rings are small but powerful.**
