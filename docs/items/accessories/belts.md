# Belts

> **Belt slot documentation for Old Town.** Belts are waist-straps — practical, preparatory, and weight-bearing. A Belt is what you carry into the unknown.
>
> **Rule:** Every Belt has a tradeoff. No Belt is universally best. Choose the Belt that matches your preparation, not the one with the highest numbers.

## Belt Philosophy

Belts are the slot for **inventory and preparation**. They are the objects that hold your pouches, your cartridges, your tools, your provisions. Belts are invisible but essential.

**Design principles:**
1. **Belts are practical.** A Belt is not decoration. It is a tool-holder, a cartridge belt, a forager's strap.
2. **Belts trade inventory for speed.** Belts that carry more slow you down. Belts that speed you up carry less.
3. **Belts affect ammo and consumables.** Many Belts modify ammo capacity, consumable efficiency, or tool durability.
4. **Belts have PvP caps.** No Belt bonus exceeds 15% in PvP.

## Design Rules for Belts

1. **Belts are practical.** The name and description must evoke a tool-holder, a cartridge belt, or a forager's strap.
2. **Belts trade inventory for speed.** Belts that carry more slow you down. Belts that speed you up carry less.
3. **Belts affect ammo and consumables.** Many Belts modify ammo capacity, consumable efficiency, or tool durability.
4. **Belts have PvP caps.** No Belt bonus exceeds 15% in PvP.
5. **Belts are cultural.** The Bellbuckled Belt references town bell motifs. The Grave Runner Belt references graveyard travel.
6. **Belts are not sashes.** A "Belt" in Old Town is a strap, a girdle, a cartridge belt. The word "belt" is the slot name. The item names are more specific.
