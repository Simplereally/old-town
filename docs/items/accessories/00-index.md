# Accessories Index

> **Entry point for all accessory documentation in Old Town.** Accessories are Keepsakes — small, personal objects that say what kind of player you are becoming. They are not stat sticks. They are build glue.
>
> **Rule:** Every accessory has a tradeoff. No accessory is universally best. Choose the accessories that match your playstyle, not the ones with the highest numbers.

## Accessory System Overview

Accessories are the items that turn a build into a *playstyle*.

**Key principles:**
- **Accessories are build glue, not power.** They amplify weapon and armour choices. They do not replace them.
- **No universal BIS.** Every slot has tradeoffs. A Cape that boosts melee speed reduces magic accuracy. An Amulet that boosts beads reduces defence.
- **PvP caps exist.** No accessory effect exceeds a 15% bonus in PvP.
- **Cultural grounding.** Every name references Old Town culture: wardens, bells, writs, graveyards, beadwork, markets, glassworks, hunters, ferries, old roads, guild badges, shrines, foundries, folk superstition.
- **Tactile language.** A Cape is "moth-eaten," "waxed," "bell-buckled." An Amulet is "tallow-sealed," "salt-stained," "glass-faceted."

## Design Rules for Accessories

1. **Never use generic fantasy names.** No "Amulet of Glory," "Ring of Wealth," "Berserker Ring."
2. **Every accessory has a tradeoff.** If it only gives bonuses, it has failed.
3. **PvP caps are hard.** 15% maximum for any bonus in PvP. No exceptions.
4. **Cultural grounding is mandatory.** The name must reference Old Town culture.
5. **Tactile language is mandatory.** The description must feel like an object you could hold.
6. **Build-glue is the goal.** The accessory must make a specific playstyle better, not every playstyle better.
7. **Higher bands are not strictly better.** A Starfall Cape is not "Cobbled Cape but more." It is a different object for a different player.
8. **Accessories are non-stackable.** One per slot. No duplicates.
9. **Rings are two slots.** Left and right ring slots. Two different rings, or one ring in each.
10. **Trophies are rare.** Not every player will have a Trophy. They are earned, not bought.
