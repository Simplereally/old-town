# Amulets

> **Amulet slot documentation for Old Town.** Amulets are neck-keeps — objects of belief, prayer, and focus. An Amulet tells the world what you trust.
>
> **Rule:** Every Amulet has a tradeoff. No Amulet is universally best. Choose the Amulet that matches your faith, not the one with the highest numbers.

## Amulet Philosophy

Amulets are the slot for **belief and focus**. They are the objects players touch for luck, the beads they count, the seals they trust. Amulets are small, heavy, and personal.

**Design principles:**
1. **Amulets are belief objects.** An Amulet is not jewellery. It is a talisman, a locket, a writ, a reliquary.
2. **Amulets trade magic for melee.** Magic-focused Amulets reduce melee power. Melee-focused Amulets reduce magic power.
3. **Amulets affect beads.** Many Amulets modify bead consumption, bead savings, or bead types.
4. **Amulets have PvP caps.** No Amulet bonus exceeds 15% in PvP.

## Design Rules for Amulets

1. **Amulets are belief objects.** The name and description must evoke trust, prayer, or focus.
2. **Amulets trade magic for melee.** Magic-focused Amulets reduce melee power. Melee-focused Amulets reduce magic power.
3. **Amulets affect beads.** Many Amulets modify bead consumption, bead savings, or bead types.
4. **Amulets have PvP caps.** No Amulet bonus exceeds 15% in PvP.
5. **Amulets are cultural.** The Warden Writ Amulet references guard writs. The Grave Bead Amulet references graveyard beadwork.
6. **Amulets are not jewellery.** An "Amulet" in Old Town is a charm, a locket, a writ, a reliquary. The word "amulet" is the slot name. The item names are more specific.
