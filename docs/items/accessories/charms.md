# Charms

> **Charm slot documentation for Old Town.** Charms are pocket-luck — small, hidden, and superstitious. A Charm is what you carry when no one is looking.
>
> **Rule:** Every Charm has a tradeoff. No Charm is universally best. Choose the Charm that matches your luck, not the one with the highest numbers.

## Charm Philosophy

Charms are the slot for **utility and superstition**. They are the objects players touch for luck, the buttons they sew into their pockets, the sprigs they carry for safety. Charms are invisible to other players but deeply felt by the wearer.

**Design principles:**
1. **Charms are luck objects.** A Charm is not magic. It is superstition, tradition, and folk belief.
2. **Charms trade skilling for combat.** Skilling Charms reduce combat power. Combat Charms reduce skilling efficiency.
3. **Charms affect drops and travel.** Many Charms modify drop rates, travel speed, or encounter rates.
4. **Charms have PvP caps.** No Charm bonus exceeds 15% in PvP.

## Design Rules for Charms

1. **Charms are luck objects.** The name and description must evoke superstition, tradition, or folk belief.
2. **Charms trade skilling for combat.** Skilling Charms reduce combat power. Combat Charms reduce skilling efficiency.
3. **Charms affect drops and travel.** Many Charms modify drop rates, travel speed, or encounter rates.
4. **Charms have PvP caps.** No Charm bonus exceeds 15% in PvP.
5. **Charms are cultural.** The Bell Charm references town bells. The Gravebone Charm references graveyard folk magic.
6. **Charms are not magic items.** A "Charm" in Old Town is a button, a sprig, a knot, a shard. The word "charm" is the slot name. The item names are more specific.
