## Resource System Overview

Resources are the raw materials of the world. They are gathered through skills (Woodcutting, Mining, Fishing) and transformed into usable items through crafting (Cooking, Smithing, Crafting, Fletching).

**Key principles:**
- **Resources are abundant.** Trees, rocks, fish are everywhere.
- **Higher tiers require higher skills.** Oak trees require level 15 Woodcutting.
- **Resources are stackable.** They go in your inventory in stacks.
- **Resources are tradeable.** They are the primary economic goods.
- **Raw resources need processing.** Logs become planks. Ore becomes bars. Fish becomes cooked food.

## Resource Design Rules

1. **Resources are stackable.** They go in inventory in stacks.
2. **Resources are tradeable.** They are the primary economic goods.
3. **Higher tiers require higher skills.** This creates progression.
4. **Raw resources need processing.** This creates a skill loop.
5. **Resources have multiple uses.** Oak logs are used for Firemaking, Fletching, and Construction.
6. **Alloys require multiple ores.** Bellmetal requires copper + tin.
7. **Coal is a fuel, not a metal.** It is required for smelting higher-tier ores.
8. **Fish have bait requirements.** Higher-tier fish require better bait/tools.
9. **Gems are found while mining.** They are a random bonus while mining ore.
10. **Hides require tanning.** Raw hide → tanned leather → crafted armour.
