# Shield Armour

> All shield armour in Old Town. 13 tiers of Ward.

## Ward

> **Ward** is the ownable term for shield in Old Town. "Argent Ward," "Blackbar Ward," "Starfall Ward" all sound good. "Shield" is generic. "Ward" is specific.

- **Slot:** Shield (off-hand)
- **Weight:** Medium
- **Defence bonus:** Medium-High
- **Attack penalty:** -2 (using shield reduces attack accuracy slightly)
- **Best for:** Defensive combat, tanking, protection
- **Tradeoff:** Cannot use two-handed weapons while equipped
