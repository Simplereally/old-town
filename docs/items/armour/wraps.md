# Wraps (Magic Legs)

> Magic leg armour. Cloth wraps or leggings with magic bonuses. The magic equivalent of Chausses.

## Design

- **Slot:** Legs
- **Weight:** Light
- **Best for:** Magic defence, magic attack
- **Tradeoff:** No melee defence, no ranged defence
- **Visual:** Cloth wraps or loose leggings, sometimes with bead trim
