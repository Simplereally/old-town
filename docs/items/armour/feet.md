# Foot Armour

> All foot armour in Old Town. 13 tiers of Sabatons.

## Sabatons

> **Sabatons** is the historical term for armoured boots. It sounds more grounded than "boots" or "greaves."

- **Slot:** Feet
- **Weight:** Light
- **Defence bonus:** Low
- **Run energy bonus:** +1% per tier (better boots help you run longer)
- **Best for:** Standard foot protection, run energy
