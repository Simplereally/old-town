# Head Armour

> All head armour in Old Town: Helm (light, open-faced) and Greathelm (heavy, full-face). 13 tiers each.

## Helm (Open-Faced)

> Light head protection. Open face, no vision penalty. Less defence than Greathelm.

- **Slot:** Head
- **Weight:** Light
- **Defence bonus:** Low
- **Magic bonus:** None
- **Best for:** Low-level combat, skilling, casual wear

## Greathelm (Full-Face)

> Heavy head protection. Enclosed face, maximum defence. Heavy.

- **Slot:** Head
- **Weight:** Heavy
- **Defence bonus:** High
- **Magic bonus:** -5 (magic penalty for enclosed vision)
- **Best for:** Heavy combat, tanking, PvP
- **Tradeoff:** Slightly reduces magic accuracy

## Helm vs Greathelm Comparison

| Feature | Helm | Greathelm |
|---------|------|-----------|
| **Defence** | Lower | Higher |
| **Weight** | Light | Heavy |
| **Magic penalty** | None | -5 |
| **Vision** | Full | Restricted (RP) |
| **Best for** | Skilling, hybrid | Tanking, pure melee |

## Design Rules

1. **Helm is open-faced.** No magic penalty. Less defence.
2. **Greathelm is full-face.** Magic penalty. More defence.
3. **Cobbled is the joke.** Bucket with straps. Barrel with eye holes.
4. **Starfall is the mythic.** Meteor flecks. Mythic.
