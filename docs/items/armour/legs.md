# Leg Armour

> All leg armour in Old Town. 13 tiers of Chausses.

## Chausses

> **Chausses** is the historical term for leg armour. It sounds more grounded than "platelegs" or "greaves."

- **Slot:** Legs
- **Weight:** Medium
- **Defence bonus:** Medium
- **Best for:** Standard leg protection
