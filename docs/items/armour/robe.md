# Robe (Magic Chest)

> Magic chest armour. Cloth robe with magic bonuses. The magic equivalent of Harness, Hauberk, or Platecoat.

## Design

- **Slot:** Chest
- **Weight:** Light
- **Best for:** Magic attack, magic defence
- **Tradeoff:** No melee defence, no ranged defence
- **Visual:** Long cloth robe, sometimes with bead trim, chalk marks, or wax seals
