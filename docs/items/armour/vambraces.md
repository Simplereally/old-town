# Vambraces (Ranged Hands)

> Ranged hand armour. Leather arm guards or bracers with ranged bonuses. The ranged equivalent of Gauntlets or Cuffs.

## Design

- **Slot:** Hands
- **Weight:** Light
- **Best for:** Ranged accuracy, ranged defence
- **Tradeoff:** Low melee defence, low magic defence
- **Visual:** Leather bracers or cloth arm wraps, sometimes with stitching, buckles, or feather accents
