# Treads (Ranged Feet)

> Ranged foot armour. Soft leather or cloth boots with ranged bonuses. The ranged equivalent of Sabatons or Softshoes.

## Design

- **Slot:** Feet
- **Weight:** Light
- **Best for:** Ranged defence, mobility, stealth
- **Tradeoff:** Low melee defence, low magic defence
- **Visual:** Soft leather boots or cloth wraps, sometimes with fringe, stitching, or bead trim
