# Cowl (Magic Head)

> Magic headgear. Cloth hood with magic bonuses. The magic equivalent of a Helm or Greathelm.

## Design

- **Slot:** Head
- **Weight:** Light
- **Best for:** Magic accuracy, magic defence
- **Tradeoff:** No melee defence, no ranged defence
- **Visual:** Cloth hood, sometimes with bead trim, chalk marks, or wax seals
