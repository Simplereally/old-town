# Chaps (Ranged Legs)

> Ranged leg armour. Leather or cloth leg coverings with ranged bonuses. The ranged equivalent of Chausses or Wraps.

## Design

- **Slot:** Legs
- **Weight:** Light
- **Best for:** Ranged defence, mobility
- **Tradeoff:** Low melee defence, low magic defence
- **Visual:** Leather leggings or cloth chaps, sometimes with fringe, stitching, or bead trim
