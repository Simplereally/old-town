# Cuffs (Magic Hands)

> Magic hand armour. Cloth cuffs or bracers with magic bonuses. The magic equivalent of Gauntlets.

## Design

- **Slot:** Hands
- **Weight:** Light
- **Best for:** Magic attack, magic defence
- **Tradeoff:** No melee defence, no ranged defence
- **Visual:** Cloth cuffs or bracers, sometimes with bead trim or chalk marks
