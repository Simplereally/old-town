# Hand Armour

> All hand armour in Old Town. 13 tiers of Gauntlets.

## Gauntlets

> **Gauntlets** is the standard term for armoured gloves. It sounds medieval and grounded.

- **Slot:** Hands
- **Weight:** Light
- **Defence bonus:** Low
- **Attack bonus:** +1 per tier (gauntlets help grip weapons)
- **Best for:** Standard hand protection, slight attack bonus
