# Buckler (Ranged Offhand)

> Ranged offhand. A small, light shield or defensive board. The ranged equivalent of a Ward or Charmward.

## Design

- **Slot:** Off-hand
- **Weight:** Light
- **Best for:** Ranged defence, blocking ranged attacks
- **Tradeoff:** Weak against melee, no magic defence
- **Visual:** Small wooden or leather shield, sometimes with hide covering, stitching, or bead trim
