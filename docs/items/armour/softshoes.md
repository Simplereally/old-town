# Softshoes (Magic Feet)

> Magic foot armour. Cloth shoes or slippers with magic bonuses. The magic equivalent of Sabatons.

## Design

- **Slot:** Feet
- **Weight:** Light
- **Best for:** Magic defence, magic attack
- **Tradeoff:** No melee defence, no ranged defence
- **Visual:** Cloth shoes or soft slippers, sometimes with bead trim or chalk marks
