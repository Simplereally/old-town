# Jerkin (Ranged Chest)

> Ranged chest armour. Light leather or cloth torso with ranged bonuses. The ranged equivalent of a Harness or Robe.

## Design

- **Slot:** Chest
- **Weight:** Light
- **Best for:** Ranged defence, ranged accuracy
- **Tradeoff:** Low melee defence, low magic defence
- **Visual:** Light leather jacket or cloth vest, sometimes with stitching, buckles, or feather trim
