# Charmward (Magic Offhand)

> Magic offhand. A defensive charm-board, bead-frame, or warding focus. The magic equivalent of a Ward (shield).

## Design

- **Slot:** Off-hand
- **Weight:** Light
- **Best for:** Magic defence, magic attack
- **Tradeoff:** No melee defence, no ranged defence
- **Visual:** Small wooden board, charm-frame, or bead-lattice held in the offhand
