# Coif (Ranged Head)

> Ranged headgear. Cloth hood with ranged accuracy bonuses. The ranged equivalent of a Helm or Cowl.

## Design

- **Slot:** Head
- **Weight:** Light
- **Best for:** Ranged accuracy, ranged defence
- **Tradeoff:** Low melee defence, low magic defence
- **Visual:** Cloth hood or leather cap, sometimes with bead trim, stitching, or feather accents
