## Weapon System Overview

Weapons are the primary vehicle for player power expression. The weapon a player wields tells other players everything about their current status:

- **Cobbled** = total beginner, no money, no kills
- **Pennywrought** = just started, but trying
- **Bellmetal** = mid-early, town-respected
- **Blackbar** = PvP-oriented, aggressive
- **Wardensteel** = guard-aligned, disciplined
- **Blueglass** = first prestige, magical
- **Carmine Steel** = war-tested, dangerous
- **Starfall** = mythic, endgame

**Every weapon family has a signature mechanic.** A Sticker is not just a smaller Shortblade — it has Backstab. A Sabre is not just a curved Longblade — it has Slash Frenzy. See [`combat/signature-mechanics.md`](../../combat/signature-mechanics.md) for the full mechanic definitions.

## Design Rules

1. **Never use generic fantasy names.** No "Bronze Sword," "Iron Dagger," etc.
2. **Families are reusable.** A "Shortblade" is always a one-handed sword.
3. **Tiers modify the base family.** The family defines the silhouette and speed. The tier defines the material and stats.
4. **Cobbled is the joke.** It should have humorous descriptions and terrible stats.
5. **Starfall is the mythic.** It should feel like a different material category.
6. **Two-handed weapons trade shield for damage.** This is intentional.
7. **Reach weapons (Spear, Billhook, Glaive) get 2-tile range.** This is their defining advantage.
8. **Staves are magic enablers, not melee weapons.** Their melee stats should be terrible.
9. **Ranged weapons use Ranged Attack/Strength, not Melee.** Separate skill path.
10. **Attack speed is measured in ticks.** 4 = very fast, 5 = fast, 6 = slow, 7 = very slow, 8 = very slow, 9 = siege.
11. **Bows use organic tier names.** Lathwood, Bogwood, Bellhorn, Warden Yew, Greenwold, Gravebent, Carmine Ash, Argentwood, Crownhorn.
12. **Crossbows use metal tier names.** Pennywrought, Pig Iron, Bellmetal, Blackbar, Wardensteel, Graveiron, Blueglass, Carmine Steel, Argent, Crownsteel, Starfall.
13. **Magic weapons use magic tier names.** Chalkmarked, Tallowbound, Bellwax, Blacksalt, Warden Script, Greenwold, Gravebound, Blueglass, Carmine Ink, Argent Script, Crownvellum.
14. **Magic requires beads.** No beads, no spells. Bead management is core.
15. **Staves provide unlimited elemental beads.** This is their defining advantage.
16. **Primers and Codices are spellbooks.** Required for casting spells.
