# Thrown Knives

> The fastest ranged weapon family in Old Town. One-handed, short range, and hungry for ammo. The signature **Fan** mechanic lets a skilled thrower unleash two blades in a single tick.

## Thrown Knife

> Fast, one-handed thrown weapon. Deadly in close quarters, but you will burn through knives faster than any other ranged family.

- **Slot:** Weapon (one-handed)
- **Attack speed:** 4 ticks (fastest ranged)
- **Range:** 4 tiles (very short)
- **Hands:** 1H
- **Best for:** Rapid close-range combat, mobile skirmishing, shield combos
- **Signature mechanic:** **Fan** — can throw 2 knives per tick (each at 60% damage)
- **Tradeoff:** Very short range, ammo-hungry

## Design Notes

1. **Thrown knives are the fastest ranged weapon.** At 4 ticks, they outpace every bow and crossbow.
2. **Fan is the signature mechanic.** Throwing two knives per tick at 60% damage each means total output is 120% per tick, but ammo consumption doubles.
3. **Range is the tradeoff.** At 4 tiles, knives demand close quarters. You cannot snipe. You cannot siege. You get in, you cut, you get out.
4. **One-handed freedom.** Unlike bows and crossbows, knives leave the off-hand free for shields, torches, or secondary items.
5. **Cobbled is the joke.** It should feel like garbage someone found in a gutter.
6. **Starfall is the mythic.** It should feel like a weapon from another world, not just a better knife.
