# Javelins

> Heavy one-handed thrown weapons. Slow, piercing, and shield-breaking. The javelin is the anti-shield answer to ranged combat, trading speed and cost for armour penetration and the ability to crack defensive formations.

## Javelin Family

- **Slot:** Weapon (one-handed)
- **Attack speed:** 6 ticks
- **Range:** 6 tiles
- **Hands:** 1H
- **Best for:** Anti-shield combat, piercing armoured targets, one-handed ranged
- **Tradeoff:** Heavy, slow, expensive ammo
- **Signature mechanic:** **Pierce** — ignores 25% of target's ranged defence; can break shield block on hit
