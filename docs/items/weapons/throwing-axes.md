# Throwing Axes

> One-handed hybrid weapons that bridge melee and ranged combat. Thrown for distance, wielded for close quarters. A favourite of skirmishers, duelists, and anyone who wants to keep their options open.

## Throwing Axe Family

> The canonical throwing axe. Fast, versatile, and uniquely hybrid.

- **Slot:** Weapon (one-handed)
- **Attack speed:** 5 ticks
- **Range:** 5 tiles
- **Hands:** 1H
- **Best for:** Hybrid combat, skirmishing, melee-to-ranged switching
- **Signature mechanic:** **Hybrid** — damage scales with both Ranged Strength and Melee Strength (uses higher of the two)
- **Tradeoff:** No shield while throwing, mid-range

## Design Rules

1. **Throwing axes use metal tier names.** Pennywrought, Pig Iron, Bellmetal, Blackbar, Wardensteel, Graveiron, Blueglass, Carmine Steel, Argent, Crownsteel, Starfall.
2. **Tier 0 is Cobbled.** Scrap, rusted, improvised. Humorous descriptions, terrible stats.
3. **Hybrid mechanic is the signature.** No other ranged weapon family uses both Ranged Strength and Melee Strength. This is the throwing axe's identity.
4. **One-handed only.** Throwing axes are always 1H. No two-handed variant.
5. **Mid-range.** 5 tiles is shorter than bows and most crossbows. The tradeoff is versatility, not reach.
6. **No shield while throwing.** The throwing animation requires a free off-hand. You can wield a shield in melee stance, but must stow it to throw.
7. **Starfall is the mythic.** Meteor metal, different category, feels otherworldly.
