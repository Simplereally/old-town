## Tool System Overview

Tools are the enablers of skilling. You cannot cut a tree without an axe. You cannot mine a rock without a pickaxe. You cannot fish without a rod.

**Key principles:**
- **Tools are tiered.** A Pennywrought axe is better than a Cobbled axe.
- **Tools are non-stackable.** They take up inventory space.
- **Tools are equipable.** Some tools are also weapons (axes, pickaxes).
- **Tools wear down.** Durability is planned.
- **Tools are tradeable.** They are crafted by Smithing and sold.

## Design Rules

1. **Tools are non-stackable.** They take up inventory space.
2. **Tools are equipable.** Equipping a tool enables the skill.
3. **Higher-tier tools are faster.** They increase gathering speed.
4. **Tools are crafted by Smithing.** Axes, pickaxes, and rods are smithed.
5. **Some tools are also weapons.** Axes and pickaxes are weak weapons.
6. **Tools have durability.** They wear down over time (planned).
7. **Basic tools are cheap.** Pennywrought tools are affordable.
8. **Endgame tools are expensive.** Starfall tools are status symbols.
9. **Utility tools are not tiered.** Tinderboxes, needles, etc. do not have tiers.
10. **Bait is a resource.** Feathers, fishing traps, etc. are consumed while fishing.
