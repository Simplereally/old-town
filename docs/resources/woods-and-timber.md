# Woods and Timber

> **The canonical wood and timber taxonomy of Old Town.** Every tree, log, and kindling in the world is defined here. Woodcutting produces these. Bowcraft, Carpentry, and Hearthcraft consume them.
>
> **Rule:** No wood may be introduced without a canonical name, a tier position, and at least one production consumer.

---

## Individual Wood Definitions

### Riverwillow

- **Canonical name:** Riverwillow
- **Item ID:** `riverwillow_log`
- **Tier:** 1
- **Woodcutting level:** 15
- **Visual identity:** Pale, drooping, flexible. Found along riverbanks.
- **Economy role:** The first real wood for new woodcutters. Fast experience, low value.
- **Cultural framing:** Grows along the Wardenbrook. The wood is flexible and light, perfect for beginner bows.
- **Respawn time:** 10 seconds
- **Secondary drops:** Kindling (50%), Riverwillow bark (20%)
- **Production consumers:** Bowcraft (shortbow shafts), Carpentry (basic furniture), Hearthcraft (kindling)

### Oldroad Oak

- **Canonical name:** Oldroad Oak
- **Item ID:** `oldroad_oak_log`
- **Tier:** 2
- **Woodcutting level:** 20
- **Visual identity:** Brown, gnarled, strong. Found along the old roads.
- **Economy role:** Basic construction wood. The backbone of early Carpentry.
- **Cultural framing:** Grows along the old roads that connect the towns. The wood is strong and durable, used for building and tools.
- **Respawn time:** 15 seconds
- **Secondary drops:** Acorns (30%), Oldroad Oak bark (15%)
- **Production consumers:** Bowcraft (shortbow staves), Carpentry (basic construction), Hearthcraft (slow-burning fuel)

### Bellmaple

- **Canonical name:** Bellmaple
- **Item ID:** `bellmaple_log`
- **Tier:** 3
- **Woodcutting level:** 30
- **Visual identity:** Red-gold, smooth grain, beautiful. Found in the Bellwood.
- **Economy role:** Balanced wood. Good for both bows and furniture.
- **Cultural framing:** Named for the Bellwood, where the finest maples grow. The wood is red-gold and smooth, prized by carpenters.
- **Respawn time:** 20 seconds
- **Secondary drops:** Maple syrup (10%), Bellmaple leaves (25%)
- **Production consumers:** Bowcraft (longbow staves), Carpentry (fine furniture), Hearthcraft (maple syrup)

### Warden Yew

- **Canonical name:** Warden Yew
- **Item ID:** `warden_yew_log`
- **Tier:** 5
- **Woodcutting level:** 50
- **Visual identity:** Dark, dense, aromatic. Found in the Warden's Grove.
- **Economy role:** Premium bow wood. The best wood for warbows before the magical tier.
- **Cultural framing:** Named for the Warden's Grove, where the oldest yews grow. The wood is dark and dense, perfect for powerful bows.
- **Respawn time:** 40 seconds
- **Secondary drops:** Warden Yew berries (15%), Yew bark (20%)
- **Production consumers:** Bowcraft (warbow staves), Carpentry (elite furniture), Hearthcraft (ritual fires)

### Witchwood

- **Canonical name:** Witchwood
- **Item ID:** `witchwood_log`
- **Tier:** 8
- **Woodcutting level:** 75
- **Visual identity:** Blue-veined, glowing faintly. Found in the Witchwood.
- **Economy role:** Magical timber. Used for elite bows and magical furniture.
- **Cultural framing:** Named for the Witchwood, a forest where the trees are said to be alive with magic. The wood is blue-veined and glows faintly in the dark.
- **Respawn time:** 2 minutes
- **Secondary drops:** Witchwood sap (20%), Witchwood bark (10%)
- **Production consumers:** Bowcraft (elite bows), Carpentry (magical furniture), Hearthcraft (mystical fires)

### Crownheart

- **Canonical name:** Crownheart
- **Item ID:** `crownheart_log`
- **Tier:** 11
- **Woodcutting level:** 90
- **Visual identity:** Gold-black, regal, ancient. Found in the Crownheart Forest.
- **Economy role:** Royal wood. Used for the finest structures and bows.
- **Cultural framing:** Named for the Crownheart Forest, the oldest forest in Old Town. The wood is gold-black and regal, used for royal and masterwork items.
- **Respawn time:** 4 minutes
- **Secondary drops:** Crownheart resin (15%), Ancient bark (10%)
- **Production consumers:** Bowcraft (crown bows), Carpentry (royal structures), Hearthcraft (crown fires)

### Starfall Ash

- **Canonical name:** Starfall Ash
- **Item ID:** `starfall_ash_log`
- **Tier:** 12
- **Woodcutting level:** 99
- **Visual identity:** Iridescent, cosmic, shifting colours. Found in the Starfall Grove.
- **Economy role:** Endgame mythic timber. The ultimate woodcutting challenge.
- **Cultural framing:** Named for the legend that the grove was created by a falling star. The wood is iridescent and shifts colour, like the ore.
- **Respawn time:** 5 minutes
- **Secondary drops:** Starfall Ash bark (10%), Starfall sap (5%)
- **Production consumers:** Bowcraft (mythic bows), Carpentry (mythic structures), Hearthcraft (star fires)

## Wood Processing

Logs can be processed into:
- **Planks** — for Carpentry (sawmill)
- **Bow staves** — for Bowcraft
- **Arrow shafts** — for Bowcraft
- **Kindling** — for Hearthcraft

---

## Design Rules

1. **No banned names.** `magic tree`, `elder tree`, `redwood` are banned. Use the canonical names above.
2. **Every wood has a place.** Every tree species has a named location in Old Town: Wardenbrook, Bellwood, Witchwood, Crownheart Forest.
3. **Every wood has a consumer.** No wood is cut for its own sake. It must feed Bowcraft, Carpentry, or Hearthcraft.
4. **Respawn scaling.** Higher-tier trees have longer respawn times, creating scarcity.
5. **Visual identity.** Every wood has a visual description that guides asset creation.
6. **Cultural framing.** Every wood has a story that connects it to Old Town's world.
