# Herbs, Roots, and Fungi

> **The canonical herb, root, and fungus taxonomy of Old Town.** Every plant, herb, mushroom, and dye in the world is defined here. Gardening produces these. Apothecary, Cooking, and Handicraft consume them.
>
> **Rule:** No herb, root, or fungus may be introduced without a canonical name, a patch type, and at least one production consumer.

---

## Individual Herb Definitions

### Graveyard Moss

- **Canonical name:** Graveyard Moss
- **Item ID:** `graveyard_moss`
- **Tier:** 0
- **Gardening level:** 1
- **Patch type:** Allotment
- **Primary use:** Weak salves, starter potions
- **Visual identity:** Grey, damp, clingy. Found on gravestones and old walls.
- **Economy role:** The starting herb for every gardener. Abundant, low value, high volume.
- **Cultural framing:** Found on the gravestones of the Old Town graveyard. The moss is grey and damp, the first thing a gardener learns to harvest.
- **Growth time:** 5 minutes
- **Secondary drops:** Stone dust (50%)
- **Production consumers:** Apothecary (weak salves), Cooking (graveyard tea)

### Ironroot

- **Canonical name:** Ironroot
- **Item ID:** `ironroot`
- **Tier:** 2
- **Gardening level:** 15
- **Patch type:** Herb patch
- **Primary use:** Strength potions, iron-rich brews
- **Visual identity:** Red, tuberous, heavy. Found in iron-rich soil.
- **Economy role:** First real herb. The backbone of early Apothecary.
- **Cultural framing:** Named for its red, iron-rich roots. The herb is found in iron-rich soil, its roots heavy with mineral.
- **Growth time:** 15 minutes
- **Secondary drops:** Iron dust (20%)
- **Production consumers:** Apothecary (strength potions), Smithing (iron dust)

### Warden Herb

- **Canonical name:** Warden Herb
- **Item ID:** `warden_herb`
- **Tier:** 5
- **Gardening level:** 40
- **Patch type:** Herb patch
- **Primary use:** Warden potions, legal brews
- **Visual identity:** Blue-grey, legal-script flecks. Found in the Warden's Grove.
- **Economy role:** Midgame herb. The backbone of mid-tier Apothecary.
- **Cultural framing:** Named for the Warden's Grove, where it grows. The herb is blue-grey with flecks that look like legal script, used in warden potions.
- **Growth time:** 30 minutes
- **Secondary drops:** Warden stone dust (10%)
- **Production consumers:** Apothecary (warden potions), Handicraft (warden charms)

### Blueglass Cap

- **Canonical name:** Blueglass Cap
- **Item ID:** `blueglass_cap`
- **Tier:** 8
- **Gardening level:** 70
- **Patch type:** Mushroom patch
- **Primary use:** Glass potions, magical brews
- **Visual identity:** Blue, translucent, glowing. Found in dark caves.
- **Economy role:** Magical mushroom. Used for high-tier potions.
- **Cultural framing:** Named for its blue, glass-like cap. The mushroom grows in dark caves, its cap glowing faintly.
- **Growth time:** 1 hour
- **Secondary drops:** Blueglass spores (20%)
- **Production consumers:** Apothecary (glass potions), Beadwork (blueglass beads)

### Bloodroot

- **Canonical name:** Bloodroot
- **Item ID:** `bloodroot`
- **Tier:** 9
- **Gardening level:** 75
- **Patch type:** Herb patch
- **Primary use:** Carmine elixirs, blood potions
- **Visual identity:** Deep red, blood-like, sap. Found in dark soil.
- **Economy role:** Dark herb. Used for high-tier elixirs.
- **Cultural framing:** Named for its deep red colour and blood-like sap. The herb is found in dark soil, its roots bleeding red when cut.
- **Growth time:** 1.5 hours
- **Secondary drops:** Blood sap (15%)
- **Production consumers:** Apothecary (carmine elixirs), Cooking (blood broth)

### Starfall Petal

- **Canonical name:** Starfall Petal
- **Item ID:** `starfall_petal`
- **Tier:** 12
- **Gardening level:** 99
- **Patch type:** Starfall orchard
- **Primary use:** Starfall philtres, mastery potions
- **Visual identity:** Iridescent, cosmic, shifting colours. The ultimate herb.
- **Economy role:** Endgame herb. The ultimate gardening challenge.
- **Cultural framing:** Named for the legend that it was born from a falling star. The petal is iridescent and shifts colour, like the ore.
- **Growth time:** 2 hours
- **Secondary drops:** Starfall pollen (10%)
- **Production consumers:** Apothecary (starfall philtres), Handicraft (starfall charms)

## Design Rules

1. **No generic names.** Every herb has a specific, culturally grounded name.
2. **Every herb has a patch.** Every herb grows in a specific patch type: allotment, herb patch, mushroom patch, tree patch, starfall orchard.
3. **Every herb has a consumer.** No herb is grown for its own sake. It must feed Apothecary, Cooking, or Handicraft.
4. **Growth time scaling.** Higher-tier herbs have longer growth times, creating scarcity and planning.
5. **Visual identity.** Every herb has a visual description that guides asset creation.
6. **Cultural framing.** Every herb has a story that connects it to Old Town's world.
7. **Dye and crop plants.** Dye plants and crop plants are separate from herbs, but share the same gardening system.
