# Signature Weapon Mechanics

> **Design authority:** This document defines the unique mechanical identity of every weapon family in Old Town. A weapon is not just stats — it is a playstyle. If two families feel interchangeable, the design has failed.
> 
> **Rule:** Every family must have a signature mechanic that is visible, memorable, and mechanically significant. Higher tiers amplify the mechanic; they do not replace it.

## Philosophy

Old Town has 468 weapons across 36 families. Without distinct mechanics, they are 468 stat sticks. With signature mechanics, they are 468 decisions.

**Design principles:**
1. **Mechanics are family-level, not tier-level.** A Starfall Sticker is still a Sticker. It is faster and deadlier, but the identity is the same.
2. **Mechanics are readable at a glance.** A player should know what a weapon does by its silhouette.
3. **Mechanics create tradeoffs.** Every advantage has a cost. No family is "strictly better."
4. **Mechanics are simple.** One sentence per family. Complex combos are emergent, not required.

## Melee Family Identities

| Family | Speed | Range | Hands | Signature Mechanic | Tradeoff |
|--------|-------|-------|-------|-------------------|----------|
| **Sticker** | 4 ticks | 1 tile | 1H | **Backstab:** +15% damage when attacking from behind or while target is engaged with another player | Very low base damage |
| **Shortblade** | 5 ticks | 1 tile | 1H | **Reliable:** Damage variance is halved; hits are consistently average | No burst potential |
| **Longblade** | 5 ticks | 1 tile | 1H | **Balanced:** Standard baseline with no penalties; the "reference" weapon | No special advantage |
| **Greatblade** | 6 ticks | 1 tile | 2H | **Cleave:** 15% chance to hit a second adjacent target for 50% damage | Slow, no shield |
| **Sabre** | 5 ticks | 1 tile | 1H | **Slash Frenzy:** +10% attack speed after every third consecutive hit (resets on miss) | Weak against armoured targets |
| **Handaxe** | 5 ticks | 1 tile | 1H | **Armour Chip:** Every hit reduces target armour durability by 1 point (stacks) | Low damage against unarmoured |
| **Fellaxe** | 6 ticks | 1 tile | 2H | **Fell:** +25% damage against targets with >50% HP (execute weapon) | Slow, no shield |
| **Cudgel** | 5 ticks | 1 tile | 1H | **Stagger:** Every third hit has a 25% chance to stun for 1 tick | Low damage, short range |
| **Maul** | 7 ticks | 1 tile | 2H | **Crush:** Ignores 30% of target's armour defence | Slowest weapon, no shield |
| **Spear** | 5 ticks | 2 tiles | 1H | **Reach:** Can attack from 2 tiles; +10% damage against charging targets | Cannot use shield while attacking at 2 tiles |
| **Billhook** | 6 ticks | 2 tiles | 2H | **Hook:** 20% chance to pull target 1 tile closer (does not work on large enemies) | Slow, no shield, hook fails on bosses |
| **Glaive** | 6 ticks | 2 tiles | 2H | **Sweep:** Attacks hit all targets in a 2-tile arc (up to 3 targets) | Low per-target damage, no shield |

### Melee Mechanic Details

**Backstab (Sticker):** Positioning matters. A Sticker is a skirmisher's weapon — hit the back line, hit distracted targets, hit fleeing enemies. In PvP, the Sticker punishes players who turn their back. In PvM, it rewards flanking.

**Reliable (Shortblade):** The Shortblade is the "standard deviation" weapon. It never surprises you. It never crits for 50. It never whiffs for 0. It is the weapon for risk-averse players and consistent training.

**Slash Frenzy (Sabre):** The Sabre is the closest thing to a scimitar in Old Town. It rewards commitment. Three hits in a row unlock a burst of speed. Miss once, and the chain resets. This makes it feel like a dueling weapon — patient, rhythmic, then explosive.

**Armour Chip (Handaxe):** The Handaxe is not just a tool. It is an armour-breaker. Every hit degrades the target's armour. Over a long fight, a Handaxe makes the target softer for everyone. This gives it a unique role in group PvM and extended PvP.

**Stagger (Cudgel):** The Cudgel is the crowd-control weapon. Every third hit has a chance to interrupt. This is not a stun-lock — it is a rhythm breaker. It punishes spellcasters, crossbow reloaders, and slow weapons.

**Crush (Maul):** The Maul ignores armour. It is the anti-tank weapon. Against a player in full Platecoat, the Maul is devastating. Against a player in rags, it is slow overkill. This creates a clear strategic niche.

**Hook (Billhook):** The Billhook is the disengagement weapon. It pulls enemies closer, breaking their range advantage. It is the anti-kite weapon. In PvP, it punishes ranged players who try to dance at 2 tiles. In PvM, it prevents flee.

**Sweep (Glaive):** The Glaive is the area-control weapon. One swing hits multiple targets. It is the weapon for fighting groups of weak enemies or controlling choke points. Against single targets, it is inferior to a Greatblade.

## Ranged Family Identities

### Bows

| Family | Speed | Range | Hands | Signature Mechanic | Tradeoff |
|--------|-------|-------|-------|-------------------|----------|
| **Bentbow** | 5 ticks | 5 tiles | 2H | **Tutorial:** No mechanic. Exists to teach bow basics. | Terrible stats |
| **Shortbow** | 5 ticks | 6 tiles | 2H | **Rapid:** +10% attack speed when target is within 3 tiles | Short range, no long-range bonus |
| **Longbow** | 6 ticks | 8 tiles | 2H | **Range:** +10% accuracy when target is beyond 6 tiles | Slow, no close-range bonus |
| **Recurve** | 5 ticks | 7 tiles | 2H | **Skirmish:** No movement penalty while drawing; can step between shots | Lower damage than Longbow |
| **Warbow** | 7 ticks | 8 tiles | 2H | **Heavy:** +20% damage when target is beyond 6 tiles; arrows have knockback | Slowest bow, no close-range |
| **Quickbow** | 4 ticks | 5 tiles | 2H | **Proccer:** 10% chance per hit to fire a second arrow instantly | Low damage per arrow, ammo-hungry |
| **Stillbow** | 6 ticks | 9 tiles | 2H | **First Shot:** First arrow after 3 seconds of stillness deals +25% damage | Requires patience; no sustained DPS |
| **Starbow** | 5 ticks | 10 tiles | 2H | **Starfall:** 15% chance to trigger a meteor strike (AOE damage, 2-tile radius) | Mythic rarity; meteor can hit friendlies |

### Thrown Weapons

Thrown weapons are one-handed, fast, and short-range. They are the bridge between melee and ranged. All 4 families are now in the full inventory.

| Family | Speed | Range | Hands | Signature Mechanic | Tradeoff |
|--------|-------|-------|-------|-------------------|----------|
| **Knives** | 4 ticks | 4 tiles | 1H | **Fan:** Can throw 2 knives per tick (each at 60% damage) | Very short range, ammo-hungry |
| **Darts** | 3 ticks | 5 tiles | 1H | **Venom:** Each dart has a 15% chance to apply poison (3 ticks, 2 damage/tick) | Lowest ranged damage, single-target |
| **Javelins** | 6 ticks | 6 tiles | 1H | **Pierce:** Ignores 25% of target's ranged defence; can break shield block on hit | Heavy, slow, expensive ammo |
| **Throwing Axes** | 5 ticks | 5 tiles | 1H | **Hybrid:** Damage scales with both Ranged Strength and Melee Strength (uses higher of the two) | No shield while throwing, mid-range |

## Magic Family Identities

| Family | Speed | Hands | Signature Mechanic | Tradeoff |
|--------|-------|-------|-------------------|----------|
| **Wand** | 4 ticks | 1H | **Efficient:** -1 bead consumed per spell (minimum 1 bead) | No melee damage, no bead savings |
| **Staff** | 5 ticks | 2H | **Channel:** Provides unlimited elemental beads of one type while equipped | Cannot use off-hand, slow melee |
| **Rod** | 5 ticks | 2H | **Battlemage:** Can cast spells AND melee attack; melee damage scales with Magic level | Lower magic bonuses than Staff, lower melee than weapons |
| **Focus** | N/A | 1H (off) | **Amplify:** +15% magic accuracy when paired with a Wand or Rod | No melee stats, no bead savings |
| **Primer** | N/A | 1H | **Basic:** Required to cast tier 1-3 spells; holds 5 spells | Cannot cast advanced spells, takes off-hand slot |
| **Codex** | N/A | 1H | **Advanced:** Required to cast tier 4-6 spells; holds 10 spells | Cannot cast basic spells efficiently, takes off-hand slot |

### Magic Mechanic Details

**Efficient (Wand):** The Wand makes cheap spells cheaper. A spell that costs 2 beads costs 1. A spell that costs 1 bead still costs 1. This makes the Wand the training weapon — it stretches your bead supply. It does not save you money on expensive spells.

**Channel (Staff):** The Staff is the sustained casting weapon. It provides unlimited elemental beads of one type (e.g., unlimited Ember beads). This means you never run out of your primary element. But you still need catalytic beads (Wit, Grave, etc.) for complex spells. The Staff is for long grinds, not for burst.

**Battlemage (Rod):** The Rod is the hybrid weapon. It can cast spells AND melee. Its melee damage is weak compared to a melee weapon but stronger than a Staff. Its magic bonuses are weaker than a Staff but stronger than a Wand. It is the "jack of all trades" for players who want to switch between magic and melee without swapping gear.

**Amplify (Focus):** The Focus is the off-hand accuracy booster. It does not cast spells. It does not save beads. It just makes your spells hit more often. Paired with a Wand, it creates a "precision caster" build. Paired with a Rod, it creates a "battlemage who actually hits."

**Basic (Primer) vs Advanced (Codex):** The Primer and Codex are not weapons — they are spellbooks. But they are equipment decisions. The Primer is for early magic: cheap, limited, but accessible. The Codex is for advanced magic: required for high-tier spells, but expensive and heavy. A player with a Codex and a Staff is a "high-tier sustained caster." A player with a Primer and a Wand is a "cheap training caster."

## How Mechanics Create Playstyle Archetypes

| Archetype | Weapons | Why It Works |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **Skirmisher** | Sticker + Sabre | Backstab + Slash Frenzy = hit-and-run, then commit |
| **Tank Buster** | Maul + Handaxe | Crush + Armour Chip = ignore armour, then degrade it |
| **Crowd Control** | Cudgel + Glaive | Stagger + Sweep = interrupt the big one, clear the small ones |
| **Duelist** | Sabre + Billhook | Slash Frenzy + Hook = stay in range, never let them escape |
| **Sniper** | Stillbow + Longbow | First Shot + Range = one devastating opener, then consistent pressure |
| **Siege** | Arbalest + Greatbow | Penetration + Siege = break armour, then break formations |
| **Battlemage** | Rod + Focus | Battlemage + Amplify = cast, melee, cast, melee — never stop |
| **Economy Mage** | Wand + Primer | Efficient + Basic = train magic on a budget |
| **Endgame Mage** | Staff + Codex | Channel + Advanced = unlimited elemental, rare spells — the full toolkit |

## Design Rules for Signature Mechanics

1. **Every family must have a mechanic.** If a family only has stats, it has failed.
2. **Mechanics are simple.** One sentence, one table cell. No multi-step combos.
3. **Mechanics scale with tier.** Higher tiers increase the percentage, the chance, or the magnitude. They do not unlock new mechanics.
4. **Mechanics create tradeoffs.** Every advantage has a clear cost.
5. **Mechanics are visible.** A player should know what a weapon does by its name and silhouette.
6. **Mechanics stack, but not exponentially.** A Sabre's Slash Frenzy and a Focus's Amplify are independent. They do not create infinite loops.
7. **Mechanics are not random.** A 10% chance is fine. A 50% chance is a coin flip. A 1% chance is a lottery.
8. **Mechanics respect the 600ms tick.** No real-time mechanics. Everything is tick-based.
9. **Mechanics are content, not code.** They are defined here. They are implemented in the engine later.
10. **Thrown weapons are in inventory.** All 4 families (Knives, Darts, Javelins, Throwing Axes) have full 13-tier tables. They follow the same signature mechanic and tier structure as all other families.

### How Accessories Amplify Weapon Identity

**Sabre duelist:** The Carmine Duelist Cape (+5% melee speed in duels) and Carmine Eye (+5% magic accuracy in duels) reward the duelist's commitment to one target. The Carmine Thrower's Belt (+5% thrown ammo capacity in duels) supports the Sabre's fast, rhythmic attack pattern by ensuring the player never runs out of throwing knives. These are not generic bonuses — they are specific to the duelist's need for speed and burst.

**Fellaxe boss hunter:** The Greenwold Cloak (+3% ranged accuracy in forests) and Greenwold Heart (+3% magic accuracy in forests) support the boss hunter's wilderness focus. The Greenwold Antler (+7% damage vs. druids) is a boss trophy that amplifies the Fellaxe's execute identity against large enemies. The Greenwold set is the hunter's kit.

**Cudgel stagger:** The Bell Boar Tusk (+4% damage vs. boars) and Bellchain (+4% magic accuracy, +1% prayer) support the Cudgel's stagger identity against large beasts. The Bellbuckled Belt (+4 inventory slots, +1% prayer) ensures the Cudgel user can sustain long fights while maintaining the stagger rhythm.

**Maul armour-breaker:** The Blackbar Gorget (+5% magic accuracy, +2% melee damage in PvP) and Blackbar Ring (+5% Strength, +2% melee damage in PvP) amplify the Maul's armour-breaking identity. The Blacksealed Mantle (+6% defence, +2% melee speed in PvP) makes the armour-breaker a PvP threat. The Blackbar set is the aggressive kit.

**Spear / lawful control:** The Warden Cape (+7% defence, +2% accuracy vs. criminals) and Warden Signet (+6% Strength, +2% accuracy vs. criminals) reinforce the Spear's lawful, defensive identity. The Warden Utility Belt (+6 inventory slots, +2% tool durability) supports the Warden's practical, regulated lifestyle.

**Bow skirmisher:** The Greenwold Cloak (+3% ranged accuracy in forests) and Greenwold Ring (+7% Strength, +3% melee accuracy in forests) support the Bow skirmisher's wilderness hit-and-run pattern. The Greenwold Sprig (+7% drop rate, +3% drop rate in forests) makes the skirmisher a profitable hunter.

**Crossbow tank-buster:** The Blueglass Lens Ring (+9% Strength, +4% ranged accuracy) and Blueglass Cartridge Belt (+9 inventory slots, +4% ammo capacity) maximize the Crossbow's precision and ammo sustain. The Blueglass Eye (+9% damage vs. golems) is a boss trophy that supports the tank-buster's anti-armour role.

**Knife fan build:** The Carmine Thrower's Belt (+10 inventory slots, +5% thrown ammo capacity in duels) directly amplifies the Knife's Fan mechanic by ensuring the knife-thrower never runs out of ammo. The Carmine Tassel (+10% drop rate, +5% drop rate in PvP) supports the skirmisher's looting identity.

**Dart venom build:** The Blacksalt Charm (+5% drop rate, +2% poison duration in PvP) extends the Dart's Venom mechanic, making the poison more oppressive. The Carmine Thrower's Belt (+5% thrown ammo capacity in duels) supports the dart-thrower's ammo needs. The Blackbar Belt (+5% inventory slots, +2% ammo capacity in PvP) provides PvP sustain.

**Javelin anti-shield:** The Blueglass Lens Ring (+4% ranged accuracy) and Warden Utility Belt (+6 inventory slots, +2% tool durability) support the Javelin's precision and preparation. The Warden Cape (+7% defence, +2% accuracy vs. criminals) adds lawful flair to the anti-shield build.

**Throwing axe hybrid:** The Blackbar Belt (+5% inventory slots, +2% ammo capacity in PvP) and Greenwold Antler (+7% damage vs. druids) support the throwing axe's hybrid identity. The Greenwold Cloak (+3% ranged accuracy in forests) and Blackbar Ring (+5% Strength, +2% melee damage in PvP) provide both wilderness and PvP bonuses.

**Bead economy mage:** The Tallow Bead Amulet (+3% magic accuracy, +5% bead savings) and Blueglass Bead (+9% drop rate, +4% bead drop rate) are the core of the bead economy. The Waxed Pouch Belt (+3% inventory slots, +5% rain resistance) supports the mage's early-game survival. The Threadbare Cape (+3% defence, +1% skilling XP) provides skilling synergy.

**Writ utility mage:** The Warden Writ Amulet (+6% magic accuracy, +2% accuracy vs. criminals) and Warden Cape (+7% defence, +2% accuracy vs. criminals) create the lawful mage identity. The Warden Signet (+6% Strength, +2% accuracy vs. criminals) and Warden Utility Belt (+6 inventory slots, +2% tool durability) support the Warden's practical, regulated lifestyle.

**Grave caster:** The Grave Bead Amulet (+8% magic accuracy, +3% magic accuracy vs. undead) and Graveshroud (+9% defence, +3% magic accuracy vs. undead) are the core of the Grave caster's undead-focused identity. The Gravebone Charm (+8% drop rate, +3% drop rate from undead) and Grave Runner Belt (+8% inventory slots, +3% run energy in graveyards) support the graveyard lifestyle.

**Cleanse/support mage:** The Argent Reliquary (+11% magic accuracy, +3% magic defence for nearby allies) and Argent Mercy Ring (+11% Strength, +3% healing received) are the core of the support identity. The Argent Prayer Knot (+11% drop rate, +3% healing received) and Argent Provision Belt (+11% inventory slots, +3% consumable efficiency) support the support mage's sustain and team protection.

**Endgame flex:** The Starfall Cape (+14% defence, +5% all accuracy) and Starfall Astrolabe (+13% magic accuracy, +5% all accuracy) are the universal endgame kit. The Starfall Ring (+13% Strength, +5% all accuracy) and Starfall Shard Charm (+13% drop rate, +5% all drop rates) provide the highest bonuses in the game. The Starfall Girdle (+13% inventory slots, +5% all ammo capacity) and Starfall Heart-Shard (+13% damage vs. all monsters) are the endgame trophies. All Starfall bonuses are tightly capped in PvP.

### Design Rules for Accessory Build Glue

1. **Accessories amplify, they do not replace.** A Cape does not make a Sticker better than a Shortblade. It makes a Sticker *more* of a Sticker.
2. **Accessories are band-matched.** A Carmine weapon pairs with Carmine accessories. A Blueglass weapon pairs with Blueglass accessories. This is not mandatory, but it is the intended path.
3. **Accessories have PvP caps.** No accessory bonus exceeds 15% in PvP. This prevents accessory stacking from breaking balance.
4. **Accessories are visible or invisible.** Capes are visible. Charms are invisible. This creates social and private identity layers.
5. **Accessories are earned or crafted.** They are not bought from a general store. This makes them meaningful choices.
