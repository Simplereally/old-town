# Starter Creatures

Use a tight first set. These creatures are enough for starter combat, drops, contracts, and resource loops.

| Creature | Area | Combat Role | Economy Role |
|----------|------|-------------|--------------|
| Cellar Rat | Sootcellar / Counting House basement | First target, low damage | Tails, bones, torn cloth |
| Soot Rat | Sootcellar deeper rooms | Tougher rat, disease hint | Soot fur, Blackcoal Ash |
| Stray Dog | Alleys / Oldroad Gate | Movement, pursuit, flee logic | Hide scrap, small bone |
| Mud Goblin | North quarry road | First real humanoid | Coins, crude tools, Pig Iron Scrap |
| Bell Bat | Roofs / old bell tower | Ranged and magic target | Bellfang, Wing Hide, sinew |
| Bog Fox | South fields | Trapping crossover | Fox Hide, Sinew Cord, Fox Tooth |
| Grave Mite | Gravegate edge | First creepy enemy | Grave Dust, Bone Chips |
| Grave Wisp | Gravegate / Shrine edge | Magic-resistant teaching enemy | Faint Bead, Grave Ash |
| Ash Drake Whelp | Old kiln / rare Sootcellar | First drake tease | Warm Scale, ash, Drake Tooth |
| Blacksealed Cutpurse | Market alleys | Rogue humanoid, Sleight theme | Blank Writ, Wax Seal, stolen coin pouch |
| Road Crow | Oldroad Gate | Low ranged/trapping bird | Bellfeathers, Crow Beak |
| River Snapper | River Stoop bank | Fishing/combat crossover | Snapper Meat, Shell Chip |
