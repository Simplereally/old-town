# Riddle and Phrase Steps

Riddle Steps are text hints that point to a location, NPC, or object. Phrase Steps require the player to say or select a specific phrase near a target. All riddles and phrases use existing world content.

## Riddle Design Rules

1. Riddles must reference existing locations, NPCs, or objects.
2. Riddles should be solvable by a player who knows the area.
3. Riddles should not require external wiki reference.
4. Riddles can be poetic but not cryptic to the point of obscurity.
5. Riddles should feel like Old Town: dusty, slightly worn, local.
6. Phrase steps must use exact phrases that an NPC can recognize.
7. Joke steps are allowed at low tiers. They should be silly, not mocking.
