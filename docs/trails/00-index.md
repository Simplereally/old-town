# Trails

Oldroad Trails are Old Town's clue-scroll equivalent. A Trail is a sequence of steps that sends a player across the world to solve riddles, read sketches, perform gestures, dig for Caches, and survive ambushes. Trails come from play, not from shops. They are repeatable, optional, and cosmetic-heavy.

## What Trails Are

- Trails are repeatable world-riddles that reuse existing content.
- Trails send players across districts, skills, stations, and NPCs they already know.
- Trails reward cosmetics, coins, materials, and strange utility.
- Trails create demand for low-tier items and forgotten districts.
- Trails give Cartography, Sleight, and Wayfaring a non-combat purpose.

## What Trails Are Not

- Trails are not quests. They have no narrative arc, no cutscenes, no permanent state.
- Trails are not a faction system. They do not unlock reputation, ranks, or standing.
- Trails are not a daily system. There is no daily limit, no streak, no FOMO.
- Trails are not a battle pass. There is no seasonal track, no paid tier, no timed exclusives.
- Trails are not mandatory for progression. A player can ignore Trails entirely and play the full game.
- Trails are not a wiki dependency. Steps should be readable without external reference.

| crown | Crown Trail (tier 5) |
| star | Starfall Trail (tier 6) |
| cache | Reward container from a completed Trail |
| nook | Buildable local storage for Trail Gear |
| trail gear | Cosmetic items worn for Dress Steps |
| trail fool | NPC that appears during certain steps (Uri equivalent) |
