# Trail Tiers

Old Town has six Trail tiers. Each tier has a distinct identity, step count, risk level, source pool, reward identity, and player shorthand.

## Tier Summary

| Tier | Trail | Steps | Risk | Reward Identity |
|------|-------|-------|------|-----------------|
| 1 | Scrap Trail | 1-2 | Safe | Coins, starter materials, Bell Tokens, silly cosmetics |
| 2 | Chalk Trail | 2-3 | Safe to Caution | Bead materials, chalk cosmetics, small utility |
| 3 | Writ Trail | 3-4 | Caution | Writ-themed cosmetics, permit fragments, Favour items |
| 4 | Grave Trail | 3-5 | Danger | Grave beads, candle trims, bone trophies, crypt keys |
| 5 | Crown Trail | 4-6 | Danger/Peril | Elegant cosmetics, route unlock hooks, rare ornaments |
| 6 | Starfall Trail | 5-7 | Peril | Master cosmetics, unique trophies, strange utility |

## Scrap Trail

Identity: gutters, rat nests, cheap paper, market scraps.

Step count: 1-2.
Risk: safe.
Sources: rats, starter skilling, bins, beginner Warden tasks.
Rewards: coins, starter materials, Bell Tokens, silly cosmetics.
Player shorthand: scrap.

Visual identity: torn parchment, grease stains, rat tooth marks.
Carrier name: Scrap of Oldroad.

Step type pool: riddle, sketch, gesture, item, dig, phrase.
Area pool: Market Bell, Foundry Row, Lath Yard, Patch Lane, Chalkhouse Court, Oldroad Gate.
Skill requirements: none. Starter-level access only.

## Chalk Trail

Identity: bead dust, chalk marks, shop walls, apprentice scribbles.

Step count: 2-3.
Risk: safe to caution.
Sources: Beadwork, Chalkhouse, Cartography, starter bosses.
Rewards: bead materials, chalk cosmetics, small utility items.
Player shorthand: chalk.

Visual identity: chalk dust, bead impressions, scribe's mark.
Carrier name: Chalked Scrap.

Step type pool: riddle, sketch, survey, gesture, dress, item, station, skill, dig, phrase.
Area pool: all starter districts plus River Stoop, Warden Steps, Sootcellar, Bellwood Copse.
Skill requirements: up to level 20 in relevant skills.

## Writ Trail

Identity: wax seals, legal nonsense, permits, stamped clues.

Step count: 3-4.
Risk: caution.
Sources: Wardenry, Counting House, Quill Office, Sleight.
Rewards: Writ-themed cosmetics, permit fragments, Favour items.
Player shorthand: writ.

Visual identity: wax seal, shrine ribbon, formal hand.
Carrier name: Sealed Writ.

Step type pool: riddle, sketch, survey, gesture, dress, item, station, skill, sleight, shrine, warden, dig, phrase, ambush.
Area pool: all starter and first-ring districts.
Skill requirements: up to level 40 in relevant skills.
Ambush: may spawn a false_clerk or low-tier nuisance.

## Grave Trail

Identity: grave dirt, bone dust, flowers, wrong names.

Step count: 3-5.
Risk: danger.
Sources: Lowgrave, Gravegate, grave monsters, Favour rites.
Rewards: Grave beads, candle trims, bone trophies, crypt keys.
Player shorthand: grave.

Visual identity: grave dirt, bone dust, crypt moss.
Carrier name: Grave Scrap.

Step type pool: riddle, phrase, sketch, survey, gesture, dress, item, station, skill, sleight, shrine, warden, boss, dig, ambush, lockbox.
Area pool: all starter and first-ring districts, including Lowgrave and crypt areas.
Skill requirements: up to level 60 in relevant skills.
Ambush: may spawn grave_scratcher, grave_mite, or grave_wisp.

## Crown Trail

Identity: perfumed paper, gold leaf, old wealthy routes.

Step count: 4-6.
Risk: danger/peril.
Sources: second-ring bosses, high Cartography, rare Warden tasks.
Rewards: elegant cosmetics, route unlock hooks, rare ornaments.
Player shorthand: crown.

Visual identity: gold leaf, heraldic ink, perfumed paper.
Carrier name: Crowned Scroll.

Step type pool: all step types except no ambush at final step.
Area pool: all areas including second-ring districts.
Skill requirements: up to level 75 in relevant skills.
Ambush: may spawn crown_courier, hired_duelist, or bellstruck_stranger.

## Starfall Trail

Identity: silver thread, star watermark, no author.

Step count: 5-7.
Risk: peril.
Sources: rare global rolls, endgame skilling, bosses, Cartography.
Rewards: master cosmetics, unique trophies, strange utility.
Player shorthand: star.

Visual identity: silver thread, star watermark, no author.
Carrier name: Starfall Parchment.

Step type pool: all step types including rare combinations.
Area pool: all areas including second-ring and hidden districts.
Skill requirements: up to level 90 in relevant skills.
Ambush: may spawn bellstruck_stranger, wrong_walker, or starless_witness.

## Tier Progression

Players naturally encounter higher tiers as they play:

- Levels 1-15: mostly Scrap, occasional Chalk.
- Levels 15-30: Scrap, Chalk, occasional Writ.
- Levels 30-50: Chalk, Writ, occasional Grave.
- Levels 50-70: Writ, Grave, occasional Crown.
- Levels 70-90: Grave, Crown, occasional Starfall.
- Levels 90+: Crown, Starfall.

This is a rough guide. A level 10 player can find a Starfall Trail from a rare roll, but the skill requirements will likely prevent completion.
