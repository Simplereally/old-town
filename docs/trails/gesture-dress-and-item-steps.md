# Gesture, Dress, and Item Steps

Gesture Steps require the player to perform an emote. Dress Steps require the player to wear specific items. Item Steps require the player to bring, show, or use an item.

## Gesture Steps

Gesture Steps require the player to perform an emote or ritual action at a specific location.

### Gesture Examples

| Gesture | Location | Tier |
|---------|----------|------|
| Bow at the Market Bell | Market Bell | Scrap |
| Shiver at Gravegate | Gravegate Entrance | Scrap |
| Cheer beside the Warden Board | Warden Steps | Scrap |
| Think near the Counting House | Counting House | Chalk |
| Panic near the Old Kiln | Old Kiln | Chalk |
| Clap at Bellwood Copse | Bellwood Copse | Chalk |
| Kneel at Lowgrave Chapel | Lowgrave Chapel | Writ |
| Salute at the Warden Steps post | Warden Steps Post | Writ |
| Wave at the River Stoop ferryman | River Stoop Ferry Dock | Writ |
| Dance at the Chalkhouse Court kiln | Chalkhouse Court | Writ |
| Cry at the Gravegate crypt | Gravegate Crypt | Grave |
| Laugh at the Crowmile Road sign | Crowmile Road | Grave |

### Gesture Rules

- Gesture Steps require the player to have the emote unlocked.
- Starter gestures are available to all players.
- Higher-tier gestures may require unlocks from other systems (quests, achievements, etc.).
- Gesture Steps should not require rare or expensive emotes.
- Gesture Steps should feel like a natural action at the location.

## Dress Steps

Dress Steps require the player to wear specific Trail Gear or cosmetic items at a location.

### Dress Examples

| Items | Location | Tier |
|-------|----------|------|
| Wear a Ragged Cape and Rope Belt | Market Bell | Scrap |
| Wear Patchhide Chaps | Patch Lane | Scrap |
| Wear Chalkmarked Wand and Primer | Chalkhouse Court | Chalk |
| Wear Bellwood cosmetic | Bellwood Copse | Chalk |
| Wear Grave candle trim | Lowgrave | Writ |
| Wear no metal | Witchwood Verge | Writ |
| Wear Crown ledger spectacles | Counting House | Grave |
| Wear Starfall thread cape trim | Wardenbrook | Crown |
| Wear Wrong Walker mask | Crowmile Road | Crown |
| Wear Trail fool outfit | Market Bell | Starfall |

### Dress Rules

- Dress Steps should use cheap or cosmetic items, not expensive mandatory gear.
- Nooks exist to reduce bank friction for Dress Steps.
- A player should not need to buy a best-in-slot item to complete a Dress Step.
- Trail Gear is cosmetic-only. It provides no stats.
- Dress Steps should be readable. The player should know what items to wear.
- Dress Steps can reference items from the Trail reward pool.

### Item Rules

- Item Steps should use items that exist in the game world.
- Item Steps should not require extremely rare or expensive items.
- Item Steps can create demand for low-tier items that are otherwise forgotten.
- Item Steps should be readable. The player should know what item to bring.
- Item Steps can reference items from the Trail reward pool or from other content systems.

## Nook Integration

Nooks reduce bank friction for Dress and Item Steps.

- A player can store Trail Gear in a Nook near the step location.
- Nooks hold narrow item sets. A Crate Nook holds food and fish. A Wall Nook holds maps and Trail Gear.
- Nooks are built, not bought. They require materials and skill levels.
- Nooks are permanent local unlocks. Once built, they remain.
- Nooks do not solve the clue. They only reduce the number of bank trips.
