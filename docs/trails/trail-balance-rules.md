# Trail Balance Rules

These rules keep Trails fair, optional, and rewarding without being mandatory.

## Core Rules

1. **Trails are optional.** A player can ignore Trails entirely and experience the full game.
2. **Trails send players across existing content.** They do not invent empty chores.
3. **Skilling must generate Trails.** Combat is not the only source.
4. **Combat must not be the only source.** Skilling, Wardenry, Cartography, and Sleight all produce Trails.
5. **Higher Trail tiers can require more areas, items, skills, and danger.** They do not require more time.
6. **Trail rewards are cosmetic-heavy.** The most desirable rewards are visual.
7. **Low-tier rare cosmetics are allowed.** A Scrap Trail can drop a silly cosmetic that a player wants.
8. **No universal best-in-slot Trail gear.** Trail Gear is cosmetic-only.
9. **No daily Trail limit.** A player can do as many Trails as they can find.
10. **No Trail battle pass.** There is no seasonal track, no paid tier, no timed exclusives.
11. **Nooks reduce friction but do not solve the clue.** A Nook stores Trail Gear but does not reveal the step answer.
12. **A player can sell a Trail they cannot complete.** The buyer accepts the risk of requirements.
13. **Starfall Trails should feel extremely rare.** A player might see one per month of regular play.
14. **Trail steps must be readable without external wiki dependency.** A player should understand what to do from the clue text alone.

## Effort Bands

| Tier | Steps | Time (estimated) | Risk |
|------|-------|-----------------|------|
| Scrap | 1-2 | 2-5 minutes | None |
| Chalk | 2-3 | 5-10 minutes | Low |
| Writ | 3-4 | 10-20 minutes | Medium |
| Grave | 3-5 | 15-30 minutes | Medium-High |
| Crown | 4-6 | 20-40 minutes | High |
| Starfall | 5-7 | 30-60 minutes | High |

These are estimates. A player who knows the world well completes Trails faster. A new player takes longer.

## Reward Calibration

| Tier | Coin Range | Material Value | Cosmetic Chance | Rare Utility Chance |
|------|------------|----------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Scrap | 10-100 | Low | 5% | 0% |
| Chalk | 50-500 | Low-Medium | 10% | 1% |
| Writ | 100-1000 | Medium | 15% | 2% |
| Grave | 500-2500 | Medium-High | 20% | 3% |
| Crown | 1000-5000 | High | 25% | 5% |
| Starfall | 5000-20000 | Very High | 30% | 10% |

These are provisional tuning values. They will be adjusted based on playtesting.

## Anti-Abuse Rules

1. **No Trail farming.** A player cannot repeatedly farm the same monster or skill node for Trails.
2. **Trail drops have cooldowns.** A specific source cannot drop a Trail more than once per time window.
3. **No botting.** Trail steps require human-readable clues. Bots cannot solve them easily.
4. **No market manipulation.** Trail items are tradeable but Caches are not. This prevents market flooding.
5. **No power creep.** Trail rewards are cosmetic or utility. They do not increase combat stats.
