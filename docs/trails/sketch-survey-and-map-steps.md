# Sketch, Survey, and Map Steps

Sketch Steps, Survey Steps, and Dig Steps are the spatial heart of Trails. They send players to landmarks, teach area memory, and give Cartography a strong non-combat purpose.

## Sketch Steps

A Sketch Step shows the player a crude drawing of a local landmark. The player must match the sketch to a real location and travel there.

### Sketch Style

Old Town sketches are low-fi and readable:

- Simple district silhouette
- Landmark outline (bell tower, ferry post, grave marker)
- Road bend or intersection
- Grave row or crypt entrance
- Riverbank or dock post
- Cellar hatch or quarry cart
- Wrong tree or stump mark

### Sketch Examples

| Sketch | Location | Tier |
|--------|----------|------|
| A bell tower with three windows, viewed from the east | Market Bell | Scrap |
| A ferry rope between two posts, viewed from the bank | River Stoop Ferry Dock | Scrap |
| A grave row with seven markers, one taller than the rest | Gravegate Crypt Row | Chalk |
| A road that bends left then right, with a stump at the bend | Oldroad Oak | Chalk |
| A kiln with a single chimney, smoke going left | Old Kiln | Chalk |
| A wall with a loose brick, viewed from the alley | Sootcellar Wall | Writ |
| A copse with a fallen bell in the center | Bellwood Deep | Writ |
| A riverbank with three stones and a rope tied to the middle | Wardenbrook Tide Pool | Writ |

### Sketch Rules

- Sketches are simple line drawings. No photorealism.
- Sketches should be recognizable by a player who has visited the area.
- Sketches can include orientation cues (compass rose, shadow direction).
- Higher-tier sketches can be more abstract or cropped.
- Sketches should teach area memory. A player who completes many Trails learns the map.

## Survey Steps

A Survey Step requires the player to use Cartography at a landmark. This gives Cartography a non-combat purpose and rewards players who invest in the skill.

### Survey Examples

| Survey | Landmark | Tier |
|--------|----------|------|
| Survey the Market Bell from the north side | Market Bell | Chalk |
| Copy the mark on Oldroad Signpost | Oldroad Signpost | Chalk |
| Rub chalk from Chalkhouse Court wall | Chalkhouse Court | Chalk |
| Map the ferry rope post at Wardenbrook | Wardenbrook Ferry Dock | Writ |
| Survey three grave markers in Lowgrave | Lowgrave Grave Row | Writ |
| Map the bend before Crowmile Road | Crowmile Road Bend | Writ |
| Survey the Old Kiln from the ridge | Old Kiln Ridge | Grave |
| Map the fallen bell in Bellwood Deep | Bellwood Fallen Bell | Grave |
| Copy the crypt mark at Lowgrave Chapel | Lowgrave Chapel | Grave |
| Survey the starfall stone at Wardenbrook | Wardenbrook Starfall Stone | Crown |

### Survey Rules

- Beginner survey steps should not need coordinate precision.
- Higher-tier surveys can use tighter tile or landmark logic.
- Survey steps require the Cartography skill. Higher tiers need higher levels.
- Survey steps should reward players who invest in Cartography.
- Survey steps can be combined with Dig Steps (survey first, then dig).

## Dig Steps

A Dig Step requires the player to use a trowel, spade, or similar tool at a specific tile or landmark.

### Dig Examples

| Dig | Location | Tier |
|-----|----------|------|
| Dig where the oldroad oak casts its shadow at noon | Tile east of Oldroad Oak | Scrap |
| Dig between the two ferry posts at River Stoop | Midpoint of River Stoop Dock | Scrap |
| Dig at the foot of the warden's first boundary stone | Base of Warden Steps Stone | Chalk |
| Dig where the chalk dust gathers at the quarry | Chalkhouse Court Quarry | Chalk |
| Dig at the base of the grave marker with the worn name | Gravegate Worn Marker | Writ |
| Dig where the crow lands but does not eat | Crowmile Road Crow Spot | Writ |
| Dig at the tide line during Wardenbrook low tide | Wardenbrook Tide Line | Grave |
| Dig beneath the fallen bell in Bellwood Deep | Bellwood Fallen Bell Base | Grave |

### Dig Rules

- Dig steps require a spade or trowel item.
- Dig steps can spawn an Ambush Step at higher tiers.
- Dig steps should point to recognizable landmarks, not arbitrary tiles.
- Higher-tier dig steps can be more precise or require survey first.
- Dig steps should not require coordinate precision at low tiers.

## Cartography Integration

Cartography is the skill that ties Sketch, Survey, and Dig steps together.

- Cartography level determines which survey steps a player can complete.
- Higher Cartography levels reveal more detail in sketches.
- Cartography tools (map tube, survey kit) can be required for higher-tier steps.
- Cartography should be a viable non-combat path to Trail completion.
