# Skill and Station Steps

Skill Steps require the player to pass a skill-level action check. Station Steps require the player to use a skilling station to produce a specific item or perform a specific action.

## Station Steps

Station Steps send players to towns, guilds, and skill hubs. They create demand for stations and drive players into the social centers of the world.

### Station Rules

- Station Steps require the player to use a specific station.
- Station Steps may require the player to produce a specific item.
- Station Steps may require the player to perform a specific action (bless, cleanse, fire).
- Station Steps should drive players into towns and guilds.
- Station Steps should create demand for stations that are otherwise underused.
- Station Steps should not require rare or endgame stations at low tiers.

## Skill Steps

Skill Steps require the player to pass a skill-level action check. The check may be a simple level requirement or a more complex challenge.

### Starter Skill Examples

| Step | Skill | Level | Tier |
|------|-------|-------|------|
| Pass a Mining check at the tin vein | Mining | 5 | Scrap |
| Pass a Woodcutting check at the oak tree | Woodcutting | 10 | Scrap |
| Pass a Fishing check at the river | Fishing | 5 | Scrap |
| Pass a Gardening check at the herb patch | Gardening | 10 | Chalk |
| Pass a Trapping check at the simple trap | Trapping | 10 | Chalk |
| Pass a Cartography check at the landmark | Cartography | 15 | Chalk |
| Pass a Sleight check at the simple lock | Sleight | 15 | Chalk |

### Higher-Tier Skill Examples

| Step | Skill | Level | Tier |
|------|-------|-------|------|
| Pass a Mining check at the iron vein | Mining | 30 | Writ |
| Pass a Smithing check at the advanced forge | Smithing | 40 | Writ |
| Pass a Cartography check at the second-ring landmark | Cartography | 50 | Grave |
| Pass a Sleight check at the complex lock | Sleight | 60 | Grave |
| Pass a Favour check at the advanced shrine | Favour | 70 | Crown |
| Pass a Beadwork check at the master kiln | Beadwork | 80 | Crown |
| Pass a Cartography check at the hidden landmark | Cartography | 90 | Starfall |

### Skill Rules

- Skill Steps can ask for specific skill levels.
- Temporary boosts can count unless explicitly banned.
- Skill Steps should not require endgame levels at low tiers.
- Skill Steps should create demand for skills that are otherwise underused.
- Skill Steps should be readable. The player should know what skill and level are required.
- Skill Steps can be combined with Station Steps (use a station and pass a skill check).

## Temporary Boosts

Temporary skill boosts (potions, food, Favour boons) can count for Skill Steps unless the step explicitly bans them.

- Steps that ban boosts must say so clearly.
- Boosts are allowed by default.
- Boosts do not count for quest requirements or permanent unlocks.
- Boosts are a way for low-level players to attempt higher-tier Trails.
