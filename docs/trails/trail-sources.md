# Trail Sources

Trails enter the world through nine source categories. The key rule is that skilling must produce Trails, not just combat.

## Source Philosophy

A player cutting oak, catching tinfin, or surveying a landmark should have a small chance to find a Trail. Combat is a source, not the source. This preserves the optional nature of Trails and rewards diverse play styles.

## Source Table

| Source | How It Works | Typical Tier | Frequency |
|--------|--------------|------------|-----------|
| Monster drops | Any creature can drop a Trail. Higher-level creatures drop higher tiers. | 1-4 | Common |
| Skilling rare rolls | Mining, Woodcutting, Fishing, Gardening have a rare chance to produce a Trail instead of a normal resource. | 1-3 | Uncommon |
| Warden contracts | Completing a contract sometimes rewards a Trail as bonus loot. | 2-4 | Uncommon |
| Cartography surveys | Surveying a landmark can reveal a hidden Trail step or a full Trail. | 2-5 | Rare |
| Quest rewards | Some quests hand a Trail directly to the player. | 2-4 | One-time |
| Fishing oddities | Catching an odd fish, boot, or sealed bottle can contain a Trail. | 1-3 | Uncommon |
| Woodcutting bark marks | Cutting certain trees reveals bark marks that are Trail steps or full Trails. | 1-3 | Uncommon |
| Mining sealed stones | Mining certain rocks reveals sealed stones that contain Trail clues. | 2-4 | Rare |
| Sleight pickpocketing | Pickpocketing certain NPCs can lift a Trail from their pocket. | 2-5 | Rare |

## Monster Drops

Any creature defeated in combat has a chance to drop a Trail. The drop chance and tier depend on the creature's level and the area's ring.

- Starter creatures: Scrap and Chalk Trails.
- First-ring creatures: Chalk, Writ, and Grave Trails.
- Second-ring creatures: Writ, Grave, Crown, and Starfall Trails.
- Bosses: guaranteed Trail drop of appropriate tier.

## Skilling Rare Rolls

Every gathering and production skill has a rare chance to produce a Trail instead of the normal resource.

| Skill | How It Works | Typical Tier |
|-------|--------------|------------|
| Mining | Sealed stone appears instead of ore. Contains a Trail. | 1-3 |
| Woodcutting | Bark mark appears on the stump. Is or contains a Trail. | 1-3 |
| Fishing | Odd catch (boot, bottle, sealed letter) contains a Trail. | 1-3 |
| Gardening | Unusual plant or buried packet contains a Trail. | 1-2 |
| Trapping | Disturbed trap contains a Trail instead of a creature. | 1-3 |
| Beadwork | Mis-fired bead reveals a chalk mark. Is a Trail step or full Trail. | 1-2 |
| Smithing | Off-cast metal reveals a stamp. Is a Trail step. | 2-3 |
| Cooking | Burnt item reveals a hidden scrap. Is a Trail. | 1-2 |

## Warden Contracts

Completing a Warden contract sometimes awards a Trail as bonus loot. The tier matches the contract difficulty.

- Starter contracts: Scrap or Chalk.
- First-ring contracts: Chalk or Writ.
- Second-ring contracts: Writ, Grave, or Crown.
- Named Warrants: Grave or Crown.

## Cartography Surveys

Surveying a landmark with Cartography can reveal a hidden Trail.

- Starter landmarks: Scrap or Chalk.
- First-ring landmarks: Chalk or Writ.
- Second-ring landmarks: Writ, Grave, or Crown.
- Rare landmarks: Crown or Starfall.

## Quest Rewards

Some quests award a Trail directly. This is a one-time reward per quest, not a repeatable source.

## Fishing Oddities

Catching an odd fish, boot, or sealed bottle can contain a Trail. The tier depends on the fishing spot.

- Starter spots: Scrap.
- First-ring spots: Chalk or Writ.
- Second-ring spots: Writ or Grave.

## Woodcutting Bark Marks

Cutting certain trees reveals a bark mark that is a Trail step or a full Trail.

- Oak and below: Scrap.
- Lathwood and above: Chalk or Writ.
- Rare trees: Writ or Grave.

## Mining Sealed Stones

Mining certain rocks reveals a sealed stone that contains a Trail clue.

- Tin and copper: Scrap or Chalk.
- Iron and coal: Chalk or Writ.
- Rare veins: Writ or Grave.

## Sleight Pickpocketing

Pickpocketing certain NPCs can lift a Trail from their pocket.

- Town NPCs: Chalk or Writ.
- Guard NPCs: Writ or Grave.
- Rare targets: Crown or Starfall.

## Source Drop Rates

Drop rates are provisional tuning. The design intent is:

- Scrap: common. A regular player sees several per hour.
- Chalk: uncommon. A few per hour.
- Writ: rare. One per hour or two.
- Grave: very rare. A few per session.
- Crown: extremely rare. One per day or two.
- Starfall: mythic. One per month or two.

These rates scale with activity level and area. A player grinding first-ring content sees more Writs than a player in starter districts.
