# The Beadwife's Errand

Giver: **Mother Tallow**

Primary systems: Beadwork, Magic, Favour crossover

Hook: "Fetch clay from the riverbed. Not the pretty clay. The listening clay."

## Items

- Bead Clay.
- Glass Sand.
- Drilled Bead.
- Chalkmarked Bead.

## Beats

1. Mother Tallow sends the player for listening clay.
2. Player shapes and drills a bead.
3. Player fires the bead at Chalkhouse Court.
4. Player blesses or strings the bead.

## Rewards

- Chalkmarked Wand or Threadbare Bead Pouch.
- Beadwork recipe unlock.
- Basic Magic/Favour hint.
