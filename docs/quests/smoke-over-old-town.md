# Smoke Over Old Town

Giver: **Pippa Hearth**

Support NPCs: Mara Bellkeeper, Osric Penny, Sister Writ

Primary systems: Cooking, Hearthcraft, object use, first danger hint

Tone: comic civic panic

Hook: "There's smoke where smoke has no paid permit to be."

## Premise

Smoke is coming from the old kiln, but everyone argues whether it is cooking smoke, forge smoke, shrine smoke, or drake smoke.

## Beats

1. Talk to Pippa Hearth at the Market kitchen.
2. Collect Bellbread or kindling.
3. Inspect the old kiln.
4. Light or dampen a hearth.
5. Notice an Ash Drake Whelp hint.
6. Return for food and a Shrine Candle.

## Rewards

- Bellbread or Old Town bread.
- Shrine Candle.
- Cooking or Hearthcraft XP.
- Access to old kiln investigation.
