# Rats Under Tally's

Giver: **Tomas Tally**

Support NPC: Warden Holt

Primary systems: combat, Wardenry, drops, Favour

Hook: "The rats have eaten the mortgage drawer. Half the town is temporarily imaginary."

## Beats

1. Tomas Tally reports missing ledgers.
2. Warden Holt issues a rat contract.
3. Player kills Cellar Rats in Sootcellar or Counting House basement.
4. Player returns Rat Tails and notices Small Bones.
5. Sister Writ introduces Shrine Hearth offering.

## Rewards

- Coins.
- Wardenry XP.
- Favour intro.
- Ratcatcher Tail chance.
- Cellar King unlock later.
