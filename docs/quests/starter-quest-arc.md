# Starter Quest Arc

The first arc is a loose ring around the Market Bell.

## Phase 1: Arrival

Quest: [`Smoke Over Old Town`](smoke-over-old-town.md)

Teaches movement, object interaction, Cooking, Hearthcraft, and NPC dialogue.

## Phase 2: First Combat

Quest: [`Rats Under Tally's`](rats-under-tallys.md)

Teaches combat, drops, bones, Wardenry, and Favour.

## Phase 3: First Equipment Identity

Optional intro quests:

- [`A Penny for the Forge`](a-penny-for-the-forge.md)
- [`String Enough to Sing`](string-enough-to-sing.md)
- [`The Beadwife's Errand`](the-beadwifes-errand.md)
- Nell's Good Hide, planned Full Patch quest.

## Phase 4: First Mystery

Quest: [`The Missing Bell-Clapper`](the-missing-bell-clapper.md)

Teaches Sleight, Cartography, NPC cross-town routing, and civic mystery.

## Phase 5: Gravegate Threshold

Quest: [`Gravegate Flowers`](gravegate-flowers.md)

Teaches Favour, Gardening, Gravegate, rot, and cleanse.

## Phase 6: Ledger Threshold

Quests in this phase introduce the Civic Ledger. Quest completion counts as a Chartered-tier ledger task. Some quests reward Oldroad Trails directly.

- **A Penny for the Forge** completion → Foundry Row deed tier credit
- **The Missing Bell-Clapper** completion → Sleight/Cartography ledger entry and possible trail reward
- **Gravegate Flowers** completion → Shrine Hearth deed tier credit
- Any starter quest completion → Errand or Stamped tier credit in the quest's district

See [`../ledger/civic-ledger-system.md`](../ledger/civic-ledger-system.md) for deed tiers and [`../trails/trail-sources.md`](../trails/trail-sources.md) for trail sources.
