# Dialogue Style Guide

Old Town dialogue should have short lines, strong voice, and no modern meme sludge.

## General Rules

1. NPCs speak in grounded trade language.
2. Use dialect lightly, not cartoonishly.
3. Every major NPC has a verbal habit.
4. Dialogue should be skimmable.
5. Avoid paragraphs longer than 2-3 lines.
6. Put jokes in implications, not punchlines.
7. Item names should appear naturally.

## Example Tone

| NPC | Sample |
|-----|--------|
| Osric Penny | "A sword's just a debt with an edge on it. Mine me copper, fetch me tinstone, and I'll show you how debt gets sharpened." |
| Mother Tallow | "Don't rattle the beads. They remember being stone." |
| Tomas Tally | "If the rats have eaten ledger seven, I am legally dead until Tuesday." |
| Sister Writ | "Bones are not rubbish. They are addresses." |
