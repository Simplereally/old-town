# A Penny for the Forge

Giver: **Osric Penny**

Primary systems: Mining, Smithing, Full Penny

Hook: "Bring ore. Not excuses. I can't smelt an excuse."

## Items

- Penny Copper.
- Tinstone.
- Pennywrought Ingot.

## Beats

1. Osric Penny sends the player to the north quarry.
2. Player mines Penny Copper and Tinstone.
3. Player smelts a Pennywrought Ingot.
4. Player forges a starter item at Foundry Row.

## Rewards

- Pennywrought Shortblade or Helm.
- Foundry shop stock unlock.
- Small Smithing XP reward.
