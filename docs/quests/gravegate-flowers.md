# Gravegate Flowers

Giver: **Gravekeeper Soll**

Support NPC: Sister Writ

Primary systems: Gardening, Favour, Gravegate, anti-rot

Hook: "The dead don't mind visitors. They mind poor manners."

## Items

- Grave Flower.
- Bone Chips.
- Grave Dust.

## Beats

1. Gravekeeper Soll asks for the right flowers before the dead wander badly.
2. Player gathers or plants Grave Flowers.
3. Player faces Grave Mites near Gravegate.
4. Player performs Name the Dead at Gravegate Shrine.

## Rewards

- Grave Mercy unlock.
- Gravegate access hint.
- Favour XP.
