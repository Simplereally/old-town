# Utility Skills

> **The five utility skills that govern travel, stealth, contracts, fire, and maps.** These skills do not produce items in the traditional sense. They unlock access, reduce friction, and open parts of the world that are otherwise unreachable.
>
> **Rule:** Every utility skill must connect to at least one production or gathering skill, or to another utility skill. No utility skill is an island.

---

## Wayfaring

Wayfaring governs shortcuts, roads, stamina recovery, climbing, ferries, and hidden routes. A player with high Wayfaring moves through the world faster and reaches places others cannot.

Train Wayfaring by using shortcuts, traversing obstacles such as walls and cliffs, and completing marked road sections between settlements. Each shortcut used grants a small amount of XP, with harder routes rewarding more.

### Key Unlocks

| Level | Unlock | Description |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Basic shortcuts | Low walls, small gaps, and simple rope swings |
| 5 | Run energy | Faster stamina recovery after running |
| 15 | Rooftop access | Climb to building roofs and cross above streets |
| 25 | River crossings | Wading and log-balancing across shallow water |
| 35 | Advanced shortcuts | Longer climbs, tightropes, and chimney routes |
| 45 | Cliff scaling | Vertical climbs up rock faces and cliff sides |
| 50 | Ferries | Unlock ferry routes across rivers and lakes |
| 65 | Underground tunnels | Hidden cave passages and sewer routes |
| 75 | Elite routes | Hidden mountain passes and avalanche tunnels |
| 85 | Rooftop highways | Continuous rooftop paths across entire districts |
| 90 | Swift travel | Permanent +10% movement speed on all known routes |
| 99 | All shortcuts | No obstacle in the world blocks this player |

### Output and Benefit

Wayfaring grants faster travel between locations, improved stamina recovery after running, and access to hidden areas that contain rare resources, shortcuts to dungeons, or secret vendors. No tool is required; the player uses their body alone.

### Skill Verbs

| Verb | Meaning | Old Town Context |
|------|---------|------------------|
| **Shortcut** | Take a hidden route | "Shortcut over the warden's wall" |
| **Climb** | Ascend a vertical surface | "Climb the oldroad oak to the rooftop" |
| **Traverse** | Cross a difficult terrain | "Traverse the river on the fallen log" |
| **Ferry** | Use a boat to cross water | "Ferry across the Wardenbrook" |
| **Sprint** | Run at increased speed | "Sprint the rooftop highway to the market" |

## Sleight

Sleight governs pickpocketing, stall theft, lock picking, trap disarming, forgery, and smuggling. It is the skill of taking what is not yours and getting away with it.

Train Sleight by pickpocketing NPCs, stealing from market stalls, and disarming traps in chests or doorways. Higher-level targets and more complex traps yield more XP but carry greater risk.

### Key Unlocks

| Level | Unlock | Description |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Basic pickpocketing | Steal coins and small items from common folk |
| 5 | Stalls | Steal from market stalls and street vendors |
| 15 | Purses | Steal from wealthier NPCs with better loot |
| 25 | Simple locks | Pick simple locks on doors and chests |
| 35 | Traps | Disarm basic mechanical traps |
| 45 | Advanced traps | Disarm complex mechanical traps |
| 50 | Forgery | Create false documents and counterfeit permits |
| 65 | Smuggling | Move contraband through low-security checkpoints |
| 75 | Master forgery | Create perfect forgeries that pass inspection |
| 85 | High-security smuggling | Move contraband through any checkpoint |
| 90 | Vault locks | Crack the most secure locks in the world |
| 99 | Master smuggling | Move any contraband through any checkpoint undetected |

### Output and Benefit

Sleight produces coins, loot, quest items, and access to restricted areas that are locked or guarded. No tool is required; the player uses their hands and wits alone. The risk of getting caught increases with target level. Guards will chase, fine, or place a bounty on repeat offenders.

Sleight connects to the Civic Ledger through Marn Lock at the Sootcellar. He can "adjust" ledger records, forge permits, and knows blacksealed Oldroad Trails. Forgery unlocks at level 50 allow counterfeit permits that bypass charter requirements for a fee. See [`../ledger/charters-and-permits.md`](../ledger/charters-and-permits.md).

### Skill Verbs

| Verb | Meaning | Old Town Context |
|------|---------|------------------|
| **Lift** | Steal from a person's pocket | "Lift the warden's badge from his belt" |
| **Palm** | Hide an object in hand | "Palm the ring while the merchant looks away" |
| **Jimmy** | Force a lock with a tool | "Jimmy the graveiron chest with a wire" |
| **Forge** | Create a false document | "Forge a permit to enter the contract zone" |
| **Smuggle** | Move contraband past guards | "Smuggle the drake hide through the gate" |

## Wardenry

Wardenry governs bounties, monster contracts, and access to dangerous creatures. A player with Wardenry is licensed to hunt beasts that are off-limits to ordinary folk.

Train Wardenry by completing bounties, killing assigned contract targets, and turning in completed contracts to a Warden post. Each contract specifies a creature, a location, and a proof item to return.

### Key Unlocks

| Level | Unlock | Description |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Basic bounties | Hunt common beasts in safe zones |
| 5 | Small contracts | Track and kill small predators in nearby areas |
| 15 | Medium contracts | Track and kill tougher monsters in wild areas |
| 25 | Group contracts | Hunt beasts that require multiple players |
| 35 | Dangerous creatures | Access zones with aggressive, high-level beasts |
| 45 | Rare creatures | Hunt rare spawns with unique drops |
| 50 | Elite bounties | Hunt named creatures with unique mechanics |
| 65 | Dungeon contracts | Clear dungeon sections of assigned creatures |
| 75 | Boss contracts | Take on single powerful bosses for major rewards |
| 85 | Legendary hunts | Track mythical creatures across multiple zones |
| 90 | World bosses | Participate in world boss events for rare trophies |
| 99 | Master warden | Access to all contract tiers and legendary hunts |

### Output and Benefit

Wardenry yields combat XP, loot, rare drops, and warden points that can be exchanged for gear or contract rerolls. A Warden badge is required to take contracts; this is a quest item earned early in the skill path. Favour protects the player during Wardenry missions, reducing damage from assigned targets and warding off environmental hazards in contract zones.

Starter contract types, proof items, and named bounties are defined in [`../wardenry/wardenry-system.md`](../wardenry/wardenry-system.md) and [`../wardenry/task-tables-starter.md`](../wardenry/task-tables-starter.md).

### Skill Verbs

| Verb | Meaning | Old Town Context |
|------|---------|------------------|
| **Track** | Follow a creature's trail | "Track the wyrm through the marsh" |
| **Bind** | Restrain a creature for capture | "Bind the drake with graveiron chains" |
| **Contract** | Accept a warden assignment | "Contract the boss of the oldroad mine" |
| **Cleanse** | Purify a zone of corruption | "Cleanse the shrine of the wyrm's curse" |
| **Claim** | Mark a bounty as complete | "Claim the trophy at the warden post" |

## Hearthcraft

Hearthcraft governs fires, candles, camps, warmth, light, bead kiln work, and rituals. It is the skill of making safe spaces in a dangerous world.

Train Hearthcraft by lighting fires, building camps in the wilderness, and maintaining shrine candles. Each fire lit or camp established grants XP based on the fuel quality and location difficulty.

### Key Unlocks

| Level | Unlock | Description |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Basic fires | Light simple campfires with kindling |
| 5 | Candles | Craft and light candles for indoor light |
| 15 | Campfires | Build full camps that restore health over time |
| 25 | Cooking fires | Build fires that improve cooking success rates |
| 35 | Bead kiln | Operate a kiln for bead crafting and bead firing |
| 45 | Warmth zones | Create warmth bubbles that prevent cold stamina drain |
| 50 | Shrine lamps | Light and maintain lamps at shrines for Favour bonuses |
| 65 | Smoke signals | Create smoke signals that reveal nearby enemies |
| 75 | Beacon fires | Light large beacons that grant area-wide buffs |
| 85 | Night travel | Permanent light radius in dark zones |
| 90 | Kiln mastery | Fire beads at 2× speed with 0% failure rate |
| 99 | Master hearth | Any fire, lamp, or kiln works at peak efficiency |

### Output and Benefit

Hearthcraft produces cooking stations for food preparation, warmth in cold zones that prevent stamina drain, safe camps that act as respawn points, and light for night travel. A tinderbox or flint is required, and the tool itself requires a minimum Hearthcraft level to use effectively. The skill consumes logs and kindling from Woodcutting. Its outputs feed Cooking through fires, Beadwork through the kiln, and Favour through shrine lamps.

### Skill Verbs

| Verb | Meaning | Old Town Context |
|------|---------|------------------|
| **Kindle** | Start a fire from sparks | "Kindle the campfire with flint and tinder" |
| **Bank** | Build up a fire for cooking | "Bank the fire for the carmine feast" |
| **Smoke** | Preserve food with smoke | "Smoke the deepwater eel over oak chips" |
| **Kiln-fire** | Bake beads in a kiln | "Kiln-fire the blueglass beads" |
| **Lamp-light** | Maintain a shrine lamp | "Lamp-light the warden's shrine at dusk" |

## Cartography

Cartography governs maps, surveys, landmarks, route unlocks, and hidden places. A player with Cartography sees the world in layers that others miss.

Train Cartography by surveying landmarks, completing map sections, and discovering hidden locations. Each new landmark surveyed or map section filled grants XP proportional to the area's obscurity.

### Key Unlocks

| Level | Unlock | Description |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Local map | Reveal the immediate area around the player |
| 5 | Road maps | Mark roads and paths between settlements |
| 15 | Regional map | Expand map coverage to a full region |
| 25 | Landmark survey | Mark landmarks for fast travel points |
| 35 | Dungeon maps | Map interior layouts of dungeons and caves |
| 45 | Secret passages | Reveal hidden doors and shortcuts |
| 50 | Sea charts | Unlock coastal and island navigation |
| 65 | Fog clearing | Reveal obscured areas on the map |
| 75 | Treasure maps | Decode and follow maps to buried caches |
| 85 | Route optimization | Automatic fastest path between any two points |
| 90 | Cartographic memory | Reveal the full map of any visited zone instantly |
| 99 | World map | Full cartographic knowledge of every zone |

### Output and Benefit

Cartography unlocks fast travel points between surveyed locations, route optimization that reduces travel time, Oldroad Trails that lead to rare rewards, and survey posts that act as waypoints for other players. A survey kit is required, and the kit itself requires a minimum Cartography level to use. Cartography unlocks routes on the map; Wayfaring then allows the player to actually traverse those routes through shortcuts and ferries.

Cartography and Wayfaring are central to the Civic Ledger. Finch Quill at Oldroad Gate uses Cartography to sketch trail steps and survey landmarks. Wayfaring route unlocks are ledger rewards for district deed tiers. See [`../ledger/civic-ledger-system.md`](../ledger/civic-ledger-system.md).

### Skill Verbs

| Verb | Meaning | Old Town Context |
|------|---------|------------------|
| **Survey** | Measure and record a landmark | "Survey the warden's tower from the hill" |
| **Sketch** | Draw a map section by hand | "Sketch the oldroad oak grove" |
| **Triangulate** | Determine position from landmarks | "Triangulate your position using the shrine and the tower" |
| **Mark** | Note a point of interest | "Mark the drake's lair on the map" |
| **Chart** | Create a sea or river map | "Chart the Wardenbrook's currents" |

## Design Rules for Utility Skills

1. **No dead-end skill.** Every utility skill must connect to at least one other skill or system. Wayfaring feeds exploration. Sleight feeds wealth. Wardenry feeds combat. Hearthcraft feeds Cooking, Beadwork, and Favour. Cartography feeds Wayfaring.
2. **Content-driven.** Unlock thresholds, route locations, and contract targets are defined in JSON content files, not hardcoded.
3. **Repeated action XP.** Every utility skill is trained by doing the action, not by turning in items or completing one-off quests.
4. **1–99 cap.** No utility skill exceeds level 99. Mastery is the end.
5. **Tool requirements.** Where a tool is required, the tool has a skill level requirement. Where no tool is required, the player uses their body.
6. **Risk where appropriate.** Sleight carries the risk of guards and bounty. Wardenry carries the risk of death in contract zones. Wayfaring, Hearthcraft, and Cartography carry no direct risk but require time and resource investment.
7. **Interlock first.** When adding a new utility skill, define its input and output connections before defining its internal mechanics.
8. **Familiar skeleton, Old Town skin.** The mechanics are recognizable to players of classic skill-based MMOs, but the names and framing are culturally grounded in the Old Town setting.
