# Gathering Skills

> **The five gathering skills of Old Town.** Mining, Woodcutting, Fishing, Trapping, and Gardening form the resource foundation of the world's economy. Every gathered material feeds at least one production skill. No resource is without purpose.

---

## Mining

Mining gathers ores, stone, salt, glass sand, and bead minerals from rock nodes scattered across the world. A miner needs a pickaxe and patience. Higher tier nodes require stronger tools and greater skill.

### Primary Tool

The pickaxe is the miner's essential companion. Each tier demands a minimum Mining level to wield effectively.

| Tier | Cultural Name | Tool ID | Required Mining Level |
|------|---------------|---------|----------------------|
| 0 | Cobbled | `cobbled_pickaxe` | 1 |
| 1 | Pennywrought | `pennywrought_pickaxe` | 1 |
| 2 | Pig Iron | `pig_iron_pickaxe` | 5 |
| 3 | Bellmetal | `bellmetal_pickaxe` | 15 |
| 4 | Blackbar | `blackbar_pickaxe` | 25 |
| 5 | Wardensteel | `wardensteel_pickaxe` | 35 |
| 6 | Greenwold | `greenwold_pickaxe` | 45 |
| 7 | Graveiron | `graveiron_pickaxe` | 55 |
| 8 | Blueglass | `blueglass_pickaxe` | 65 |
| 9 | Carmine Steel | `carmine_steel_pickaxe` | 75 |
| 10 | Argent | `argent_pickaxe` | 85 |
| 11 | Crownsteel | `crownsteel_pickaxe` | 90 |
| 12 | Starfall | `starfall_pickaxe` | 99 |

### Node Types

Rock nodes yield different materials based on their type and the miner's level. Higher level nodes take longer to deplete and grant more valuable ore.

|------|-------------|---------------|-------|
| Copper ore | 1 | Copper ore | The starting node for every miner |
| Tin ore | 1 | Tin ore | Often found near copper deposits |

### Output Destination

Mining feeds two major production skills. Smithing consumes every ore type to smelt them into metal bars for weapons and armour. Beadwork uses glass sand and certain bead minerals to craft spell beads and charmwards.

The five gathering skills form the raw material backbone of Old Town. Every gathered resource flows into at least one production skill.

| Gathering Skill | Primary Output | Production Consumers |

## Design Rules

1. **No dead-end resource.** Every material gathered by these five skills must be consumed by at least one production skill.
2. **Tool tier gates content.** A player cannot gather from a node, tree, or creature above their tool tier.
3. **Level requirements are absolute.** There are no ways to bypass level requirements for gathering nodes.
4. **Temporary boosts apply.** A player may use potions, food, shrine blessings, or capes to boost their gathering level up to +5 above base, allowing access to slightly higher tier content.
5. **Node scarcity creates value.** Higher tier nodes are rarer, respawn slower, and attract more competition.
6. **Multiple outputs per node.** Some nodes yield secondary materials alongside the primary. Coal nodes may yield stone; witchwood trees may yield kindling.
7. **No combat required.** Gathering is peaceful. A player can mine, woodcut, fish, trap, or garden without fighting, though dangerous areas may require combat skill to reach.
