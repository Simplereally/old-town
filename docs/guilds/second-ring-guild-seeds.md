# Second-Ring Guild Seeds

These are light concepts for seven guilds and hubs in the second ring of areas beyond Old Town. None are fully designed. They exist to give area writers, quest designers, and content authors a direction to work toward.

---

## Crownheart Lodge

**guild_id:** `crownheart_lodge`

**Name:** Crownheart Lodge

**Area:** Crownheart Forest

**Category:** Skill Guild

**Purpose:** A woodcutting lodge built around the great Greenwold trees of Crownheart Forest. It offers safer, faster training for high-level woodcutting and bowcraft than any first-ring copse.

**Likely entry requirement:** Woodcutting level 40 and a Crownheart permit from the Oldroad ledger.

**Reward identity:** Greenwold bows and staves, plus rare timber not found closer to town.

**Skill links:** Woodcutting, Bowcraft.

**NPC concept:** Bracken Heartwood, a silent old forester who grades timber by eye and sells Greenwold bow blanks.

**Quest hook:** `The Greenwold Mark` — Bracken asks the player to find a tree with a specific heartwood grain pattern, teaching them to read the forest.

---

## Blackbar Works

**guild_id:** `blackbar_works`

**Name:** Blackbar Works

**Area:** Blackbar Cut

**Category:** Skill Guild

**Purpose:** A dangerous mining face at the deepest end of Blackbar Cut, where the ore is richer and the tunnels less stable. The Works are for miners who have outgrown Tinstone.

**Likely entry requirement:** Mining level 40 and a Blackbar deed from the ledger, or completion of the `Blackbar Collapse` quest.

**Reward identity:** Blackbar ore variants and alloy recipes not available at Foundry Row.

**Skill links:** Mining, Smithing.

**NPC concept:** Garr Blackbar, a one-eyed pit boss who keeps the hoist running and buys ore by the cartload.

**Quest hook:** `The Hoist Chain` — Garr needs a replacement chain link forged from a specific Blackbar alloy, forcing the player to mine, smelt, and smith in one loop.

---

## Underway Chapel

**guild_id:** `underway_chapel`

**Name:** Underway Chapel

**Area:** Grave Underways

**Category:** Shrine Hub

**Purpose:** A hidden shrine deep in the Grave Underways where crypt rites are performed and Gravebound Favour is earned. It is the spiritual anchor of the second ring.

**Likely entry requirement:** Favour level 30 and discovery of the chapel entrance during the `Underway Pilgrim` quest.

**Reward identity:** Gravebound blessings, crypt-cleansing candles, and anti-curse rites.

**Skill links:** Favour, Hearthcraft.

**NPC concept:** Cryptwarden Underway, a hooded keeper who tends the dead and sells grave-candles by the bundle.

**Quest hook:** `The Cryptwarden's Vigil` — The Cryptwarden asks the player to light and guard a full ring of chapel candles while undead press in from the dark.

---

## Witchwood House

**guild_id:** `witchwood_house`

**Name:** Witchwood House

**Area:** Witchwood Verge

**Category:** Underground Hub

**Purpose:** A half-buried house in the Witchwood Verge where magic timber, beadwork, and odd spells converge. It is not a formal guild, but practitioners gather here because the wood remembers magic.

**Likely entry requirement:** Magic level 35 or Beadwork level 35, plus a Witchwood token found in the Verge.

**Reward identity:** Witchwood wands, oddity beads, and spell variants that behave unpredictably.

**Skill links:** Magic, Beadwork, Woodcutting.

**NPC concept:** Hazel Witchwood, a sharp-tongued beadwright who carves wand cores and warns customers that her spells come with no warranty.

**Quest hook:** `Hazel's Unfinished Wand` — Hazel needs a specific Witchwood branch cut at moonrise, and the tree only grows in a cursed grove.

---

## Moth Ferry Lodge

**guild_id:** `moth_ferry_lodge`

**Name:** Moth Ferry Lodge

**Area:** Moth Ferry Crossing

**Category:** Route Hub

**Purpose:** A river lodge at the Moth Ferry Crossing that serves travelers, sells supplies, and brokers ferry contracts for passage up and down the waterway.

**Likely entry requirement:** Wayfaring level 25 or completion of the `Moth Ferry Passage` quest.

**Reward identity:** Ferry contracts, river maps, and waterproof travel gear.

**Skill links:** Wayfaring, Cartography, Hearthcraft.

**NPC concept:** Moth Ferryman, a weathered boatman who knows every eddy and snag and sells passage by the league.

**Quest hook:** `The Ferryman's Ledger` — The Ferryman needs a lost cargo manifest recovered from a wrecked barge downstream.

---

## Carmine Yard

**guild_id:** `carmine_yard`

**Name:** Carmine Yard

**Area:** Carmine Yard

**Category:** Combat Yard

**Purpose:** A fenced dueling ground in Carmine Yard where fighters train fast Arms and Guard combat against live opponents in controlled bouts.

**Likely entry requirement:** Combat level 40 or a Carmine challenge token earned by defeating a first-ring combat yard champion.

**Reward identity:** Dueling titles, combat training buffs, and practice weapons that teach form without risk.

**Skill links:** Arms, Guard, Wardenry.

**NPC concept:** Carmine Duelmaster, a retired champion who judges bouts, sells practice gear, and never fights for free.

**Quest hook:** `The Duelmaster's Test` — The Duelmaster offers a ranked bout series; winning three earns the right to challenge the yard's hidden champion.

---

## Argent Chapel

**guild_id:** `argent_chapel`

**Name:** Argent Chapel

**Area:** Argent Chapel Road

**Category:** Shrine Hub

**Purpose:** A roadside shrine on Argent Chapel Road that specializes in cleansing, support magic, and anti-curse rites for travelers heading into dangerous territory.

**Likely entry requirement:** Favour level 35 or completion of the `Argent Pilgrim` quest.

**Reward identity:** Cleanse rites, support magic beads, and curse-warding charms.

**Skill links:** Favour, Magic, Hearthcraft.

**NPC concept:** Argent Chantress, a traveling singer who settled at the chapel and now leads rites for passing merchants and soldiers.

**Quest hook:** `The Chantress's Lost Verse` — The Chantress needs the second half of an old cleansing hymn recovered from a ruined shrine deeper in the wilds.
