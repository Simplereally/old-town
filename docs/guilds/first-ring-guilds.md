# First-Ring Guilds

The first-ring guilds are the first true unlockable skill hubs beyond Old Town. Each sits inside a first-ring area and offers concentrated training, storage, and services that are not available in the starter town. They do not replace the open world. They reward the effort of reaching them.

## Foundry Hall

**guild_id:** `foundry_hall`

**player-facing name:** Foundry Hall

**shorthand:** Foundry

**area:** Tinstone Cut

**category:** Skill Guild

**skills supported:** Mining, Smithing

**entry requirement:** Mining 20 and Smithing 20, plus completion of the quest `The Shift Bell Rings Twice`.

**key NPC:** Sella Coalhand

**what it offers:** Foundry Hall houses a working furnace, several anvils, an ore deposit crate for short-term storage, a blackcoal shop, and a repair bench for fixing damaged tools. It is the first place where a player can smelt and smith in the same building without walking across town.

**what makes it better than outside:** The furnace is closer to the anvils than any starter layout. The ore deposit crate lets miners drop a load without banking. The blackcoal shop sells fuel in bulk, and the repair bench saves trips to a general store.

**what it does not replace:** High-risk ore nodes still sit outside the safe routes. Foundry Hall does not remove the danger of mining deep in Tinstone Cut. It only makes the safe parts more efficient.

**player shorthand:** "Foundry"

**quest hook:** `The Shift Bell Rings Twice` — the quarry shift bell at Foundry Hall is ringing at the wrong hour. Someone outside the quarry is pulling the rope, and the forgemasters cannot start work until the bell is silenced. The quest introduces the furnace and ends with the guild unlocked.

**mastery hook:** `Coal That Won't Burn` — blackcoal from the deep cut burns cold. Sella asks the player to investigate why the fuel has lost its heat, and whether the seam itself is tainted. This leads to a blackcoal processing recipe.

## Bellwood Yard

**guild_id:** `bellwood_yard`

**player-facing name:** Bellwood Yard

**shorthand:** Bellwood

**area:** Bellwood Copse

**category:** Skill Guild

**skills supported:** Woodcutting, Bowcraft

**entry requirement:** Woodcutting 20 and Bowcraft 20.

**key NPC:** Aunt Bracken

**what it offers:** Bellwood Yard provides a bow bench for bowcraft, a woodpile storage for logs, a string seller, and a feather trade post. It is a small clearing with covered workstations, built around a central fire pit.

**what makes it better than outside:** The bow bench is faster than a pocket knife. The woodpile storage accepts any log type and holds more than a starter bank slot. The string seller and feather trade mean players do not need to hunt birds or spin flax before crafting.

**what it does not replace:** Rare wild trees still spawn deeper in Bellwood Copse. The yard only covers common species. Players who want yew or better must still venture out.

**player shorthand:** "Bellwood"

**quest hook:** `Staves in the Wrong Stack` — bow staves are being sorted like firewood by a new yardhand. Aunt Bracken is furious because the grain alignment is being ruined, and she needs someone who understands wood to set things right. The quest teaches bowcraft basics and opens the guild.

**mastery hook:** `The Bracken Mark` — later, Aunt Bracken recruits the player to mark specific trees for the seasonal harvest. The marks must be hidden from casual observers but clear to the yard crews. This unlocks a hidden grove with faster respawns.

## Patchfield Tannery

**guild_id:** `patchfield_tannery`

**player-facing name:** Patchfield Tannery

**shorthand:** Patch

**area:** Patchfield

**category:** Trade Yard

**skills supported:** Trapping, Tailoring

**entry requirement:** Trapping 20 or Tailoring 20.

**key NPC:** Rowen Hidepeg

**what it offers:** Patchfield Tannery runs a tanning frame for hides, a dye vat for colour work, a hide order board for bulk requests, and a stitch table for advanced tailoring. The yard smells of lime and oak bark, and the workers are quiet.

**what makes it better than outside:** The tanning frame processes hides faster than the starter method. The dye vat offers colours not sold in Old Town. The hide order board lets players trade bulk hides for pre-cut leather, saving time.

**what it does not replace:** Hunting rare creatures for unique hides still happens in the wild. The tannery only processes common hides. Rare pelts must still be taken to specialists.

**player shorthand:** "Patch"

**quest hook:** `Hide and Seek` — Rowen Hidepeg needs a sample hide from a creature that has been avoiding the traps. The quest introduces tracking and ends with guild access.

**mastery hook:** `The Dye Master's Secret` — later, Rowen asks the player to find a lost shipment of rare dye ingredients. This unlocks a new colour palette for the vat.

## Wardenbrook Fishery

**guild_id:** `wardenbrook_fishery`

**player-facing name:** Wardenbrook Fishery

**shorthand:** Brook

**area:** Wardenbrook

**category:** Route Hub

**skills supported:** Fishing, Cooking

**entry requirement:** Fishing 20 and route unlock to Wardenbrook.

**key NPC:** Pell Hookline

**what it offers:** Wardenbrook Fishery maintains several fish spots along the bank, a cleaning table for preparing catches, a bait shop for supplies, and fish hamper support for bulk storage. The building is a long wooden shed on stilts, with nets hanging from the rafters. Pell also runs the River Stoop in Old Town; anglers who trained with him in town will recognize his voice before they see his face.

**what makes it better than outside:** The fish spots are closer together than any riverbank in Old Town. The cleaning table is faster than a dockside board. The bait shop stocks lures that are not sold in starter shops. Fish hamper support lets anglers store catches without leaving the water.

**what it does not replace:** Dangerous deep-water fish that appear later in the game are not found here. The fishery only covers safe, shallow species. Deep-sea or hazardous fishing still requires travel.

**player shorthand:** "Brook"

**quest hook:** `Hookline's First Catch` — Pell Hookline challenges the player to land a specific fish using only basic bait. The quest teaches spot selection and unlocks the fishery.

**mastery hook:** `The Net Menders` — later, Pell asks the player to help repair a damaged net in a risky part of the brook. This unlocks a hidden spot with better catches.

## Lowgrave Chapel

**guild_id:** `lowgrave_chapel`

**player-facing name:** Lowgrave Chapel

**shorthand:** Lowgrave

**area:** Lowgrave

**category:** Shrine Hub

**skills supported:** Favour, Gardening, Apothecary

**entry requirement:** Completion of the quest `Gravegate Flowers` and Favour 15.

**key NPC:** Cress Lowgrave

**what it offers:** Lowgrave Chapel provides a shrine for favour gain, grave flower patches for gardening, a cleanse service for removing minor curses, and a candle seller for ritual supplies. The chapel is small, cold, and always dim.

**what makes it better than outside:** The shrine gives favour at a slightly faster rate than roadside shrines. The grave flower patches grow herbs used in apothecary work. The cleanse service is cheaper than travelling to a major temple. The candle seller stocks ritual items in bulk.

**what it does not replace:** Full Argent or Grave progression chains are not available here. The chapel is a stepping stone, not an endpoint. Players seeking high-level favour rewards must still travel to larger shrine networks.

**player shorthand:** "Lowgrave"

**quest hook:** `Gravegate Flowers` — Cress Lowgrave asks the player to plant and tend a specific flower on a forgotten grave. The quest introduces gardening and shrine mechanics, and unlocks the chapel.

**mastery hook:** `The Unnamed Dead` — later, Cress asks the player to identify a set of unmarked graves using old records. This unlocks a hidden patch with rare herbs.

## Old Kiln House

**guild_id:** `old_kiln_house`

**player-facing name:** Old Kiln House

**shorthand:** Kiln

**area:** The Old Kiln

**category:** Skill Guild

**skills supported:** Hearthcraft, Beadwork, Magic

**entry requirement:** Completion of the quest `Smoke Over Old Town` and Hearthcraft 20 or Beadwork 20.

**key NPC:** Noll Kilnwatch

**what it offers:** Old Kiln House contains a bead kiln for firing glass and clay beads, an ash sorting station for refining materials, heat-safe firing tables, a tallow and ember bead workshop, and a small repair bench for fixing damaged tools. The house is built into the side of a dormant kiln, and the walls are always warm.

**what makes it better than outside:** The bead kiln fires faster than a campfire. The ash sorting station recovers more usable material from burnt resources. The heat-safe tables let players work with hot materials without injury. The tallow and ember bead workshop supports early magic crafting.

**what it does not replace:** Chalkhouse starter magic training in Old Town remains the entry point for spellcasting. Old Kiln House extends that foundation but does not replace it. Players still start magic in town.

**player shorthand:** "Kiln"

**quest hook:** `Smoke Over Old Town` — Noll Kilnwatch notices smoke rising from the old kiln and asks the player to investigate. The quest introduces hearthcraft and beadwork, and ends with guild access.

**mastery hook:** `The Kilnwatch Oath` — later, Noll asks the player to help rekindle a sealed chamber in the kiln's base. This unlocks a hidden firing table with reduced failure chance.

## Sootstairs Rooms

**guild_id:** `sootstairs_rooms`

**player-facing name:** Sootstairs Rooms

**shorthand:** Soot

**area:** Sootstairs

**category:** Underground Hub

**skills supported:** Sleight, low-level combat

**entry requirement:** Completion of the quest `Keys That Open Nothing` or Sleight 20.

**key NPC:** Vey Falsewick

**what it offers:** Sootstairs Rooms hides behind a false wall in the lower stairwell. Inside, there is a lockpick practice board, a false pouch worktable, a cutpurse training dummy, a small contract board for local jobs, and a map of risky routes through the undercity. The air is thick with coal dust and whispered deals.

**what makes it better than outside:** The lockpick practice board trains Sleight without breaking real locks. The false pouch worktable lets players craft hidden compartments. The cutpurse training dummy teaches pickpocketing safely. The risky route map reveals shortcuts that bypass guarded checkpoints.

**what it does not replace:** Lawful Warden access and legal routes still exist. Sootstairs Rooms offers an alternative, not a replacement. Players who want to stay on the right side of the law can ignore this hub entirely.

**player shorthand:** "Soot"

**quest hook:** `Keys That Open Nothing` — Marn Lock sells keys that only work on doors that are not there. The player must figure out whether this is a scam, a code, or something stranger. The quest introduces Sleight and unlocks the rooms.

**mastery hook:** `Falsewick's Ledger` — later, Vey Falsewick asks the player to plant a forged ledger in a guardhouse. This unlocks a permanent safe route through Sootstairs.

## Crowmile Road Camp

**guild_id:** `crowmile_road_camp`

**player-facing name:** Crowmile Road Camp

**shorthand:** Crowmile

**area:** Crowmile Road

**category:** Route Hub

**skills supported:** Wayfaring, Cartography, Wardenry

**entry requirement:** Wayfaring 20 or a mapped route to Crowmile Road.

**key NPC:** Orven Roadcap

**what it offers:** Crowmile Road Camp is a travelling post with a campfire for resting, a route board for sharing path information, a survey post for updating maps, a road task notice board for local jobs, and a hidden shortcut to the old road fork that skips a guarded bend. The camp moves slightly each season, but the signpost always stays in the same clearing.

**what makes it better than outside:** The campfire restores travel fatigue faster than resting on bare ground. The route board reveals hidden paths that are not on starter maps. The survey post lets players sell map data for coin. The road task board offers repeatable jobs that train Wayfaring and Wardenry.

**what it does not replace:** Town services such as banks, shops, and full guild halls are not available here. The camp is a roadside stop, not a settlement. Players still need towns for heavy resupply.

**player shorthand:** "Crowmile"

**quest hook:** `Roadcap's Marker` — Orven Roadcap asks the player to place a survey marker at a dangerous crossroads. The quest introduces Wayfaring and unlocks the camp.

**mastery hook:** `The Lost Mile` — later, Orven asks the player to verify a rumoured shortcut that does not appear on any map. This unlocks a permanent fast route between two first-ring areas.
