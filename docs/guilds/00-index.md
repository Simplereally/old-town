# Guild and Skill Hub System

Old Town's guilds and skill hubs are places where players train skills, access specialized tools, and buy or sell related goods. They are not factions. There is no reputation system, no daily quests, and no mandatory membership. A guild is simply a convenience: a concentrated cluster of resources, NPCs, and training spots for a specific skill or craft.

## Design rules

1. **Guilds are not factions.** Joining the Mining Guild does not make you an enemy of the Woodcutting Guild. There are no allegiance conflicts.
2. **No reputation grind.** Access is gated by levels, items, or quest state, not by a hidden reputation meter.
3. **No dailies.** Guilds do not hand out daily tasks or daily reward chests. Training is continuous and player-driven.
4. **Convenience, not mandatory.** Every resource inside a guild can be found somewhere else in the world. Guilds cluster resources to save time, not to lock content.
5. **Skill-centric, not combat-centric.** Guilds focus on skilling. Combat training grounds exist, but they are not guilds.
6. **NPCs are tutors and merchants, not quest givers.** Guild NPCs teach, sell, and demonstrate. Full quest scripts and dialogue trees live in quest documents. Guild documents may mention the quest hook that unlocks the guild as context for what the guild offers.
