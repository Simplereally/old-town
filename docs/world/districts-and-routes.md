# Districts and Routes

Old Town uses a compact hub-and-spoke layout. The Market Bell is the central shorthand. Routes should be easy to say and easy to remember.

## Districts

| District | Role | Player Shorthand |
|----------|------|------------------|
| The Market Bell | Central spawn, general trading, noticeboard | bell |
| Counting House | Bank, item reclaim, coin exchange | bank |
| Foundry Row | Smithing, mining turn-ins, anvils, furnace | foundry |
| Lath Yard | Bowcraft, woodcutting, arrows, strings | lath |
| Patch Lane | Tailoring, hides, tanning, ranged armour | patch |
| Chalkhouse Court | Beadwork, basic magic, bead pouches | chalk |
| Warden Steps | Contracts, guards, early combat tasks | warden |
| Shrine Hearth | Favour, offerings, candles, cleanse | shrine |
| River Stoop | Fishing, cooking, ferries, tinfin stalls | river |
| Oldroad Gate | Woodcutting, cartography, wayfaring routes | oldroad |
| Gravegate | Crypts, Grave beads, bone drops, danger | grave |
| Sootcellar | Rats, Sleight, black market hints | cellar |

## Starter Routes

| Route | Connects | Teaches |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Bell to Bank | Market Bell to Counting House | Safety, storage, reclaim |
| Bell to Foundry | Market Bell to Foundry Row | Mining and Smithing |
| Bell to Lath | Market Bell to Lath Yard | Woodcutting and Bowcraft |
| Bell to Patch | Market Bell to Patch Lane | Trapping and Tailoring |
| Bell to Chalk | Market Bell to Chalkhouse Court | Beadwork and Magic |
| Bell to River | Market Bell to River Stoop | Fishing and Cooking |
| Bell to Warden | Market Bell to Warden Steps | Contracts and combat |
| Bell to Cellar | Market Bell to Sootcellar | First rats and Sleight |
| Warden to Grave | Warden Steps to Gravegate | Danger escalation |
| Oldroad to Quarry | Oldroad Gate to north quarry | Early Mining route |
