# Starter Economy Loops

The first 30 minutes should be playable on paper. These loops define what the player can do immediately in and around Old Town.

## Loop 1: Full Penny

1. Spawn at Market Bell.
2. Talk to Osric Penny at Foundry Row.
3. Mine Penny Copper and Tinstone at the north quarry.
4. Smelt Pennywrought Ingot at the Bellows Furnace.
5. Forge Pennywrought Shortblade, Helm, or Ward at the Anvil.
6. Fight cellar rats or road goblins.

## Loop 2: Full Lath

1. Talk to Letha Lath at Lath Yard.
2. Cut Oldroad Oak or Lathwood near Oldroad Gate.
3. Whittle shafts and staves.
4. Trap or buy Bellfeathers and Sinew Cord.
5. Make Lathwood Shortbow and Lathwood Arrows.
6. Kill rats or goblins from range.

## Loop 3: Full Patch

1. Talk to Nell Patch at Patch Lane.
2. Trap rabbits or foxes south of town.
3. Cure hides at the Tanning Frame.
4. Stitch Patchhide Coif, Jerkin, or Chaps.
5. Pair with a Lathwood bow.

## Loop 4: Full Chalk

1. Talk to Mother Tallow at Chalkhouse Court.
2. Gather Bead Clay or Glass Sand.
3. Use Bead Drill and Bead Kiln.
4. Buy or produce basic beads.
5. Equip Chalkmarked Wand and Primer.
6. Cast Ember Flick on rats or goblins.

## Loop 5: Food and Survival

1. Fish Ditch Shrimp or Tinfin at River Stoop.
2. Cook on Market Hearth or Kitchen Range.
3. Make Bellbread or Tinfin Pie.
4. Use food for contracts.

## Loop 6: Warden Contract

1. Accept rat contract from Warden Holt.
2. Kill 8 Cellar Rats.
3. Bring Ratcatcher Tail.
4. Receive coins, Favour, or a low-tier keepsake.
5. Unlock the next contract.
