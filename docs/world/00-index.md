## Core Decision

The starter area is **Old Town**. Do not invent a separate tutorial village yet.

Player language target:

- "Meet at the Bell."
- "Bank at the Counting House."
- "Go north quarry."
- "Patch Lane for hides."
- "Chalkhouse for beads."
- "Warden board has rat tasks."
- "Gravegate is scary at low level."
