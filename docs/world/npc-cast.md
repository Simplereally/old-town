# NPC Cast

Every starter NPC owns a system. Do not add decorative-only NPCs to the first vertical slice.

## Starter NPCs

| NPC | Location | Role | Ledger Role |
|-----|----------|------|-------------|
| Mara Bellkeeper | Market Bell | Welcome, town rumours, basic orientation | — |
| Osric Penny | Foundry Row | Smith, starter hammers and pickaxes, Full Penny path | — |
| Letha Lath | Lath Yard | Bowyer, strings, shafts, Full Lath path | — |
| Nell Patch | Patch Lane | Tanner and tailor, Full Patch path | — |
| Mother Tallow | Chalkhouse Court | Beadwife, Full Chalk path, bead pouches | — |
| Warden Holt | Warden Steps | Starter Wardenry contracts | Stamps combat deeds and contracts |
| Sister Writ | Shrine Hearth | Favour, blessings, shrine candles | Records shrine rites, blesses ledger |
| Finch Quill | Oldroad Gate | Cartographer, maps, route unlocks | Sketches trails, surveys landmarks |
| Edda Tinfin | River Stoop | Fishmonger and cook, food loop | — |
| Bramble Hook | River Stoop | Fishing tools, ferry hooks later | — |
| Marn Lock | Sootcellar | Sleight intro, lockpicks, blacksealed hints | Can "adjust" records, blacksealed trails |
| Pippa Hearth | Market kitchen | Cooking, Hearthcraft, stews and tea | — |
| Gravekeeper Soll | Gravegate | Crypt warnings, bone and grave economy | — |
| Tomas Tally | Counting House | Bank, storage, reclaim, audit flavour | Keeps the formal Civic Ledger |

## Naming Rule

Use named NPCs in docs and content. Avoid generic labels such as blacksmith, banker, or quest giver except as descriptors after the name.
