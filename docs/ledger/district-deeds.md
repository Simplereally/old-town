# District Deeds

Every district has a deed card. Each card holds 5-7 tasks across four tiers. Complete a tier, visit Tomas Tally, and claim the reward.

## Market Bell Deed Card

| Tier | Task | Reward |
|------|------|--------|
| Errand | Ring the Market Bell | Bell Token (5) |
| Errand | Buy from 3 different stalls | Market tip: restock timer |
| Errand | Talk to Mara Bellkeeper | Local gossip unlock |
| Stamped | Deliver a message to every district | Market Bell Stamp |
| Stamped | Spend 100 coins in one visit | 5% discount at all market stalls |
| Chartered | Complete a public work 10 times | Market Rush priority queue |
| Mastered | Own every starter shop item once | Bellkeeper's Sash (cosmetic) |

## Reward Summary by Tier

| Tier | Reward Identity | Example |
|------|-----------------|---------|
| Errand | Small convenience | Tokens, tips, free minor service |
| Stamped | Shop discount or minor service | Stamp, stall discount, free repair |
| Chartered | Route, station, or contract unlock | Nook blueprint, ferry pass, member discount |
| Mastered | Cosmetic, cape trim, or rare utility | Named cosmetic override, district title |

All prices and token counts are provisional tuning.
