# Rewards and Stamps

The Civic Ledger rewards world authority, not power creep. A Mastered player does not deal more damage. They move faster, pay less, and know secrets.

## Reward Philosophy

1. **No universal BiS gear.** Ledger rewards do not include weapons or armour that outclass crafted gear. A Mastered cape is cosmetic. A Chartered route is access.
2. **World authority over stats.** Rewards should change how the player interacts with the world. Discounts, routes, stations, and titles matter more than +1 attack.
3. **Cosmetics are earned.** A cape trim or a cosmetic override from the ledger tells other players where you have been. It is a badge, not a buff.
4. **Convenience is valuable.** A player who never pays ferry tolls or who always has a private station is richer in time, not just coins.

## Reward Types

| Type | Example | Tier |
|------|---------|------|
| District stamp | Foundry Stamp, Gravegate Stamp | Stamped |
| Utility item | Private tinderbox, survey kit restock, lockpick bundle | Errand, Stamped |
| Service discount | 5% at all market stalls, free repairs at Penny and Sons | Stamped, Chartered |
| Shop stock unlock | Access to higher-tier items in a district shop | Chartered |
| Route unlock | Hidden shortcut, ferry route, rooftop path | Chartered |
| Nook unlock | Blueprint for a Crate Nook, Wall Nook, or Grave Nook | Chartered |
| Charter unlock | Foundry Charter, Warden Charter, Shrine Charter | Mastered |
| Cosmetic | Cape trim, tool override, district title | Mastered |
| Trail cache | Box of coins, materials, or a trail scroll from a completed trail | Any trail tier |
| Rare trophy | Unique item that sits in the trophy slot, no stats | Mastered, Starfall Trail |

## Stamp Use

A stamp is a physical item in the player's inventory. It is consumed when claiming the next tier in the same district. Stamps can be displayed in a player's home or nook as decoration, but they have no gameplay effect after consumption.

| Stamp Action | Result |
|--------------|--------|
| Turn in to Tomas Tally | Unlocks next deed tier rewards |
| Display in nook | Cosmetic decoration |
| Trade to another player | No, stamps are bound to the earner |
| Sell to shop | No, stamps have no coin value |
