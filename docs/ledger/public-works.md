# Public Works

Public works are repeatable town jobs. They are minigame-like but non-FOMO. Any player can do a public work at any time. Rewards are modest and aimed at low-level players who need coins, XP, or a sense of place.

## Starter Works

| Work | Location | Gameplay | Reward |
|------|----------|----------|--------|
| Bell Run | Market Bell | Carry messages to 3 districts within a time limit. No combat. | XP (Wayfaring), Bell Tokens (3-5) |
| Soot Sweep | Sootcellar | Clear rat nests and debris from the cellar tunnels. Light combat. | XP (Combat), Sootcellar Tokens (2-4) |
| Kiln Watch | Chalkhouse Court | Tend Mother Tallow's kiln for a fixed number of firing batches. | XP (Hearthcraft), Chalk Tokens (3-5) |
| Ledger Sort | Counting House | Sort Tomas Tally's incoming records into the correct district piles. | XP (none, coins only), Coins (10-20) |
| Graveflower Round | Gravegate | Deliver flowers to 5 grave markers and return to Gravekeeper Soll. | XP (Favour), Gravegate Tokens (3-5) |
| Ferry Rope Duty | River Stoop | Help the ferryman pull the rope for 10 crossings. | XP (Might), River Tokens (2-4), free ferry credit |
| Warden Posting | Warden Steps | Post new contract notices on the Warden Board in the correct order. | XP (Wardenry), Warden Tokens (3-5) |
| Market Rush | Market Bell | Buy a specific list of items from stalls before they sell out. | XP (Sleight), Bell Tokens (3-5), shop discount voucher |

## Work Rules

1. **Repeatable.** A player can do the same work multiple times in a row. There is no cooldown.
2. **No daily lockouts.** The ledger does not use daily limits. A player who wants to sweep soot for six hours can sweep soot for six hours.
3. **No FOMO.** Works do not expire, rotate, or disappear. They are always available.
4. **Modest rewards.** Public works do not give rare drops or BiS gear. They give XP, tokens, coins, and small conveniences.
5. **Good for low-level players.** A player with no combat gear and no skills can still run bells or sort ledgers.
6. **Scale with effort.** Some works reward more for faster completion or perfect accuracy, but the base reward is always given.

## Token Use

Tokens earned from public works are district-specific currency. They are not coins. They can be spent at the district's shops for small items, services, or ledger progress boosts.

| Token | Spent At | Buys |
|-------|----------|------|
| Bell Token | Market Bell stalls | Food, basic tools, map copies |
| Sootcellar Token | Sootcellar hidden vendor | Lockpicks, dark hints, Sleight items |
| Chalk Token | Chalkhouse Court | Bead materials, kiln time, pouch repairs |
| Gravegate Token | Gravegate shrine | Candles, offerings, cleanse services |
| River Token | River Stoop | Fish, cooking assist, ferry rides |
| Warden Token | Warden Steps | Contract rerolls, badge repairs, danger gear |

All token values and XP amounts are provisional tuning.
