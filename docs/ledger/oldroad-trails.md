# Oldroad Trails

Oldroad Trails are the ledger's clue-scroll equivalent. A trail is a sequence of steps that sends a player across Old Town to solve riddles, read maps, perform tasks, and dig for caches. Trails come from play, not from shops.

## Trail Tiers

| Tier | Name | Steps | Identity | Typical Reward |
|------|------|-------|----------|----------------|
| 1 | Scrap Trail | 1-2 | Found in gutters, bins, and rat nests. Low value, low risk. | Coins, basic materials, token stack |
| 2 | Chalk Trail | 2-3 | Left by beadworkers and chalk scribes. Moderate value. | Bead materials, small lamp, local tip |
| 3 | Writ Trail | 3-4 | Tied to shrine rites and formal records. Moderate risk. | Favour item, offering, shrine candle |
| 4 | Grave Trail | 3-5 | Buried with the dead or scratched on crypt stones. Dangerous. | Grave beads, bone trophies, crypt key |
| 5 | Crown Trail | 4-6 | Rare trails left by wealthy travelers or hidden guilds. High value. | Charter scroll, permit fragment, rare cosmetic |
| 6 | Starfall Trail | 5-7 | Mythic trails. Some say Finch Quill has never solved one. | Unique trophy, master cosmetic, district title |

## Trail Sources

A trail can enter the world through any of these sources. The key rule is that skilling must produce trails, not just combat.

| Source | How It Works | Typical Tier |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| Monster drops | Any creature can drop a trail. Higher-level creatures drop higher tiers. | 1-4 |
| Skilling rare rolls | Mining, Woodcutting, Fishing, and Gardening have a rare chance to produce a trail instead of a normal resource. | 1-3 |
| Warden contracts | Completing a contract sometimes rewards a trail as bonus loot. | 2-4 |
| Cartography surveys | Surveying a landmark can reveal a hidden trail step or a full trail. | 2-5 |
| Quest rewards | Some quests hand a trail directly to the player. | 2-4 |
| Fishing oddities | Catching an odd fish, boot, or sealed bottle can contain a trail. | 1-3 |
| Woodcutting bark marks | Cutting certain trees reveals bark marks that are trail steps. | 1-3 |
| Mining sealed stones | Mining certain rocks reveals sealed stones that contain trail clues. | 2-4 |
| Sleight pickpocketing | Pickpocketing certain NPCs can lift a trail from their pocket. | 2-5 |

## Key Rules

1. **Skilling produces trails.** A player cutting oak or catching tinfin should have a small chance to find a trail. Combat is not the only source.
2. **Trails are single-use.** Once completed, the trail item is consumed. The reward is given at the final step.
3. **Trails can be traded.** A player who finds a trail they cannot complete can sell it. A player who buys a trail they cannot complete has wasted their coins.
4. **No shop sales.** Tomas Tally does not sell trails. Marn Lock might trade for them, but never at a fixed price.
5. **Steps use existing systems.** Every step type references a skill, station, NPC, or district that already exists. No trail invents new content.
6. **Starfall trails are rare.** A Starfall Trail should feel like a world event. A player might see one per month of regular play.
