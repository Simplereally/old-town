# Clue Step Types

Every Oldroad Trail is built from steps. Each step type uses an existing world system. No step invents new content.

## Riddle

A riddle points to a location, NPC, or object. The player must read the text and figure out where to go.

| Example | Answer |
|---------|--------|
| "I ring without hands, I stand where all roads meet." | Ring the Market Bell |
| "The penny smith's breath is fire, but his heart is coal." | Speak to Osric Penny at the furnace |
| "Where the dead sleep, the living leave flowers." | Visit Gravekeeper Soll at Gravegate |

## Map Sketch

Finch Quill draws a partial map. The player must match the sketch to a real location and go there.

| Example | Location |
|---------|----------|
| A sketch of a tree with three branches over a wall | Oldroad Oak at Oldroad Gate |
| A sketch of a bell tower with a missing clapper | Market Bell, viewed from the east |
| A sketch of a ferry rope and two posts | River Stoop ferry dock |

## Landmark Rubbing

The player carries a rubbing kit. They must find the correct landmark and create a rubbing to prove they were there.

| Example | Landmark |
|---------|----------|
| "Rub the stone that marks the warden's first post." | Warden Steps boundary stone |
| "Rub the oldest grave marker in the crypt." | First crypt stone at Gravegate |
| "Rub the forge sign that lost its 'S'." | Penny and Sons Forge sign |

## Bell Phrase

The player must ring a specific bell or chime and then speak a phrase to a nearby NPC.

| Example | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Ring the Market Bell, then tell Mara Bellkeeper "The oldroad is open." | Mara responds with the next clue |
| Ring the Shrine Hearth bell, then tell Sister Writ "The dead remember." | Sister Writ blesses the trail |
| Ring the Warden Steps alarm bell, then tell Warden Holt "The rats are counted." | Holt stamps the step |

## Station Task

The player must use a skilling station to produce a specific item or perform a specific action.

| Example | Station |
|---------|---------|
| "Smelt a Pennywrought Ingot at the public furnace." | Foundry Row Bellows Furnace |
| "Fire a Clay Bead at Mother Tallow's kiln." | Chalkhouse Court Bead Kiln |
| "String a shortbow at Letha Lath's bench." | Lath Yard Bow Bench |

## Dress Step

The player must wear specific items and stand in a specific location.

| Example | Required Items |
|---------|----------------|
| "Wear a Leather Apron and stand on the Foundry Row anvil." | Leather Apron |
| "Wear a Beadwife's Shawl and stand before the Chalkhouse kiln." | Beadwife's Shawl |
| "Wear a Warden Badge and salute at the Warden Steps post." | Warden Badge |

## Gesture Step

The player must perform an emote or gesture in a specific location.

| Example | Gesture |
|---------|---------|
| "Bow at Gravekeeper Soll's feet." | Bow emote |
| "Wave at the River Stoop ferryman from the dock." | Wave emote |
| "Cheer at the Market Bell after a successful trade." | Cheer emote |

## Dig Step

The player receives a map or riddle that points to a specific tile. They must dig with a spade.

| Example | Location |
|---------|----------|
| "Dig where the oldroad oak casts its shadow at noon." | Tile east of Oldroad Oak |
| "Dig between the two ferry posts at River Stoop." | Midpoint of River Stoop dock |
| "Dig at the foot of the warden's first boundary stone." | Base of Warden Steps stone |

## Route Step

The player must traverse a specific route or shortcut using Wayfaring.

| Example | Route |
|---------|-------|
| "Shortcut over the warden's wall from Warden Steps to Market Bell." | Warden wall shortcut |
| "Ferry across the Wardenbrook and back." | River Stoop ferry route |
| "Climb the oldroad oak and traverse the rooftop to the gate." | Oldroad Gate rooftop route |

## Shrine Step

The player must perform a rite or offering at a specific shrine.

| Example | Shrine |
|---------|--------|
| "Leave a Grave Bead at the Gravegate shrine." | Gravegate shrine |
| "Light a candle and cleanse rot at the Shrine Hearth." | Shrine Hearth |
| "Perform a prepared rite at the Warden Steps shrine." | Warden Steps shrine |

## Sleight Step

The player must use Sleight to steal, pickpocket, or forge something.

| Example | Sleight Action |
|---------|----------------|
| "Lift the ferryman's toll pouch without being seen." | Pickpocket ferryman |
| "Palm the bead from Mother Tallow's display table." | Steal from Chalkhouse stall |
| "Forge a copy of the warden's contract stamp." | Forge document |

## Ambush Step

The player is attacked by a creature or NPC when they reach the step location. They must survive to proceed.

| Example | Ambush |
|---------|--------|
| "A crypt rat bursts from the grave when you dig." | Crypt rat at Gravegate |
| "A Sootcellar Drudge catches you picking the lock." | Drudge at Sootcellar |
| "A Warden guard challenges you at the wall shortcut." | Warden guard at shortcut |

## Step Type Summary

| Step Type | Skill or System | Risk |
|-----------|-----------------|------|
| Riddle | Reading, world knowledge | None |
| Map Sketch | Cartography | None |
| Landmark Rubbing | Cartography | None |
| Bell Phrase | NPC dialogue | None |
| Station Task | Production skill | None |
| Dress Step | Item ownership | None |
| Gesture Step | Emote system | None |
| Dig Step | Spade item | Low (ambush chance) |
| Route Step | Wayfaring | Low (fall damage) |
| Shrine Step | Favour | None |
| Sleight Step | Sleight | High (guards, bounty) |
| Ambush Step | Combat | High (death possible) |
