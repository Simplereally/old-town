# Charters and Permits

Charters are guild-style access documents. A charter unlocks stations, resources, and social standing in a district. Permits are smaller. A permit unlocks a door, a route, or a single service.

## Charters

| Charter | District | Unlocks | How to Earn |
|---------|----------|---------|-------------|
| Foundry Charter | Foundry Row | Private anvil, higher-tier furnace, member discount | Mastered Foundry Row deed |
| Lath Charter | Lath Yard | Private bow bench, arrow batch assist, member discount | Mastered Lath Yard deed |
| Patch Charter | Patch Lane | Private tanning frame, dye vat priority, member discount | Mastered Patch Lane deed |
| Chalk Charter | Chalkhouse Court | Private bead kiln, blessing priority, member discount | Mastered Chalkhouse Court deed |
| River Charter | River Stoop | Private kitchen range, ferry priority, member discount | Mastered River Stoop deed |
| Warden Charter | Warden Steps | Elite contract access, danger pay, member discount | Mastered Warden Steps deed |
| Quill Charter | Oldroad Gate | Private map table, survey kit restock, member discount | Mastered Oldroad Gate deed |
| Shrine Charter | Shrine Hearth | Private shrine lamp, rite priority, member discount | Mastered Gravegate deed and Favour rite |

## Charter Benefits

| Benefit | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| Private station | A station that only charter members can use, reducing queue time |
| Member discount | 10% discount at all shops and services in the district |
| Priority access | First in line for public stations during busy periods |
| Restock alert | Notification when shop stock refreshes |
| Danger pay | Bonus coins on Warden contracts |

## Starter Permits

| Permit | Unlocks | How to Earn |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| Gravegate Permit | Enter the deep crypt beyond the starter gate | Stamped Gravegate deed |
| Old Kiln Permit | Use the old kiln behind Chalkhouse Court | Stamped Chalkhouse Court deed |
| Ferry Pass | Use the River Stoop ferry without paying the toll | Stamped River Stoop deed |
| Blacksealed Pass | Enter the blacksealed alley in Sootcellar | Stamped Sootcellar deed |
| Foundry Licence | Use the Penny and Sons private furnace once per day | Chartered Foundry Row deed |
| Warden Seal | Take one elite contract per week without prerequisite | Chartered Warden Steps deed |
| Shrine Leave | Perform one free rite per day at any shrine | Chartered Gravegate deed and Favour offering |
| Road Writ | Unlock one hidden shortcut without Wayfaring level | Chartered Oldroad Gate deed |

## Permit vs Charter

| Feature | Permit | Charter |
|---------|--------|---------|
| Scope | Single door, route, or service | Full district membership |
| Duration | Permanent once earned | Permanent once earned |
| Renewal | None | None |
| Social | No title | District title option |
| Stations | None | Private or priority station access |
| Shops | None | Member discount |

## Forged Permits

Marn Lock in Sootcellar can forge copies of some permits. A forged permit works until inspected by a guard or Warden Holt. If caught, the permit is confiscated and the player gains a bounty.

| Forged Permit | Risk | Cost |
|---------------|------|------|
| Gravegate Permit | High (crypt guards inspect) | Coins, Sleight level |
| Ferry Pass | Low (ferryman rarely inspects) | Coins, Sleight level |
| Blacksealed Pass | Medium (drudges do not care, guards do) | Coins, Sleight level |
| Warden Seal | Very high (Holt inspects all seals) | Coins, high Sleight level |

Forged permits are a Sleight output. They feed into the ledger by creating alternate paths for players who prefer stealth over deeds.
