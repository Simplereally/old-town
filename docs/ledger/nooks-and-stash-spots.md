# Nooks and Stash Spots

Nooks are buildable local storage spots. They are smaller than a bank chest and narrower than a full inventory. A nook holds a specific item set and must be built on location.

## Nook Types

| Nook | Location | Skill | Item Set | Materials |
|------|----------|-------|----------|-----------|
| Crate Nook | Market corners, alleyways | Carpentry | Food, raw fish, cooking tools | 2 planks, 1 iron fitting |
| Wall Nook | Alley walls, building sides | Carpentry, Cartography | Maps, trail scraps, survey kits | 2 planks, 1 bronze fitting, 1 map scrap |
| Grave Nook | Gravegate crypt walls | Favour, Carpentry | Bones, grave beads, burial items | 2 planks, 1 grave fitting, 1 grave bead |
| Bell Nook | Civic places, town hall corners | Civic Ledger | Tokens, stamps, charter scrolls | 2 planks, 1 brass fitting, 1 bell token |
| Road Nook | Route markers, road signs | Cartography | Travel items, ferry passes, rope | 2 planks, 1 iron fitting, 1 route scrap |
| Blacksealed Nook | Shady alleys, hidden corners | Sleight | Lockpicks, forged permits, dark items | 2 planks, 1 blacksealed fitting, 1 lockpick |

## Build Rules

1. **Built, not bought.** A player must carry the materials to the location and build the nook. No shop sells a pre-built nook.
2. **Narrow item sets.** A Crate Nook only holds food and fish. It will not accept a sword or a bead.
3. **Require deed progress.** Most nooks need a Chartered-tier deed in the district before the blueprint unlocks.
4. **Look ordinary before built.** An unbuilt nook location looks like a pile of crates, a loose brick, or a hollow tree. After building, it becomes a usable storage spot.
5. **One per player per type.** A player can build one of each nook type in the world. Building a second Crate Nook destroys the first.
6. **Not safe storage.** Nooks are local storage, not bank storage. They do not protect items on death. They are convenience, not security.

## Material Sources

| Material | Source |
|----------|--------|
| Planks | Woodcutting or sawmill |
| Iron fitting | Smithing or Foundry Row shop |
| Bronze fitting | Smithing or Foundry Row shop |
| Brass fitting | Smithing or higher-tier shop |
| Grave fitting | Grave bead drop or Gravekeeper Soll |
| Blacksealed fitting | Marn Lock or Sleight drop |
| Map scrap | Cartography survey or Finch Quill |
| Route scrap | Cartography survey or route completion |
| Bell token | Civic Ledger deed reward |
| Lockpick | Sleight or Marn Lock |

All material costs are provisional tuning.
