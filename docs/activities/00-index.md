# Activity System

Old Town's activities are repeatable gameplay loops that give players something to do between quests, gathering runs, and open-world skilling. They are not factions, not daily chores, and not mandatory. An activity is a place or a method that trains a skill or set of skills through a structured loop, with inputs, outputs, and a failure state.

## Design rules

1. **Activities are repeatable, not daily.** A player can run the same activity as many times as they want. There is no daily reset, no streak bonus, and no "complete 10 today" counter.
2. **Activities are alternatives, not obligations.** Every activity can be ignored. Open-world skilling and questing remain viable paths.
3. **No reputation grind.** Access is gated by levels, items, or quest state, not by a hidden standing meter.
4. **No battle pass.** No seasonal tracks, no tiered reward ladders, no time-limited participation.
5. **Rewards are narrow and local.** An activity reward says "I did this thing" rather than "I am stronger now." Cosmetics, container upgrades, and shortcut access are good. Universal best-in-slot gear is banned.
6. **Activities are safe, risky, or dangerous — not all three.** Each activity declares its safety classification and sticks to it.
7. **Groupable activities must not require a group.** A player can participate in a public activity alone and still receive full rewards.
8. **Failure costs time or resources, not permanent loss.** A failed activity run means lost inputs, not lost gear or levels.
