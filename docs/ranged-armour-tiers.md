# Ranged Armour Tier System

> **Design authority:** This document is the canonical reference for all ranged armour tiers in Old Town. Ranged uses the same 13-tier power ladder as melee and magic, but with its own material logic: hide, cloth, fieldcraft, stitching, and buckles.
> 
> **The key rule:** Ranged armour is hide, cloth, and fieldcraft — not metal. It is lighter, faster, and less protective than melee armour, but it boosts ranged accuracy and defence.

## Philosophy

Ranged armour is not "leather, hard leather, studded leather." That is too RuneScape. Old Town uses its own cultural tier language for ranged armour, just as it does for melee (Pennywrought) and magic (Chalkmarked).

The material logic is:
- **Melee = metal / martial** — heavy, protective, slow
- **Ranged = hide / cloth / fieldcraft** — light, mobile, accurate
- **Magic = script / bead / ritual** — cloth, magical, enchanted

**Patchhide** is the canonical starter ranged armour tier. Players will call it **"patch"** or **"full patch."**. It is the ranged armour equivalent of **"full penny"** (melee), **"full lath"** (ranged weapons), and **"full chalk"** (magic).

## The "Full Patch" Set (Starter Ranged Armour)

When a player completes the ranged tutorial and gets their first real gear, they should receive or craft:

- Patchhide Coif
- Patchhide Jerkin
- Patchhide Chaps
- Patchhide Vambraces
- Patchhide Treads
- Patchhide Buckler

Players will call this **"full patch"** or **"patch set."** It is the ranged armour starter milestone.

Total ranged defence: +15 (reasonable for early game)
Total cost: ~250 coins (affordable for beginners)

## The Symmetry: Penny, Lath, Chalk, and Patch

The four starter identities are:

- **Melee:** "Got full penny." → Pennywrought Helm, Harness, Ward, Chausses, Sabatons, Gauntlets.
- **Ranged Weapons:** "Got full lath." → Lathwood Shortbow, Longbow, Arrows, War Arrows, Latchbow, Bolts.
- **Ranged Armour:** "Got full patch." → Patchhide Coif, Jerkin, Chaps, Vambraces, Treads, Buckler.
- **Magic:** "Got full chalk." → Chalkmarked Cowl, Robe, Charmward, Wraps, Softshoes, Cuffs.

**Penny**, **Lath**, **Patch**, and **Chalk** are the four starter identities.

## Design Rules

1. **Never use "leather body", "leather chaps", "studded leather".** These are RuneScape terms. Old Town uses Jerkin, Chaps, Coif, Vambraces, Treads, Buckler.
2. **Never use "hard leather", "dragonhide" as tier names.** Use the cultural tier ladder: Patchhide, Boghide, Bellhide, Blackscale, Wardenhide, Graveskin, Argentweave, Crownhide, Starfall Hide.
3. **Tiers are cultural, not elemental.** Patchhide is about being cheap and repaired. Wardenhide is about guard-issue field kit. Starfall Hide is about cosmic rarity.
4. **Armour families are reusable.** A "Jerkin" is always a light torso. A "Buckler" is always a small off-hand shield. The tier changes the material and stats, but the silhouette stays consistent.
5. **Visual identity must be readable at a glance.** A ranger in full Patchhide should look cheap and repaired. A ranger in full Starfall Hide should look like they fell from the sky.
6. **Shorthand is intentional.** Players will say "patch coif", "full bell", "warden chaps", "starfall buckler". Design for this language.
7. **Cobbled is not a tier to be proud of.** It is tutorial gear. It should look like literal garbage.
8. **Ranged armour is lighter than melee.** It trades melee defence for ranged accuracy and mobility.
9. **Buckler is not a Ward.** It is a small, light off-hand item. It blocks ranged attacks but is weak against melee.
10. **Starfall Hide is the mythic.** It should feel like a different material category.
