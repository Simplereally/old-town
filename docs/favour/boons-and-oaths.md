# Boons and Oaths

Boons are readable buffs. Wards are defensive boons. Oaths are powerful commitments with tradeoffs.

## Starter Boons

| Boon | Level | Effect Identity | Cost |
|------|------:|-----------------|------|
| Steady Hand | 1 | Small accuracy boost for Arms/Ranged | Slow drain |
| Warm Blood | 5 | Minor poison/rot resistance | Slow drain |
| Hearthguard | 10 | Small damage reduction | Medium drain |
| Clear Sight | 15 | Ranged/magic accuracy clarity | Medium drain |
| Warden's Nerve | 20 | Reduced fear/interrupt chance | Medium drain |
| Grave Mercy | 25 | Better crypt sustain, anti-undead | Medium drain |

## Midgame Boons

| Boon | Level | Effect Identity |
|------|------:|-----------------|
| Bellward | 30 | Defence against humanoids / town contracts |
| Road Grace | 35 | Stamina and route utility |
| Ashen Hide | 40 | Heat/fire/drake resistance |
| Writshield | 45 | Barrier / Writ utility |
| Bone Quiet | 50 | Undead aggro reduction / Gravegate |
| Greenwold Luck | 55 | Trapping/foraging support |

## High-Tier Boons

| Boon | Level | Effect Identity |
|------|------:|-----------------|
| Argent Mercy | 65 | Cleanse support, anti-curse |
| Crown Oath | 75 | Strong defensive oath with tradeoff |
| Carmine Vow | 80 | Duelist burst, higher drain |
| Blueglass Clarity | 85 | Spell/ranged precision |
| Starfall Witness | 99 | Prestige boon, capped, visible effect |

## Wards

| Ward | Identity |
|------|----------|
| Hearthguard | Reduce small hits |
| Bellward | Resist humanoid pressure |
| Writshield | Resist snares and binds |
| Graveward | Resist rot and undead |
| Argent Ward | Resist curses and poisons |

## Oaths

| Oath | Tradeoff |
|------|----------|
| Crown Oath | Strong defence, reduced damage |
| Carmine Vow | Burst/duel power, weaker sustain |
| Grave Oath | Anti-undead power, worse shrine restore outside Gravegate |
| Warden Oath | Stronger contracts, cannot use blacksealed tools while active |
| Starfall Oath | Rare endgame flex, heavy drain, PvP capped |
