## Core Framing

Favour is not religion mana. Favour is the player's standing with Old Town's shrines, bells, gravekeepers, hearths, saints, wardens, ferrymen, household charms, and old road spirits.

Players train it by performing rites and making offerings. Players spend it by activating boons.
