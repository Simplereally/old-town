# Starter Favour Progression

## First 10 Minutes

1. Player kills Cellar Rats.
2. Player gets Small Bones.
3. Sister Writ teaches Shrine Hearth.
4. Player offers bones for Favour XP.
5. Player unlocks Steady Hand.

## First Hour

1. Player completes Rats Under Tally's.
2. Warden Holt gives Bell Token or Tin Badge.
3. Sister Writ teaches Shrine Candle.
4. Player learns Hearthguard.

## First Gravegate Step

1. Player gets Bone Chips and Grave Dust.
2. Gravekeeper Soll teaches Name the Dead.
3. Player learns Grave Mercy.
4. Gravegate becomes less suicidal.

Quest hook: [`Gravegate Flowers`](../quests/gravegate-flowers.md).
