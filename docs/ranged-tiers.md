# Ranged Weapon Tier System

> **Design authority:** This document is the canonical reference for all ranged weapon and ammunition tiers in Old Town. Ranged uses the same 13-tier power ladder as melee, but with its own material logic: wood, horn, sinew, fletching, metal heads, and bow mechanisms.
> 
> **The key rule:** Bows use organic tier language, while crossbows, arrowheads, bolt-heads, and fittings inherit the melee metal language. So melee has **Full Pennywrought**. Ranged has **Full Lathwood** or **Penny-tipped** ammo.

## Philosophy

Ranged weapons are built from organic materials and metal fittings. The tier language reflects this duality:

- **Bows and staves** use wood, horn, and sinew names (Lathwood, Bogwood, Bellhorn, Warden Yew, Greenwold, Gravebent, Carmine Ash, Argentwood, Crownhorn, Starfall).
- **Crossbows and mechanical parts** use metal names (Pennywrought, Pig Iron, Bellmetal, Blackbar, Wardensteel, Graveiron, Blueglass, Carmine Steel, Argent, Crownsteel, Starfall).
- **Ammunition** follows the material of its head: arrows often use bow-tier names, bolts and heavy heads use metal-tier names.

**Lathwood** is the canonical starter ranged tier. It is better than "wooden", "oak", or "willow." It sounds cheap, handmade, and local. Players will say **"lath bow"**, **"lath arrows"**, **"full lath"**.

### Memorable names

These are the most grounded and sticky ranged weapon names:
- **Bentbow** — sounds broken, cheap, tutorial-grade
- **Latchbow** — sticky, readable, distinct from generic crossbow spam
- **Crankbow** — mechanical, repeating, implies loading
- **Stillbow** — precision, quiet, patient
- **Starbow** — mythic, celestial, final form

## Ammo Naming

Keep ammo dead simple. Players need to parse it instantly.

| Ammo type | Base name | Examples | Notes |
|-----------|-----------|----------|-------|
| Arrows | **Arrows** | Lathwood Arrows, Blackstring Arrows | Standard bow ammo |
| Heavy arrows | **War Arrows** | Gravebent War Arrows | High damage, slow bows |
| Fine arrows | **True Arrows** | Argentwood True Arrows | High accuracy, precision bows |
| Bolts | **Bolts** | Pig Iron Bolts | Standard crossbow ammo |
| Heavy bolts | **Quarrels** | Bellmetal Quarrels | Armour-piercing crossbow |
| Barbed arrows | **Barbs** | Greenwold Barbs | Bleed damage, fast bows |
| Piercing ammo | **Bodkins** | Wardensteel Bodkins | Armour-piercing, first "serious" unlock |
| Broadhead ammo | **Broadheads** | Carmine Steel Broadheads | High damage, large heads |
| Mythic ammo | **Starheads** | Starfall Starheads | Meteor-metal, endgame |

### Why Bodkins?

**Bodkins** are the first "oh this is serious" ranged ammo unlock. Historically real, they sound sharp, and players will remember them. They are the ranged equivalent of upgrading from a Shortblade to a Longblade.

## Ranged Starter Progression

The early ranged journey should feel like this:

1. **Cobbled Bentbow** — first bow, literally scrap
2. **Cobbled Arrows** — first ammo, also scrap
3. **Lathwood Shortbow** — first real bow, proud moment
4. **Lathwood Arrows** — first real ammo
5. **Pig Iron Latchbow** — first crossbow, mechanical
6. **Pig Iron Bolts** — first bolts, heavier
7. **Bellhorn Recurve** — first "pretty" bow, golden horn
8. **Bellmetal Quarrels** — first heavy crossbow bolts
9. **Blackstring Warbow** — first heavy bow, slow but strong
10. **Blackbar Crankbow** — first repeating crossbow, PvP-intimidating
11. **Lathwood Knife** — first thrown weapon, fast and close
12. **Pennywrought Javelin** — first anti-shield throw, heavy

## The Full Lathwood Set (Starter Ranged Set)

When a player completes the ranged tutorial and gets their first real gear, they should receive or craft:

- Lathwood Shortbow
- Lathwood Longbow
- Lathwood Arrows
- Lathwood War Arrows
- Pennywrought Latchbow
- Pennywrought Bolts
- Lathwood Knife
- Lathwood Dart
- Lathwood Quiver
- Lathwood Bracer

Players will call this **"full lath"**. It is the ranged starter milestone.

## The Symmetry: Penny and Lath

The beginner identities are:

- **Melee:** "Got full penny." → Pennywrought Sticker, Shortblade, Handaxe, Cudgel, Helm, Harness, Ward, Chausses, Sabatons, Gauntlets.
- **Ranged:** "Got full lath." → Lathwood Shortbow, Longbow, Arrows, War Arrows, Pennywrought Latchbow, Bolts, Lathwood Knife, Lathwood Dart, Quiver, Bracer.

**Penny** and **Lath** are the two starter identities. They are the first social milestones for melee and ranged respectively.

## Design Rules

1. **Bows and thrown knives/darts use organic tier language.** Lathwood, Bogwood, Bellhorn, Warden Yew, Greenwold, Gravebent, Carmine Ash, Argentwood, Crownhorn.
2. **Crossbows and thrown javelins/throwing axes use metal tier language.** Pennywrought, Pig Iron, Bellmetal, Blackbar, Wardensteel, Graveiron, Blueglass, Carmine Steel, Argent, Crownsteel, Starfall.
3. **Ammo follows the head material.** Arrowheads and bolt-heads use metal names. Bow-tier arrows (Lathwood Arrows, Greenwold Barbs) use bow-tier names. Thrown weapons use their family's tier naming system.
4. **Never use "Wooden Bow", "Oak Bow", "Willow Bow".** These are generic. Lathwood is the ownable starter term.
5. **Never use "Bronze Arrow", "Iron Arrow", "Steel Arrow".** Use tier names: Pennywrought Arrow, Pig Iron Bolt, Wardensteel Bodkin.
6. **Tiers are cultural, not just elemental.** Lathwood is about being cheap and handmade. Warden Yew is about guard-issue discipline. Starfall is about cosmic rarity.
7. **Weapon families are reusable.** A "Shortbow" is always a short bow. A "Latchbow" is always a light crossbow. The tier changes the material and stats, but the silhouette stays consistent.
8. **Visual identity must be readable at a glance.** A player with a Lathwood bow should look cheap and local. A player with a Starfall Starbow should look like they fell from the sky.
9. **Shorthand is intentional.** Players will say "lath bow", "full bell", "warden arbalest", "starfall starbow". Design for this language.
10. **Cobbled is not a tier to be proud of.** It is tutorial gear. It should look like literal garbage.
