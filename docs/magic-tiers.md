# Magic Tier System

> **Design authority:** This document is the canonical reference for all magic items, beads, and spell gear in Old Town. Magic uses the same 13-tier power ladder as melee and ranged, but with its own material logic: chalk, wax, ink, vellum, cloth, bead-cords, and blessed seals.
> 
> **The key rule:** Magic keeps familiar elements but gives them unfamiliar flavor. Runes are not renamed — they are replaced by beads. Staves are not renamed — they are joined by wands, rods, foci, primers, and codices. The shape is recognizable; the names are ownable.

## Philosophy

Magic in Old Town is not ancient mysticism. It is a town technology. Beads are drilled, glazed, etched, strung, blessed, cracked, and burned. Shopkeepers sell them by the pouch. Apprentices string them. Priests bless them. Wardens license them. Thieves counterfeit them. Rich mages flex rare bead strings like jewellery.

**Beads** are the core magic consumable. They are not runes. They are tiny drilled spell-tokens carried in pouches, strung onto cords, socketed into staves, cracked between fingers, burned in candleflame, or scattered into spell circles.

**Chalkmarked** is the canonical starter magic tier. Players will call it **"chalk"** or **"full chalk"**. It is the magic equivalent of **"full penny"** (melee) and **"full lath"** (ranged).

## Beads

### Element Beads

The four elemental beads. Familiar elements, unfamiliar flavor.

| Function | Name | Shorthand | Notes |
|----------|------|-----------|-------|
| Air | **Gust Bead** | gust | Instantly legible, more flavor than "air" |
| Water | **Tide Bead** | tide | Broad enough for water, ice, healing, rivers, rain |
| Earth | **Loam Bead** | loam | Earthy, old, village-coded |
| Fire | **Ember Bead** | ember | Starter-friendly, warm, iconic |

### Catalytic Beads

These are where Old Town gets personality.

| Function | Name | Shorthand | Used for |
|----------|------|-----------|----------|
| Basic spell intent | **Wit Bead** | wit | Starter combat, simple utility |
| Body / weakness / binding | **Bone Bead** | bone | Curses, snares, weakness |
| Enchanting / cosmic | **Star Bead** | star | Jewellery, staves, portals, magical items |
| Chaos / unstable damage | **Hex Bead** | hex | Mid-level combat, curses |
| Nature / alchemy | **Grove Bead** | grove | Growth, alchemy, conversion, farming magic |
| Law / teleport | **Writ Bead** | writ | Teleports, permissions, barriers |
| Death | **Grave Bead** | grave | Higher combat, necrotic spells |
| Blood | **Blood Bead** | blood | Siphon, sacrifice, sustain |
| Soul | **Soul Bead** | soul | High curses, possession, binding |
| Wrath / top damage | **Ruin Bead** | ruin | Highest destructive spell tier |

**The best catalytic beads:** Wit, Writ, Grave, and Ruin.

- **Wit Bead** replaces "mind rune" without sounding like a clone. "I'm out of wit beads" is funny, sticky, and player-speak friendly.
- **Writ Bead** gives teleport and law magic an Old Town identity: court orders, permits, warded doors, licensed passage, signed magical authority.

### Combination Beads

Crafted beads that count as two element types.

| Combination | Name |
|-------------|------|
| Gust + Tide | **Mist Bead** |
| Gust + Loam | **Dust Bead** |
| Tide + Loam | **Mud Bead** |
| Gust + Ember | **Smoke Bead** |
| Tide + Ember | **Steam Bead** |
| Loam + Ember | **Slag Bead** |

**Slag Bead** is the important divergence. It feels Old Town: forge, furnace, foundry, soot, industry. Better than "lava" for this world.

## Bead Pouches

Bead management is part of the fantasy. Pouches, strings, loops, charms, cords, and licensed containers.

| Pouch | Function | Identity |
|-------|----------|----------|
| **Threadbare Bead Pouch** | Holds 2 bead types | Early-game cloth pouch, cheap and fraying |
| **Waxed Bead Pouch** | Holds 3 bead types | Weatherproof, candlewax-sealed, apprentice upgrade |
| **Warden Bead Pouch** | Holds 4 bead types | Licensed pouch for proper spellwork and teleports |
| **Grave Bead Pouch** | Stores Bone / Grave / Soul beads more efficiently | Crypt-runner pouch with bone toggles and black cord |
| **Starfall Bead Pouch** | Endgame omni-pouch | Meteor-thread, silver clasps, rare prestige item |

Players will say:
- "Get a waxed pouch before training magic."
- "Warden pouch is huge for teleports."
- "Grave pouch makes crypt runs cheaper."
- "Starfall pouch is best-in-slot."

## Magic Gear Families

### Weapons

| Slot / type | Base name | Example | Notes |
|-------------|-----------|---------|-------|
| Wand | **Wand** | Chalkmarked Wand | Fast, one-handed, low damage |
| Staff | **Staff** | Bellwax Staff | Two-handed, rune savings, slow |
| Battle staff | **Rod** | Blacksalt Rod | Two-handed, melee/magic hybrid |
| Orb / focus | **Focus** | Blueglass Focus | Off-hand, magic accuracy boost |
| Spellbook | **Primer** | Chalkmarked Primer | Low-level spellbook |
| Higher spellbook | **Codex** | Argent Script Codex | High-level spellbook |

Use **Primer** early, **Codex** later. That gives progression texture.

### Armour

| Slot | Base name | Example | Notes |
|------|-----------|---------|-------|
| Head | **Cowl** | Gravebound Cowl | Cloth hood, magic bonus |
| Chest | **Robe** | Greenwold Robe | Cloth torso, magic bonus |
| Legs | **Wraps** | Tallowbound Wraps | Cloth legs, magic bonus |
| Hands | **Cuffs** | Carmine Ink Cuffs | Cloth gloves, magic bonus |
| Feet | **Softshoes** | Crownvellum Softshoes | Cloth boots, magic bonus |
| Off-hand | **Charmward** | Warden Script Charmward | Defensive charm-board, bead-frame, warding focus |

**Charmward** is the offhand identity: not a shield, not a book, not an orb. It is a defensive charm-board, bead-frame, or warding focus held in the offhand.

## Spell Naming

Do not call early spells "Wind Strike / Water Strike / Earth Strike / Fire Strike". Too close.

Use a shared verb ladder:

| Tier | Spell force word | Example |
|------|-----------------|---------|
| 1 | **Flick** | Ember Flick |
| 2 | **Dart** | Gust Dart |
| 3 | **Bolt** | Tide Bolt |
| 4 | **Burst** | Loam Burst |
| 5 | **Lance** | Ember Lance |
| 6 | **Ruin** | Starfall Ruin |

So early magic looks like:
- Gust Flick
- Tide Flick
- Loam Flick
- Ember Flick

Midgame:
- Ember Bolt
- Gust Burst
- Tide Lance

Endgame:
- Ruin of Ember
- Grave Ruin
- Starfall Ruin

## The Full Chalkmarked Set (Starter Magic Set)

When a player completes the magic tutorial and gets their first real gear, they should receive or craft:

- Chalkmarked Wand
- Chalkmarked Staff
- Chalkmarked Primer
- Chalkmarked Cowl
- Chalkmarked Robe
- Chalkmarked Wraps
- Chalkmarked Cuffs
- Chalkmarked Softshoes
- Chalkmarked Charmward
- Threadbare Bead Pouch

Players will call this **"full chalk"**. It is the magic starter milestone.

## The Symmetry: Penny, Lath, Patch, and Chalk

The four starter identities are:

- **Melee:** "Got full penny." → Pennywrought Sticker, Shortblade, Handaxe, Cudgel, Helm, Harness, Ward, Chausses, Sabatons, Gauntlets.
- **Ranged Weapons:** "Got full lath." → Lathwood Shortbow, Longbow, Arrows, War Arrows, Pennywrought Latchbow, Bolts, Quiver, Bracer.
- **Ranged Armour:** "Got full patch." → Patchhide Coif, Jerkin, Chaps, Vambraces, Treads, Buckler.
- **Magic:** "Got full chalk." → Chalkmarked Wand, Staff, Primer, Cowl, Robe, Wraps, Cuffs, Softshoes, Charmward, Threadbare Bead Pouch.

**Penny**, **Lath**, **Patch**, and **Chalk** are the four starter identities.

## Design Rules

1. **Beads are not runes.** They are drilled, strung, traded, licensed, blessed, cracked, burned, counterfeited, socketed, hoarded, and flexed. They feel physically real.
2. **Never use "air rune", "water rune", "fire rune", "earth rune".** Use Gust, Tide, Loam, Ember.
3. **Never use "mind rune", "body rune", "chaos rune", "death rune", "blood rune", "soul rune", "law rune", "nature rune".** Use Wit, Bone, Hex, Grave, Blood, Soul, Writ, Grove.
4. **Never use "Wind Strike", "Water Strike", "Fire Strike", "Earth Strike".** Use the verb ladder: Flick, Dart, Bolt, Burst, Lance, Ruin.
5. **Tiers are cultural, not elemental.** Chalkmarked is about being cheap and apprentice-made. Warden Script is about being regulated and licensed. Starfall is about cosmic rarity.
6. **Magic gear families are reusable.** A "Wand" is always a fast one-handed magic weapon. A "Primer" is always a low-level spellbook. The tier changes the material and stats, but the silhouette stays consistent.
7. **Visual identity must be readable at a glance.** A mage in full Chalkmarked should look like a cheap apprentice. A mage in full Starfall should look like they fell from the sky.
8. **Shorthand is intentional.** Players will say "chalk wand", "full bell", "warden script", "starfall codex". Design for this language.
9. **Cobbled is not a tier to be proud of.** It is tutorial gear. It should look like literal garbage.
10. **Bead pouches are progression.** Upgrading from Threadbare to Waxed to Warden to Grave to Starfall is as important as upgrading a weapon.
