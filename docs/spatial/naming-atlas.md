# Naming Atlas

Old Town's place names are cultural artefacts. They sound like the people who live there made them up, not like a design team generated them. The naming atlas defines the grammar, the good patterns, the banned patterns, and the starter outer area names.

## Core Rule

**Names must sound like locals would say them.**

A name that sounds like a quest marker or a zone label breaks immersion. A name that sounds like a farmer pointed and said "that's the Patch" keeps it.

## Naming Grammar

| Source | How It Works | Examples |
|--------|--------------|----------|
| **Civic object** | Named after the landmark or building | Market Bell, Counting House, Warden Steps |
| **Trade object** | Named after the craft or tool | Foundry Row, Lath Yard, Chalkhouse Court |
| **Route object** | Named after the road or gate | Oldroad Gate, North Quarry Road, River Stoop |
| **Material** | Named after the ground or resource | Sootcellar, Gravegate, Bellstone |
| **Place memory** | Named after an event or person | Tally's (implied former owner), The Old Kiln |
| **Creature sign** | Named after what lives there | Crowmile Road, Batroof (future) |

## Good Place Name Patterns

| Pattern | Structure | Examples |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| **Object + place** | Thing + location word | Market Bell, Counting House, Lath Yard |
| **Trade + lane** | Craft + road word | Foundry Row, Patch Lane, Chalkhouse Court |
| **Route + threshold** | Road + gate or stoop | Oldroad Gate, River Stoop, Gravegate |
| **Material + terrain** | Substance + ground word | Sootcellar, Bellstone, Quarry grit |
| **NPC legacy** | Person + possessive or memorial | Tally's cellar, The Old Kiln (implied builder) |
| **Folk phrase** | Common saying or observation | Warden Steps (steps you climb to see the warden), Crowmile (crows for a mile) |

## Banned Naming Patterns

The following patterns are banned from Old Town naming. They sound like generic fantasy or game design, not like local culture.

- **Dark Forest** — Too generic. Use "Bellwood Copse" or "Witchwood Verge."
- **Dragon Cave** — Too generic. Use "The Old Kiln" or "Ash Flats."
- **Goblin Camp** — Too generic. Use "Tinstone Cut" or "North Quarry Road."
- **Mystic Tower** — Too generic. Use "Chalkhouse Court" or "Shrine Hearth."
- **Ancient Ruins** — Too generic. Use "Oldroad Gate" or "The Old Kiln."
- **Shadowlands** — Too generic. Use "Sootstairs" or "Lowgrave."
- **Crystal Woods** — Too generic. Use "Bellwood Copse" or future names.
- **Beginner Zone** — Meta name. Never use.
- **Starter Cave** — Meta name. Never use.
- **Cave 1 / Forest 1** — Numbered zones. Never use.
- **North Area** — Direction-only. Use "North Quarry Road" or "Tinstone Cut."
- **East Field** — Direction-only. Use "Patchfield" or "Wardenbrook."
