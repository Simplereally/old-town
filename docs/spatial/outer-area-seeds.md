# Outer Area Seeds

Outer area seeds are the named spaces beyond the 96x96 starter region. They are not yet playable. They exist as silhouettes, road signs, and NPC dialogue to create a sense of a larger world and to give future content a named place to land.

## Core Rule

**Seed expansion but do not overbuild.**

Name the spaces. Place the road signs. Hint at the creatures. But do not build maps, quests, or spawns for outer areas until the starter region is complete and playable.

## First Ring

The first ring is visible from the starter region edges or reachable by short road extensions. Players should be able to name these places by the end of their first week.

| Area | Direction | Future Role |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Crowmile Road** | West | Oldroad Trail extension. Cartography, road crows, first long walk. |
| **Bellwood Copse** | North-west | Small wood. Woodcutting expansion, foraging, first wolf tease. |
| **Tinstone Cut** | North | Quarry extension. Better ore (tinstone, iron), more goblins, first climb. |
| **Patchfield** | South | Field expansion. Trapping, future farming, creature pens. |
| **Wardenbrook** | East | Stream and ford. Fishing expansion, patrol routes, bridge building tease. |
| **Lowgrave** | South-east | Grave expansion. Group crypts, Favour rites, deeper rot. |
| **The Old Kiln** | East | Foundry extension. Drake lair, fire clay, advanced smithing. |
| **Sootstairs** | Down | Under expansion. Blacksealed territory, Sleight hub, hidden market. |

## Second Ring

The second ring is named in NPC dialogue, road signs, and ledger trails. Players hear about these places before they can go there.

| Area | Future Identity |
|------|-----------------|
| **Crownheart Forest** | Deep wood beyond Bellwood. Advanced woodcutting, first dungeon tease. |
| **Blackbar Cut** | Deep quarry. Barred entrance, advanced mining, first group combat. |
| **Grave Underways** | Crypt network below Lowgrave. First multi-level under space. |
| **Witchwood Verge** | Haunted wood edge. Magic resources, witch NPC tease. |
| **Moth Ferry Crossing** | River crossing beyond Wardenbrook. First ferry mechanic, new region access. |
| **Carmine Yard** | Red-dyed tannery district. Advanced tailoring, dye mechanics. |
| **Argent Chapel Road** | Shrine road beyond Shrine Hearth. Advanced Favour, first chapel building. |

## Seeding Techniques

| Technique | How It Works | Example |
|-----------|--------------|---------|
| **Road sign** | Signpost at region edge pointing to named road | Oldroad Gate sign: "Crowmile Road — 2 miles" |
| **NPC dialogue** | NPC mentions place in conversation | Warden: "We lost a patrol in Lowgrave last week." |
| **Ledger trail** | Clue scroll references outer area | Oldroad Trail step: "Where the bellwood meets the road" |
| **Smoke silhouette** | Visible effect on horizon | Drake smoke visible from Foundry Row on clear days |
| **Blocked path** | Physical barrier with reason | Blackbar Cut gate: "Barred by order of the Warden" |
| **Ferry absence** | Missing boat with note | Moth Ferry dock: "Ferry departs at dawn. Return tomorrow." |
