# Accessory Tier System

> **Design authority for all accessories in Old Town.** In-world, these are called **Keepsakes** — small, personal objects that say what kind of player you are becoming. They are not "gear." They are not stat sticks. They are build glue.
>
> **Rule:** No accessory is universally best. Every choice is a tradeoff. Every choice is a statement.

## Philosophy

Accessories are the items that turn a build into a *playstyle*. A Cape does not make you stronger. It makes you *the kind of player who wears that cape*.

**Design principles:**
1. **Accessories are build glue, not power.** They amplify weapon and armour choices. They do not replace them.
2. **No universal BIS.** Every slot has tradeoffs. A Cape that boosts melee speed reduces magic accuracy. An Amulet that boosts beads reduces defence.
3. **PvP caps exist.** No accessory effect exceeds a 15% bonus in PvP. PvE bonuses can be higher but must feel earned.
4. **Cultural grounding.** Every name references Old Town culture: wardens, bells, writs, graveyards, beadwork, markets, glassworks, hunters, ferries, old roads, guild badges, shrines, foundries, folk superstition.
5. **Tactile language.** A Cape is "moth-eaten," "waxed," "bell-buckled." An Amulet is "tallow-sealed," "salt-stained," "glass-faceted." No generic fantasy filler.
6. **Six slots, six identities.** Cape (visibility), Amulet (belief), Ring (commitment), Charm (luck), Belt (preparation), Trophy (history).

## The 13 Progression Bands

Accessories use the same 13-tier structure as weapons and armour, but the names are softer. These are not "materials." They are conditions, treatments, and cultural markers.

| Order | Band | Shorthand | Condition | Visual Identity |
|------|------|-----------|-----------|-----------------|
| 0 | **Cobbled** | cobbled | Found, scavenged | Rags, twine, rust, hope |
| 1 | **Threadbare** | thread | Worn, inherited | Faded cloth, loose stitching, old leather |
| 2 | **Waxed** | waxed | Treated, preserved | Beeswax, oil, waterproofed, stiff |
| 3 | **Bellstruck** | bell | Town-forged, blessed | Brass fittings, warm patina, bell motifs |
| 4 | **Blacksealed** | black | PvP-marked, aggressive | Dark iron, salt seals, prison marks |
| 5 | **Warden-Issued** | warden | Guard-aligned, disciplined | Blue-white trim, writ stamps, official |
| 6 | **Greenwold** | green | Forest-touched, wild | Green enamel, vine etching, moss |
| 7 | **Gravebound** | grave | Death-touched, solemn | Charcoal steel, bone-white, ash |
| 8 | **Blueglass** | blue | Magical, prestige | Blue inlays, polished glass, gem facets |
| 9 | **Carmine** | carmine | War-tested, dangerous | Red lacquer, black leather, silver edges |
| 10 | **Argent** | argent | Noble, merciful | Bright silver, clean highlights, heraldic |
| 11 | **Crown** | crown | Royal, heraldic | Gold edging, white plates, lion motifs |
| 12 | **Starfall** | starfall | Mythic, endgame | Dark steel, pale highlights, meteor flecks |

**Key difference from weapon tiers:** Accessories do not have "families." Each slot has 13 unique items, one per band. The items are not variations of a base type. They are distinct objects with distinct stories.

## The Six Slots

| Slot | In-World Name | What It Says | Typical Bonuses |
|------|---------------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Cape** | Back-cloth | "This is who I am in a crowd." | Speed, defence, visibility, set bonuses |
| **Amulet** | Neck-keep | "This is what I believe." | Magic, prayer, beads, accuracy |
| **Ring** | Finger-bind | "This is what I commit to." | Single-stat focus, proc chance, resistance |
| **Charm** | Pocket-luck | "This is what I carry for luck." | Utility, skilling, drop rate, travel |
| **Belt** | Waist-strap | "This is what I prepare for." | Inventory, ammo, consumables, weight |
| **Trophy** | Wall-mount (worn) | "This is what I have survived." | Boss bonuses, rare drops, bragging rights |

## Rules for Every Accessory

### 1. No Stat Sticks
An accessory must do something interesting. "+5 Strength" is not interesting. "+10% attack speed, -5% magic accuracy" is interesting because it is a tradeoff.

### 2. No Universal BIS
There is no "best ring for all situations." A ring that boosts melee is worse for magic. A ring that boosts skilling is worse for combat. Players must choose per-activity.

### 3. PvP Caps
No accessory bonus exceeds 15% in PvP. This includes:
- Damage bonuses
- Accuracy bonuses
- Speed bonuses
- Defence bonuses
- Proc chances

PvE bonuses can exceed 15% but must require specific conditions (e.g., "against undead," "while below 25% HP").

### 4. Tradeoffs Everywhere
Every positive has a negative. Examples:
- Cape: +10% melee speed, -5% magic accuracy
- Amulet: +15% bead savings, -10% melee defence
- Ring: +10% poison chance, -5% HP regeneration
- Charm: +5% drop rate, -5% XP rate
- Belt: +3 inventory slots, -5% run energy
- Trophy: +20% damage against bosses, -10% damage against normal monsters

### 5. Cultural References
Every name and description must reference Old Town culture. No "Amulet of Power." No "Ring of Wealth." Instead:
- "Warden Writ Amulet" — issued by the town guard, stamped with a writ seal
- "Bellfounder's Ring" — forged by the bell-foundry guild, warm brass
- "Gravebone Charm" — carved from graveyard soil and old bone, folk superstition

### 6. Build-Glue Philosophy
Accessories answer the question: "What kind of player are you becoming?"

A player with:
- **Carmine Duelist Cape** + **Carmine Eye Amulet** + **Carmine Duelist Ring** = "I am a PvP duelist who commits to the red."
- **Greenwold Cloak** + **Greenwold Heart Amulet** + **Greenwold Ring** = "I am a forest ranger who lives in the wild."
- **Blueglass Cape** + **Blueglass Focus Amulet** + **Blueglass Lens Ring** = "I am a prestige mage who bought into the glassworks."

## Acquisition Philosophy

Accessories are not bought from a general store. They are:
- **Dropped** by specific monsters (e.g., Gravekeeper's Tooth from graveyard wights)
- **Crafted** by specific skills (e.g., Blueglass Lens Ring from Glassworking)
- **Rewarded** by specific quests (e.g., Warden's Broken Badge from the Warden quest line)
- **Found** in specific locations (e.g., Starfall Heart-Shard from meteor strike sites)
- **Traded** from specific NPCs (e.g., Bellkeeper Cloak from the Bell Tower keeper)

## Design Rules for Adding Accessories

1. **Never use generic fantasy names.** No "Amulet of Glory," "Ring of Wealth," "Berserker Ring."
2. **Every accessory has a tradeoff.** If it only gives bonuses, it has failed.
3. **PvP caps are hard.** 15% maximum for any bonus in PvP. No exceptions.
4. **Cultural grounding is mandatory.** The name must reference Old Town culture.
5. **Tactile language is mandatory.** The description must feel like an object you could hold.
6. **Build-glue is the goal.** The accessory must make a specific playstyle better, not every playstyle better.
7. **Higher bands are not strictly better.** A Starfall Cape is not "Cobbled Cape but more." It is a different object for a different player.
8. **Accessories are non-stackable.** One per slot. No duplicates.
9. **Rings are two slots.** Left and right ring slots. Two different rings, or one ring in each.
10. **Trophies are rare.** Not every player will have a Trophy. They are earned, not bought.
