# Boss System

Old Town’s boss layer is a named-encounter system built on OSRS-style primitives: simple mechanics, memorable lairs, unique drops, and low punishment. Bosses are local, strange, dirty, and useful. They teach combat style, connect to skills, and drop rewards that players remember.

## Design rules

1. **A boss is not just a high-HP monster.** Every boss must have a lair, an access rule, a mechanic, and a unique drop.
2. **Bosses are repeatable.** A player can fight a boss as many times as they want. No daily lock, no cooldown.
3. **Bosses are local, not cosmic.** They do not have giant lore, multi-phase god forms, or raid mechanics. They are strange named creatures in specific places.
4. **Bosses teach one thing.** A boss should teach a combat style, a skill connection, or a movement pattern.
5. **No best-in-slot creep.** Boss drops are narrow, memorable, or cosmetic. They do not replace open-world gear progression.
6. **Failure is recoverable.** A lost boss fight means lost food or time, not lost levels or permanent gear.
7. **Bosses use existing places.** Lairs are in existing areas, districts, or guilds. They do not require new world zones.
8. **Wardenry can assign, but not require.** A boss can be a Wardenry task target, but it must remain accessible without Wardenry.

## Player Shorthand

| Shorthand | What players mean |
|-----------|-------------------|
| "Do Cellar King for rat tail." | The Cellar King is a starter boss with a useful drop. |
| "Mudhook is free with javelins." | Mudhook Grib is weak to javelins. |
| "Kiln Ashling drops warm scale." | Ashling in the Kiln has a material drop. |
| "Lowgrave boss needs candles." | The Wrong Flower requires candles to fight. |
| "Blackbar foreman eats shields." | Blackbar Foreman breaks armour. |
| "Carmine Yard has the parry boss." | Redcord Champion is a duelist boss. |
| "Bring Tide beads to Kiln." | Tide beads help against Ashling in the Kiln. |
| "Don’t fight Gravekeeper’s Wrong Son without cleanse." | That boss requires cleansing items. |
| "The Cut Bell ate my pick." | The Cut Bell damages tools. |
| "The Door That Bites is a Sleight check." | That boss is a puzzle-combat hybrid. |
| "Crowpost Jack drops the crow token." | That boss has a unique currency drop. |
| "Aunt Bracken’s Stump is a woodcutting boss." | That boss connects to woodcutting. |
| "I got the bat claw." | Defeated the Bell Bat Mother and earned the trophy. |
| "The Snapper got my bait." | Lost to Old Snapper after luring. |
| "The stump dropped a bark bundle." | Aunt Bracken’s Stump has a crafting drop. |
