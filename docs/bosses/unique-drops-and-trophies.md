# Unique Drops and Trophies

This document defines the unique drop rules for Old Town bosses. A unique drop is an item that only one boss drops. It is narrow, memorable, and connected to the boss's fantasy. Unique drops are not best-in-slot items. They are identity items, crafting materials, and trophies.

## Unique Drop Rules

1. **A unique should make players remember the boss.** The drop is tied to the boss's name and fantasy.
2. **A unique must be narrow, not universally best.** It is useful for a specific build, skill, or purpose.
3. **A unique should connect to the boss fantasy.** The drop is a part of the boss: a tooth, a claw, a scale, a petal.
4. **Starter uniques should be modest.** They are useful but not mandatory. They are stepping stones, not endgame items.
5. **Trophies and cosmetics are safer than raw power.** A trophy is a proof of victory. A cosmetic is a display of identity.
6. **Materials can feed crafting without becoming mandatory.** A unique material is useful for crafting but not the only source.
7. **Pets can be seeded later, not required now.** Pet drops are a future feature. They are not required for the initial boss layer.

## Drop Categories

| Category | Description | Example |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| **Trophy** | A proof item that shows the player defeated the boss. | cellar_king_whisker |
| **Cosmetic** | An appearance item that changes the player's look. | foundry_burnt_apron_trim |
| **Material** | A crafting material that feeds a skill or recipe. | tallow_drake_scale |
| **Narrow utility** | An item that improves a specific skill or activity. | snapped_hook |
| **Recipe unlock** | A hint or item that unlocks a crafting recipe. | ashling_ember_recipe |
| **Container upgrade part** | A part that improves a specific container. | shell_latch |
| **Emote** | A gesture or animation that the player can perform. | ratcatcher_bow |
| **Pet seed** | A future pet drop. Not required now. | tiny_cellar_rat |

## Drop Identity by Boss

### The Cellar King

- **ratcatcher_tail:** A proof item for Wardenry. The tail is a common drop that shows the player has fought the King.
- **torn_ledger_scrap:** A crafting material for Cartography. The scrap is a common drop that feeds document restoration.
- **cellar_fur_bundle:** A crafting material for Tailoring. The fur is a common drop that feeds hide crafting.
- **bent_nail_ring:** A rare unique accessory. The ring is a starter unique that grants a small bonus against rats.
- **cellar_king_whisker:** A trophy. The whisker is a proof item that players display.

### Mudhook Grib

- **pig_iron_scrap_bundle:** A crafting material for Smithing. The scrap is a common drop that feeds metal crafting.
- **mudhook_charm:** A unique accessory. The charm grants a small bonus against mud creatures.
- **crude_hookblade:** A starter weapon. The hookblade is a common drop that is useful for early combat.
- **quarry_token:** An activity currency. The token is earned by fighting the boss and spent at the quarry shop.
- **mudhook_jaw:** A trophy. The jaw is a proof item that players display.

### Ashling in the Kiln

## Reward Tiers

| Tier | Condition | Reward Quality |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| **Base** | Defeat the boss. | Common drops, basic materials. |
| **Standard** | Defeat the boss with moderate success. | Above plus uncommon drops, more materials. |
| **Advanced** | Defeat the boss with high success. | Above plus rare drop, trophy. |
| **Rare** | Defeat the boss with exceptional success or rare chance. | Above plus rare unique, quest clue, or cosmetic. |

## Drop Rate Rules

1. **Common drops should drop every kill.** They are the baseline reward.
2. **Uncommon drops should drop 1 in 3 kills.** They are reliable but not guaranteed.
3. **Rare drops should drop 1 in 10 kills.** They are special but not obligatory.
4. **Trophies should drop 1 in 5 kills.** They are proof items that players earn over time.
5. **Unique drops should drop 1 in 20 kills.** They are memorable but not mandatory.
6. **No drop should be the only source of a material.** The material must be available elsewhere.
7. **No drop should be best-in-slot.** The drop is narrow, not universally best.
