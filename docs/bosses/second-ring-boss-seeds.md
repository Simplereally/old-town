## Crownheart Antler-King

**boss_id:** `crownheart_antler_king`

**area:** Crownheart Forest

**category:** Duel Boss

**fantasy:** A massive stag with a crown of antlers that has grown into a throne. The Antler-King is the spirit of the Crownheart Forest, and it challenges any hunter who enters its domain. It is a duelist that tests hunting and precision.

**likely access:** Crownheart Forest route unlocked, Hunting 30 or higher.

**mechanic promise:** The Antler-King charges in a pattern. The player must dodge the charge and strike during the recovery. The antlers can be broken, reducing the charge speed. Ranged weapons are effective but the forest cover makes line of sight difficult.

**reward identity:** Hunting materials, antler trophies, and a rare unique that improves hunting.

**memorable drop:** crownheart_antler — a trophy that proves the player has faced the king of the forest.

## Blackbar Foreman

**boss_id:** `blackbar_foreman`

**area:** Blackbar Cut

**category:** Duel Boss

**fantasy:** A foreman who has been buried under a blackbar collapse and has returned as a spirit of the mine. He is covered in blackbar ore and carries a shield made of mine scrap. He is a duelist that tests shield breaking and armour penetration.

**likely access:** Blackbar Cut route unlocked, Mining 30 or higher.

**mechanic promise:** The Foreman raises a scrap shield that blocks all frontal damage. The player must break the shield with a maul, javelin, or armour-breaking ability. Once the shield is down, the Foreman is vulnerable but deals heavy damage.

**reward identity:** Mining materials, shield-breaking tools, and a rare unique that improves armour penetration.

**memorable drop:** blackbar_shield_breaker — a rare unique that grants armour-breaking bonus against shielded enemies.

## Mourner Below

**boss_id:** `mourner_below`

**area:** Grave Underways

**category:** Quest Boss

**fantasy:** A mourner who has been buried below the Grave Underways and has become a spirit of grief. The Mourner is a Gravebound Favour boss that tests cleanse and support abilities.

**likely access:** Grave Underways route unlocked, Favour 30 or higher, and a quest that introduces the Mourner.

**mechanic promise:** The Mourner emits a grief aura that reduces the player's damage and healing. The player must cleanse the aura with Favour abilities or salves. The Mourner also summons grave wisps that must be cleared before the aura can be cleansed.

**reward identity:** Favour materials, grave items, and a rare unique that improves cleanse abilities.

**memorable drop:** mourner_shroud — a rare unique that grants grief resistance and improves cleanse effects.

## Witchwood Listener

**boss_id:** `witchwood_listener`

**area:** Witchwood Verge

**category:** Skilling Boss

**fantasy:** A creature that has grown from the Witchwood trees and listens to bead magic. The Listener is a bead and magic boss that tests beadwork and spellcasting.

**likely access:** Witchwood Verge route unlocked, Beadwork 30 or Magic 30.

**mechanic promise:** The Listener is immune to physical damage. Only bead magic and spells can harm it. The player must craft and use specific beads to weaken the Listener's defences. The Listener also reflects spells back at the caster if the wrong bead is used.

**reward identity:** Bead materials, magic items, and a rare unique that improves bead magic.

**memorable drop:** listener_bead — a rare unique bead that improves listening and detection abilities.

---

## Moth Ferry Lantern

**boss_id:** `moth_ferry_lantern`

**area:** Moth Ferry Crossing

**category:** Skilling Boss

**fantasy:** A giant moth that carries a ferry lantern. The moth is attracted to light and attacks anyone who carries a lantern or torch. It is a river and light boss that tests light management and water combat.

**likely access:** Moth Ferry Crossing route unlocked, Wayfaring 30 or higher.

**mechanic promise:** The moth is attracted to light sources. The player must manage their light or the moth will swarm. The moth is weak to water and Tide spells. The player must douse the moth's lantern with water to weaken it.

**reward identity:** Light materials, river items, and a rare unique that improves light management.

**memorable drop:** lantern_moth_wing — a rare unique that grants light resistance and improves lantern duration.

---

## Redcord Champion

**boss_id:** `redcord_champion`

**area:** Carmine Yard

**category:** Duel Boss

**fantasy:** A champion duelist who has never lost a fight. The Redcord Champion is a master of parry and riposte, and he challenges any player who enters the Carmine Yard. He is a duelist that tests parry and combat skill.

**likely access:** Carmine Yard route unlocked, Arms 30 or higher.

**mechanic promise:** The Champion attacks with a pattern that can be parried. A successful parry opens a riposte window. The Champion also has a feint that breaks parry if the player parries too early. The fight is a rhythm test.

**reward identity:** Combat materials, parry tools, and a rare unique that improves parry timing.

**memorable drop:** redcord_glove — a rare unique that grants parry bonus and improves riposte damage.

---

## Argent Penitent

**boss_id:** `argent_penitent`

**area:** Argent Chapel Road

**category:** Quest Boss

**fantasy:** A penitent who has been walking the Argent Chapel Road for centuries, seeking forgiveness. The Penitent is a cleanse and support boss that tests the player's ability to heal, cleanse, and protect.

**likely access:** Argent Chapel Road route unlocked, Favour 30 or higher, and a quest that introduces the Penitent.

**mechanic promise:** The Penitent does not attack directly. Instead, it places curses and debuffs on the player. The player must cleanse the curses, heal through the damage, and protect themselves with wards. The Penitent is a support test, not a damage test.

**reward identity:** Favour materials, cleanse items, and a rare unique that improves support abilities.

**memorable drop:** argent_chalice — a rare unique that improves cleanse and healing effects.
