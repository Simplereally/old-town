# Reward Calibration

Rewards should reinforce loops and avoid direct power dumps.

## Reward Profiles

| Activity | Reward Profile |
|----------|----------------|
| Gathering | Low coin, material value |
| Production | Low direct coin, trade value |
| Combat | Coin trickle plus drops |
| Wardenry | Best early structured coin |
| Quests | Unlocks, small coin, unique utility |
| Sleight | Risky coin, reputation consequences |
| Boss/miniboss | Rare trophy, not stable income |
| Shops | Convenience at premium |

## Wardenry Reward Bands

| Contract | Coin Reward | Other Reward |
|----------|------------:|--------------|
| Rats Under Tally's | 25-50 | Bell Token, Favour XP |
| Mud on the North Road | 50-90 | Pig Iron Scrap |
| Bats in the Bell Rafters | 40-80 | Bellfeathers |
| Mites at Gravegate | 80-140 | Grave Dust / Favour |

## Design Rules

1. Quests mostly unlock.
2. Minibosses produce memories and trophies, not wages.
3. Wardenry is a clear early route for structured coin.
4. Combat drops should feed skilling.
