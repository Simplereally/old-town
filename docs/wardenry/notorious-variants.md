# Notorious Variants

Old Town equivalent of superior variants. Rare stronger spawns that appear while on a Wardenry contract.

## Notorious Variant Rules

1. Only appears while on a Wardenry contract.
2. Stronger than the normal creature.
3. Gives bonus Wardenry XP and marks.
4. Drops a narrow trophy or material.
5. Visually distinct.
6. Not required for progression.
7. Cannot be forced to spawn.
8. Does not replace the normal creature spawn.

## Spawn Mechanics

1. 1 in 50 chance per eligible creature kill while on contract.
2. Spawn replaces the next normal creature spawn in the area.
3. Variant is announced with a message.
4. Variant has a 5-minute despawn timer.
5. Only one variant per contract.

## Drop Rules

1. Variant always drops its unique trophy.
2. Variant drops extra normal creature drops.
3. Variant gives 2x base Wardenry XP.
4. Variant gives 3x base Warden Marks.
5. Variant drops are not required for any quest or recipe.
