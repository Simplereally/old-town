# Named Warrants and Boss Tasks

Boss tasks in Wardenry. Named Warrants are the Old Town equivalent of boss Slayer tasks.

## Named Warrant Rules

1. Boss tasks are opt-in or uncommon.
2. Completing a Named Warrant gives marks and a chance at boss-specific reward.
3. Wardenry can improve drop rolls slightly but does not force unique drops.
4. Boss access rules still apply — the player must still meet the boss's unlock requirements.
5. Widening a boss task is expensive and limited.
6. Named Warrants do not replace normal boss farming.
7. Named Warrants are completable solo.
8. Named Warrants do not require a group.

## Named Warrant Drop Rules

1. Completing a Named Warrant gives base marks + boss-specific bonus.
2. The boss's normal drop table still applies.
3. Wardenry gives a small drop roll bonus (e.g., +5% to rare drop chance).
4. The bonus does not guarantee unique drops.
5. The bonus does not stack with other bonuses.
