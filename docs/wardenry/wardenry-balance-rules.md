# Wardenry Balance Rules

Balance rules for the entire Wardenry system.

## Core Balance Rules

1. Wardenry should guide combat, not replace exploration.
2. Free-killing remains valid.
3. Contracts improve structure and rewards.
4. Marks buy utility, unlocks, blocks, and cosmetics — not universal power.
5. Chain bonuses should feel exciting but not mandatory.
6. Starter contracts are safe and short.
7. First-ring contracts can require travel.
8. Risk contracts pay more because they are inconvenient or dangerous.
9. Task-only monsters should be rare and clearly signposted.
10. Notorious Variants are bonuses, not obligations.
11. Boss Warrants are opt-in or uncommon.
12. Wardenry should feed drops into skills and economy.

## Effort Bands

| Band | Wardens | Combat Level | Contract Length | Danger | Reward |
| Starter | Warden Holt | 1–30 | Short | Safe | Low marks, modest drops |
| First-Ring | Orven, Cress, Sella, Pell, Vey | 20–60 | Medium | Risky | Medium marks, good drops |
| Second-Ring | Rook Redcord, Argent Bailiff | 50–90 | Long | Dangerous | High marks, rare drops |

## Mark Economy Rules

1. Marks are not convertible to coins.
2. Marks do not decay.
3. Marks are spent on the Warden Locker.
4. Marks are a sink, not a source.
5. The player should always have something to spend marks on.
6. The Warden Locker should have items at every price point.

## Anti-Abuse Rules

1. Reposting should not be optimal abuse.
2. Widening should not be mandatory.
3. Pinning should not be deterministic.
4. Refusing should not trivialize the system.
5. Chain bonuses should not create FOMO.
6. No daily lock or streak pressure.

## Drop Balance Rules

1. Task drops are boosted, not exclusive.
2. The player can still get the same drops without a contract.
3. Contract drops are a bonus, not a requirement.
4. Boss Warrant drops are slightly boosted but not guaranteed.
5. Notorious Variant drops are narrow and cosmetic.
