# Beadwork Recipes

Beadwork is the magic production spine. It turns minerals, clay, cord, kiln heat, and shrine work into spell beads, foci, pouches, and charmwards.

## Recipe Types

| Recipe | Inputs | Output |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Shape blank | Clay, glass, or mineral | Bead blank |
| Drill bead | Blank plus drill | Drilled bead |
| Glaze bead | Drilled bead plus glaze | Glazed bead |
| Fire bead | Glazed bead plus kiln | Spell bead |
| String beads | Beads plus bead cord | Bead strand |
| Build pouch | Cloth plus bead cord plus fitting | Bead pouch |
| Make focus | Bead strand plus inlay plus frame | Focus or charmward |

## Design Rules

1. Beadwork should use both tool and station gates earlier than most skills.
2. Hearthcraft should matter through kiln fire.
3. Favour can matter through blessed beads and shrine-adjacent recipes.
4. Cracked beads, spilled glaze, and snapped cords are flavourful failures.
5. Bead strands should feed Magic, Favour, Handicraft, and accessories.
